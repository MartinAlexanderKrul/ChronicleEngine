## What 1.82 changed, and the measurement that caused it

**Ability points were the last growth axis in this profile with no arithmetic gate behind them.**

Decision 079 gives the engine one invariant — `current_value = baseline_value + Σ(delta)` over an entity's `tracked_counters`, with every change declared as `counter_deltas` on the causing Event and repository validation rejecting drift. Through 1.81 the Gatefall Bearer carried it on **XP** and on **every skill counter**: uses, qualifying scenes, mastery progress, mastery level, rank ascensions, scope floors, magnitude floors. **Ability points, allocations, base Stats and the unspent pool carried none of it.** They were prose, and prose has nothing to disagree with.

**The consequence was measured on a live campaign rather than supposed.** `gatefall_pendragon_001` allocated **twenty-five points across the level 38–40 span and recorded fifteen.** The ten that reached Intelligence were present in base Intelligence 488, in effective Intelligence 491, in `maximum_mana` 982 and in every damage figure derived that span — and named in no Event. The pool balanced exactly, so nothing was created or lost; what failed was the account. It was found by reconciling stored Stats against the Section 3.2 ladder by hand, and recorded at that campaign's `EVT-000594`.

The same span carried a second instance of the class: **a title's grade payment was spent but never counted**, `titles.points_granted` reading 35 against a thirteenth title that had paid +5.

**This is `F-017`'s shape on a different axis.** XP went untracked across an entire instant dungeon until a player asked where it was, and the running total then resumed from a number matching no derivable sum — because XP was a rendered string. The remedy there was making it a counter. This is that remedy, applied to the axis that still lacked it.

## The six paths

| Path | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `progression.ability_points_earned` | Lifetime points awarded, never reset — level-ups, daily rewards, title grade payments, item and skill bonuses |
| `stats.strength_allocated` | Lifetime points spent into Strength |
| `stats.agility_allocated` | ″ Agility |
| `stats.vitality_allocated` | ″ Vitality |
| `stats.perception_allocated` | ″ Perception |
| `stats.intelligence_allocated` | ″ Intelligence |

## Two values stop being stored

**The unspent pool** is `ability_points_earned − Σ allocated`. A rendered `unspent_points` is a rendering of that difference.

**A base Stat** is:

```text
base_stat = creation_array[stat] + automatic(level) + allocated[stat]

automatic(level) = Σ over every level crossing to date of the Section 3.2 rung gain
                   for the System Rank held entering that crossing
```

**The check is one line, and it exists because Section 3.2 is uniform.** Every crossing raises *all five* base Stats by the same rung gain, so `base_stat − creation_array[stat] − allocated[stat]` must be **the same integer for all five Stats**, and must equal `automatic(level)`. A point that reaches a Stat without an Event declaring it makes that Stat's residual disagree with the other four, immediately and unhideably.

Worked on the adopting campaign at level 40, creation array 11 / 12 / 8 / 11 / 8, `automatic(40) = 9×2 + 10×4 + 10×9 + 10×23 = 378`:

| Stat | Base | Creation | Allocated | Residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strength | 441 | 11 | 52 | **378** |
| Agility | 417 | 12 | 27 | **378** |
| Vitality | 499 | 8 | 113 | **378** |
| Perception | 418 | 11 | 29 | **378** |
| Intelligence | 488 | 8 | 102 | **378** |

Had the ten Intelligence points not been allocated, Intelligence's residual would read **368** against four Stats reading 378, and `EVT-000594`'s defect would have been a failing gate rather than an audit finding.

## What did not change

**No roll, rate, band, timer, formula, threshold, ladder, price or distribution moves.** Section 3.2's rung ladder, Section 4.1's creation array, Section 4.2's point sources and Section 16.1's grade payments are all unamended — 1.82 changes where the numbers are *kept*, not what they are. **No Stat, pool, level, XP, skill, Rank, mastery, title, item or resolved outcome moves in any campaign.**

**Data Model 0.1.5 does not advance.** It reserves the `tracked_counters` shape and lets a world name its own paths; naming six more is opting in, not changing the shape. No migration of engine state, no schema retag.

## Migration procedure

**Required migration, 1.81 → 1.82.** No stored value moves; the counters record what is already true.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.82"`.**

**2. Derive each allocated counter from present state and record it as that counter's baseline.**

```text
allocated[stat] = base_stat − creation_array[stat] − automatic(level)
```

Every one must come out a non-negative integer, and the five residuals must agree. **If they do not, the campaign has an undeclared allocation and it is found here rather than gated over** — reconcile it in its own Event first, as `gatefall_pendragon_001` did at `EVT-000594`, and adopt afterwards.

**3. Derive the earned baseline from the pool identity.**

```text
ability_points_earned = Σ allocated + unspent_points
```

**Read from the pool rather than re-totalled from its sources, and deliberately so.** Awarded points are a matter of historical record across every level-up, daily and Title Assessment a campaign has ever run, and a re-total that disagrees with the pool would be asserting something about that history which the ledger cannot prove. The identity is exact, needs no reconstruction, and reopens nothing.

**4. Record `unspent_points` as a rendering.** Its value is unchanged; what changes is that it is now derived from two counters rather than maintained beside them.

**5. Baselines are prospective and reconstruct nothing.** `baseline_as_of` is the adopting Event. **No historical Event gains `counter_deltas`**, no past allocation decision is revisited, and no question the ledger could not previously answer about its own past becomes answerable. What the counters guarantee begins at adoption.

**6. From adoption forward, every point declares itself.** A level-up writes `+5` to `ability_points_earned`; a daily Ability Point reward and a Title Assessment grade payment write theirs; an allocation writes its points to the named Stat's counter. A settlement that moves a base Stat without a matching delta fails validation.

**7. Nothing else moves.** No Rank, mastery, counter, title, item, gold, cash, quest, class, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.81-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.82 at readiness.

## What this does not close, stated because it is easy to assume otherwise

**It does not settle what a campaign's past awards were.** Step 3 takes the earned baseline from the pool identity precisely because the sources cannot be re-totalled with confidence: in the adopting campaign the level-up and Title Assessment lines are exact, and the daily Ability Point line lands within one point of closing depending on an unresolved ruling about which daily first paid an Ascetic-boosted +4. **The identity is unaffected** — allocated and unspent are hard state, so earned is fixed regardless — but the attribution stays open, and gating does not close it.

**It does not gate the title ledger.** `titles.points_granted` disagreeing with the sum of its own `earned_names` grade payments is a real defect of this class and a different field; the counters do not reach it. A one-line assertion would, and is not written here.
