## What 1.75 changed

**Section 14.4 stated the Bearer-only boundary correctly and in a form the failing check could not consume.**

New **Section 14.4.1** adds the **Bearer-Only Register**: a ten-row closed list of the quantities no NPC holds under any circumstance short of the Bearer stating them aloud — level, XP, System Rank, skills, mastery, quest state, notifications and panels, pending grants, exact pool values, and the System's existence.

**Nothing about the boundary moved.** 14.4's own sentences — the System is *"perceptible only to its Bearer — always, without exception"*, and *"System-issued quests are Bearer-only and therefore unknown to the world"* — are unchanged and remain the statement of the rule. 14.4.1 restates their consequence from the narration side.

**Why restating it is the change.** The prose was in force for every leak in the `F-003` → `F-021` family, and two of those leaks asserted a register item anyway: `F-005(b)` had an NPC cite the Bearer's System Rank, and `F-021` had one describe him as *"currently levelled up."* The rule was present, resident, and gated. What the check had to *do* with it was the problem.

- Deciding whether **this** NPC has a channel to **that** fact is a four-way judgment made at the moment the Runtime is under pressure to make a line sound informed.
- Deciding whether a quantity sits on a ten-row list is a **lookup**, and it carries no per-NPC reasoning **because the answer never varies by NPC** — which is why it belongs in a step before the channel test rather than inside it.

## Its relationship to Decision 091, stated because it would otherwise look like a contradiction

Decision 091 (*An NPC Is a Character, Not a Filtered Narrator*, accepted 2026-08-09) names `F-020` and `F-021` among its own evidence and **explicitly rejects** the alternative *"keep strengthening the channel check"* — on the grounds that three prohibitions had not moved the recurrence rate, because the defect was that the NPCs were nobody.

**That rejection is not reopened here, and this is not a fourth prohibition.**

- 091's own text states that **the channel test *"is unchanged and is not replaced. It governs knowledge; this decision governs personhood."*** 14.4.1 operates entirely inside the test 091 retained.
- It **removes** a judgment rather than adding a rule. The register makes one class of question cheaper to answer correctly, which is the opposite of enlarging the remedy surface 091 measured as ineffective.
- **A false belief about the Bearer remains legitimate characterisation under 091** (Data Model Section 7.8). An NPC may hold, voice, and dig in on a conviction that the Bearer is stronger than his card or that something is wrong with him. What 14.4.1 bars is holding the **quantity** as knowledge: *"you're carrying something you don't talk about"* is a belief; *"you're level 25"* is a register item, and no `Confidence` rating makes it knowable.

**Section 20.5 is extended in scope, not in strength.** 20.5 forbids a character quoting HP, damage, a die result, or a Rank. 14.4.1 applies the same prohibition to the **quantity itself**, whether or not a number is spoken: *"you levelled"* is barred exactly as *"you're at 9 of 40"* is, and it is not a softer version of *"you're moving better than last week."* The first is register state; the second is visible conduct and remains correct play.

**The footprint rule is untouched and is the permitted branch.** An NPC reads the Bearer by visible conduct — how he fights, what he carries, what he can evidently do — and states it as a read or a guess. 14.4's rooftop example still governs: the quest is secret, its footprint is not.

**One consequence for the Closed Channels mechanism.** A row closing a register item carries `Opens on:` **Nothing** unless the Bearer discloses it in words, and a row written otherwise is wrong.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.74 → 1.75.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.75"`.**

**2. Nothing stored changes, and nothing is recomputed.** No pool, Stat, level, XP, System Rank, Card Rank, skill, mastery level, mastery progress, counter, scope floor, magnitude floor, title, item, gold, cash, quest, candidate, class, or fragment is touched. No damage preview, reduction fraction, or rendered magnitude moves. 14.4.1 adds no mechanic and reads no state.

**3. No narration already promoted is re-adjudicated.** Past leaks of register items are recorded where they were caught — `F-005`, `F-021`, and the Closed Channels rows dated 2026-08-18 — and their forward repairs stand. 14.4.1 is prospective: it governs the next NPC line, not lines already promoted. Nothing in the chronicle is rewritten to have said something else.

**4. No Knowledge State changes, no channel opens or closes, and no disposition is disturbed.** The register describes facts no NPC ever had a channel to, so no NPC's knowledge set moves in either direction. Every `Want`, `Fear`, `Secret`, `Voice` and `Belief` authored under Decision 091 is untouched, and no belief becomes invalid by being about a register item.

**5. No campaign owes a re-audit.** Nothing consumed 14.4 at resolution time and nothing consumes 14.4.1 at resolution time; both govern what may be said, not what is computed.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.74-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.75 at readiness.
