## What 1.81 changed, and why it came due now rather than later

**Section 7.3's three category ladders stopped at A-Rank, and the ceiling moved past them.**

1.53 authored A-Rank and left S-Rank explicitly deferred, on a reason stated at the time: S-Rank is the top of the ladder and the point at which a *learned* technique would stand level with the singular signature abilities six named individuals carry, so whether a starting skill reaches it at all is a question about the world's power ceiling rather than about a category ladder. The deferral was correct and cost nothing while S-Rank was unreachable, because Section 7.5's ceiling is the Bearer's System Rank + 1 and S-Rank needs System Rank A, which is level 40.

**Level 40 arrived.** In the adopting campaign the Section 7.5 ceiling moved to S with the System Rank B → A crossing, four skills stood at **Master** beneath it, and the settlement produced the defect the deferral had always implied:

| Skill | State at the barrier | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Rupture** | [A] Master, eligible | Taken — and its S category `Unmaking` was **ruled in play** because no table authored one |
| **Twin Fang** | [C] Master, eligible at B | Taken normally; B was authored |
| **Keen Sense** | [A] Master, at the ceiling | **Offer withheld — no S rung existed to grant** |
| **Exploit Pattern** | [A] Master, at the ceiling | **Offer withheld — no S rung existed to grant** |

Both withheld offers had already been **presented and resolved in play** before the authoring was checked, with categories the Runtime invented rather than read. Withholding them was Section 7.2 working exactly as written — *a rung that returns an unauthored result is not an ascension the profile can honour* — and it is the same discipline that correctly and permanently withheld Twin Fang's and Broken Rhythm's offers until 1.52 authored their D rungs. **That is `F-014`'s failure shape one Rank higher**, and the remedy is the same one: author the rung rather than improvise it.

## The owner ruling

**A learned technique reaches S-Rank, and does not thereby become a signature.** The power-ceiling question the 1.53 deferral raised is answered on the distinction the profile already keeps: a **signature** is innate, one per awakening, fixed for life, and authored per NPC under Section 13.6 — Section 13.6 gives one to every named NPC hunter of D-Rank or above, including C-Rank Naomi Okafor's **Still Room**. A **ladder rung** is what a *learned* technique gains from Rank, and no quantity of rungs produces a signature. The two have coexisted at every Rank from D upward since 1.6 without anyone suggesting a contradiction; S-Rank is the same relation at the top of the scale.

**What an S rung grants is what every rung below it grants: a category, and nothing else.** No modifier step (Section 4.3's cap of +1 is untouched), no Mana change (that axis stays mastery's alone), and no magnitude the skill did not already have. The quantitative grants are unchanged and continue to apply in full.

## One limit is expressly refused

For four skills — **Keen Sense, Flash Step, Mana Bolt, Dimensional Projection** — the obvious S rung was crossing a **Gate boundary, a Section 9.6 seal, or a Section 17 instance wall**. It is the natural top of each of those ladders, it is the rung the Runtime improvised for Keen Sense (`Across the seal`), and it is **refused**.

Those are the edges of a space rather than obstacles within one. The clause is load-bearing across Sections 7.3, 9.6, 12.9, 17 and 18.8, Flash Step's and Dimensional Projection's own cells restate it at C and B, and a rung dissolving it would let a Bearer reach into an instance he has not entered — which is the exact reason the clause was written. **The four skills take rungs that do not touch it**, and the three whose wording could be read as reaching it restate the limit in their own cells rather than relying on the reader to carry it there.

Healing was deliberately excluded from folding effective Intelligence into magnitude, so the Rank baseline still multiplies its output by ~2.5 a step and the Rank Dominance Law holds on the number alone.

## What did not change

**No roll, rate, band, timer, formula, threshold, price, population, distribution or Rank ladder moves.** Section 7.2's eligibility rule, Rank Dominance Law, scope ratchet and magnitude ratchet are unamended. Section 7.4's mastery ladders and axis table are unamended. Section 7.5's two routes, ceiling, settlement, standing readiness check and withholding rule are unamended — **1.81 does not change how ascension works, only what exists to ascend into.** Section 13.6's signature abilities are untouched, and no NPC gains or loses one. Section 4.4's Stat Passives are outside Section 7.5 and are not reached by this migration.

**No skill's Rank, mastery level, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `rank_ascensions`, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, Mana cost or magnitude changes**, in any campaign.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.80 → 1.81.** No stored value moves.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.81"`.**

**2. Re-evaluate Section 7.5's standing readiness check.** It reads four stored values per skill and reaches no history, so it costs the same whether the last barrier was ten minutes or six in-fiction days ago. A skill at `mastery_level` 5, below the Section 7.5 ceiling, and now ascension-eligible at the next Rank under Section 7.2 becomes **ascension-ready**, and an offer is surfaced for it at the next dangerous-scene settlement or promotion barrier. **This is the whole mechanical effect of the migration.**

**3. Withdraw no prior withholding and reverse no prior settlement.** An offer withheld under 1.80 was withheld correctly on the rules in force; it does not become retroactively owed, and no mastery it would have consumed is restored, because none was consumed. Section 7.2's *deferral, not denial* is discharged by the rung existing from adoption forward.

**4. No past Event, roll, damage figure, kill, clear or outcome is retroactively altered.** No Chronicle entry changes.

**5. Nothing else moves.** No level, XP, Stat, Rank, counter, title, item, gold, cash, quest, class, fragment, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.80-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.81 at readiness.
