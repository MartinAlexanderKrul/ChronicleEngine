## The finding this version exists for

**A live S-Rank creature was carried onto Earth with no rule behind it.** At `EVT-001221` the Bearer used Sample Handling to carry a bound Glasswalker out of an instant dungeon on his voluntary exit and left it in the Denver facility's cage room, held by Binding Grip. Section 17 said only that closing *returns everyone inside to the world* and that *anything left inside is gone with it*; neither sentence reached a creature. The grant was recorded as a provisional mechanic owing a hold duration and the creature's pools outside an instance. Its record cited *Section 12.9 (instance boundaries)*; Section 12.9 is the Dimensional Inventory, and the owed law sits in Section 17.

## What 1.125 changes

**Section 17 gains *Carrying a Creature Out*.**

- Only a technique authored to carry a creature through a seam takes one across; restraint alone is not passage.
- The reach is any seam the Bearer can pass: an instance boundary, a Gate mouth, a released red gate, a far door. A creature carried out of a Gate mouth is not a break.
- The creature's pools behave normally on Section 5.2's fixed-Rank rules, and it is tracked as a recurring entity.
- A hold on it outside an instance lasts until the next 00:00 Chicago; recasting on a creature already held needs no roll, only the tier's Mana.

**Section 12.9** gains one cross-reference: a creature is carried, never stored.

## What did not change

Hold duration and renewal **inside** an instance are unchanged. Section 8.4.2 is unchanged; its self-created exclusion already reaches a creature the Bearer brought out. No XP, level, pool, gold, Stat, item or holding moves. No checkpoint is rewritten.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.125** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Regenerate the runtime index** (`tools/generate_runtime_index.ps1`), then confirm with `-Check`.

**3. Validate** with `tools/validate_repository.ps1`.

**4. A campaign holding a carried creature** settles its hold against this version: the hold runs to the first 00:00 Chicago after adoption unless renewed. A campaign with none has nothing to do.

## What is owed and not done here

**The ratification is campaign canon and belongs to play.** This record lands the world side only. `gatefall_pendragon_001` owes the adoption Event, the close of its provisional mechanic (moved to settled with this version cited), and the Glasswalker's hold settled under Section 17: **it lapses at 2026-10-02 00:00 Chicago unless recast**.

**Open, and deliberately not decided here.** What the world makes of a live Gate creature on Earth — a BGM finding, jurisdiction, a breach — is fiction and carries no priced consequence.
