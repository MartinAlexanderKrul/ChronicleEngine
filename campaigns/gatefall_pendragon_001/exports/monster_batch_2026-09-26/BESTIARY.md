# Gatefall: Pendragon — Bestiary

Every creature the campaign record gives a form, as of 2026-10-01 (in-world). Compiled 2026-09-26 at the player's direction; the player overruled the no-bestiary position for this reference. It is a reference document only. It changes no rule, adds no spawn table and gives no creature a stat block beyond what the Section 9.7 formula already gives it.

**How to read an entry.** *From the record* quotes or closely summarises the cited Events and is canon. The *field description* is authored for this bestiary, to make each creature picturable. It builds on the record and never contradicts it. Names marked *(ledger designation)* are this bestiary's handles for creatures the fiction left unnamed; they are not canon names. Rank Health is the Section 9.7 figure (Rank Health × 1 common, × 2 elite, × 4 boss or alpha).

### MON-001 · Scaffold Hound

**S-Rank Common** · Shattered City · Body: Predator (unrecorded — read from name and fight) · Signature: — · Rank Health: 4,000  
Met: Winnetka lakefront Gate, Chicago, 2026-09-21 · Count: 18 · Events: `EVT-001049`

*Pack hunter of the Shattered City's scaffolds; eighteen of them came out of the Winnetka Gate's doubled population.*

**From the record.** Winnetka lakefront Gate, true Rank S, anomaly rolled 'population one Rank above' twice — doubled population at S: 18 commons named Scaffold Hounds. Flux Sight read them yellow, 4,000 Health / 2,000 Mana each. Alexander killed all eighteen across six exchanges with projected blades; none of their attacks landed.

**Field description.** A long-limbed, low-chested hound built for a city with no ground. Its forelimbs end in hooked, two-part digits that lock over girders and ledge-lips, and its spine is jointed like a folding ladder, so it can hang from a strut by its forepaws and swing its hindquarters onto the next. The hide is the dull grey-white of fired ceramic, crazed with hairline cracks that seep a faint light when it exerts itself. It has no visible eyes. A ridge of glassy nodules above the muzzle reads the air instead, and the whole pack turns its heads together when one of them hears something. They hunt in chains, not packs: one hound on every level of a collapsed tower, driving prey upward toward the one waiting above.

**Behaviour.** Moves vertically through broken scaffolding; converges from above and below at once; attacks in rolling waves rather than all together.  
**Known weakness.** Low individual Health for an S-Rank body; the pack's timing is its strength and dies with its numbers.  
**Fate.** All 18 killed, EVT-001049. Each dropped one S-Rank crystal.  
**Loot.** 1 S-Rank mana crystal each.

### MON-002 · Gantry Brute

**S-Rank Elite** · Shattered City · Body: Brute (unrecorded — read from name and fight) · Signature: — · Rank Health: 8,000  
Met: Winnetka lakefront Gate, Chicago, 2026-09-21 · Count: 4 · Events: `EVT-001049`

*Heavy load-bearer of the Winnetka Gate; four of them walked in behind the Scaffold Hounds.*

**From the record.** 4 elites named Gantry Brutes (1d2 rolled 2, doubled). Flux Sight: 8,000 Health / 2,000 Mana each. They arrived in exchange 3; Brute 1 died to Rupture in exchange 5, Brutes 2–4 to projected blades in exchange 6 — Brute 4 by 250 points over its pool. None of their attacks landed.

**Field description.** Where the hounds climb the ruin, the Brute carries it. It stands three times a man's height on four columnar legs set wide as a crane's base, and its back is a flat, braced platform of ceramic plate over a lattice of bone that looks deliberately trussed. Two shorter forelimbs hang under the chest like the hooks of a gantry crane, thick at the shoulder and ending in blunt clamps that close with a crushing, ratcheting grip. It moves slowly and without hesitation, and the scaffold hounds use it as terrain — riding its back up to ledges they could not reach, dropping off it into a fight. Its head is small, low and armoured, set almost between the shoulders, with a single horizontal slit of black glass where a face should be.

**Behaviour.** Advances steadily as a mobile platform for the pack; clamps, lifts and crushes; weight-bearing rather than fast.  
**Known weakness.** Slow to turn; its bulk commits it to a line.  
**Fate.** All 4 killed, EVT-001049.  
**Loot.** 1 S-Rank crystal and 1 S-Rank core each.

Boss drop: Stone Skin rune [S], a duplicate.

**Field description.** The Keystone is shaped like the thing that holds an arch up. Its body is a wedge-shaped mass of fitted stone and fired ceramic, broad at the shoulders and tapering to a narrow, anchoring base where a nest of short, thick legs grips the ground like tree roots gripping a cliff. Two immense arms come off the upper corners of the wedge and end in open, shovel-broad hands. It does not charge. It stands at the centre of its broken city and tears the city apart — prising cornice-stones and slabs of black glass from the towers around it and throwing them with slow, enormous force, so that the Gate itself becomes its ammunition. Its face is set in the middle of its chest: a vertical seam of bright metal, like a join in a repaired vase, that opens a finger's width when it throws.

**Behaviour.** Holds the centre; bombards with ripped-out masonry; lets the pack and the Brutes close while it breaks the ground around the intruder.

### MON-004 · Glassback Stalker

**C-Rank Common** · Shattered City · Body: Predator (unrecorded) · Signature: — · Rank Health: 250  
Met: Evanston, Central Street at the Metra — a break in progress, 2026-10-01 · Count: 12 · Events: `EVT-001202`

*The commons of the Central Street break; four of them were on the embankment one exchange from a stranded Metra train.*

**From the record.** A C-Rank Gate broke at Central Street, Evanston. Its Shattered City population: 12 Glassback Stalkers, all C-Rank (250 Health). Keen Sense placed 9 commuters on a stranded Metra train, a driver in a hatchback, 6 people in a bakery and 2 Evanston officers in reach of them. Alexander killed them across exchanges 3–5 with projected volleys; the 'embankment four' died in the exchange they would have reached the train. 15/15 dead, nobody hurt.

**Field description.** A lean, low stalker about the size of a big dog, with a long back sheathed in plates of black glass that overlap like roof tiles. In daylight the glass throws the sky back, so a stalker lying flat on a rooftop or a rail embankment reads as a puddle, or a skylight, or nothing at all. Under the glass the body is sinewy and grey, with long narrow feet that spread on landing to take a fall from a height, and a thin head that is mostly jaw. When a Glassback moves fast its plates flex and click against each other with a sound like someone stepping on a broken window. Loose in a street they go for anything that moves and anything that is stuck.

**Behaviour.** Lies flat and reflective; ambushes; loose in the open it drifts toward the densest target — here the stranded train.  
**Known weakness.** Light armour under the glass; C-Rank health.  
**Fate.** All 12 killed, EVT-001202. Harvest rights waived to the Chicago branch.  
**Loot.** 1 C-Rank crystal each (waived to the branch).

### MON-012 · Heartwood Custodian

**B-Rank Boss** · Overgrown Temple · Body: Unrecorded (Arcane/Carapaced reading) · Signature: — · Rank Health: 2,400  
Met: B-Rank instant dungeon (Section 17), opened at Alexander's apartment, 2026-08-20 · Count: 1 · Events: `EVT-000570`, `EVT-000578`

*The boss beneath the shrine, named by Flux Sight. Its heartwood core-mass stayed warm nine hours after death.*

**From the record.** Overgrown Temple instant dungeon, B-Rank. Pulling the rooted Warden out of the summit opened a line to the boss chamber; Flux Sight's first read: 'Heartwood Custodian, 2,400/2,400'. Rupture from concealment for 1,895, then a 604 main-hand. It never took a turn. Field harvest: the central bole 'where the growth rings stop being rings and start being something laid down deliberately', and four lengths of limb-fibre. With Priscilla Nakamura the heartwood core-mass [B-Rank] was warm nine hours after the kill, with no lattice, no binding and no housing: material holding a charge it has no mechanism for holding (EVT-000578).

**Field description.** The Custodian is a tree that decided to guard something. Its body is a great upright bole of dark, dense wood, trunk-thick and ribbed with bark like overlapping scale, standing on a splay of root-legs that grip the chamber floor and run off into the temple's root network. Four long limbs come off its upper trunk, fibrous and jointed, and gold sap glows in the cracks of its bark where the living layers are. At the top, where a crown should be, a knot of wood has grown into something like a masked face, turned always toward the stair. It hears the temple through its roots — every footstep on the causeway, every body that falls on the root-floor — and the commons above it are its ears. Opened up, its core is not rings but layers laid down on purpose.

**Behaviour.** Waits beneath its shrine; listens through the root network; guards the stair.  
**Known weakness.** Deaf to anything that does not touch the roots — stone, masonry and canopy are silent ground.  
**Fate.** Killed, EVT-000570. Heartwood core-mass in Nakamura's custody (EVT-000578).  
**Loot.** B-Rank core; boss drop Ascendant Warlord's Loop [A-Rank]; field harvest: heartwood core-mass [B] and limb-fibre.

### MON-013 · Red Line Leaper *(ledger designation)*

**E-Rank Boss** · Shattered City · Body: Predator · Signature: Leaping ledge strike (Mobility) — knocks a target off a ledge · Rank Health: 160  
Met: Red Line corridor Gate, Chicago, 2026-07-24 · Count: 1 · Events: `EVT-000059`, `EVT-000060`

*The first boss of the campaign, killed the day the System arrived.*

**From the record.** The Red Line corridor clear, E-Rank, Shattered City — the session of System onset. The boss: 'a wrong-jointed, cat-shaped apex predator adapted to the Shattered City's verticality, signature a leaping strike intended to knock a target off a ledge.' A first read found nothing; a second, from watching it strike Elias, found that its leap leaves it locked and unable to redirect for about a second after landing. Alexander's setup credit (Kingmaker) came from that kill.

**Field description.** A big cat drawn by someone who had heard of cats. It has the long body and heavy shoulders of a leopard, but every joint bends a little the wrong way — the elbows forward, the hocks too high — so that when it crouches it folds up like a spring and when it moves it seems to hitch. Its hide is a mottled grey of broken plaster with pale veining. It lives on ledges and leaps between them, and its killing move is the leap: it launches from above and hits a target shoulder-first to throw it off whatever it is standing on, and lets the fall do the rest. After it lands it freezes for a heartbeat, locked in its own momentum. That heartbeat killed it.

**Behaviour.** Stalks from above; leaps to knock prey off ledges.  
**Known weakness.** Locked for about a second after landing a leap.  
**Fate.** Killed on setup by Alexander, EVT-000059.  
**Loot.** E-Rank core; an E-Rank armoured greave off the boss.
