## What 1.72 changed

**Three edges, all additive.** Each authors a value where the profile previously said nothing. No existing cost, multiplier, count, price, counter, or resolved outcome moves, and no restore under 1.71 becomes invalid.

### 1. Section 18.8 — an anchored rift-step outside an instance

The rule priced it off a term that does not exist in half the places the same paragraph advertises it:

```text
"Rift-stepping to an anchor rather than a visible point costs the
 Rank-ladder Mana of the instance's Rank rather than a flat 6."

"...from anywhere in the same instance, Gate, or contiguous stretch
 of the city."
```

Standing in a city there is no instance and no instance Rank, so the ability had no price — and §18.8's own worked example fills the panel with three anchors of which **two are outside any instance** (a Rogers Park stairwell, a lot behind a facility). Raised as `F-032` and ruled by the owner on 2026-08-11.

**The city case now reads the ladder at E-Rank: 5 Mana.** The reasoning is that ordinary city ground is the bottom of that ladder, because crossing air nobody warded is not the act of crossing a sealed instance. The authored side is untouched — an anchored step out of a B-Rank instance still costs 40.

**The flat 6 was explicitly rejected.** §18.8 names it as what an anchored step costs *instead of*, so adopting it for the city would have overruled the sentence rather than completed it. Pricing off the Bearer's own System Rank and pricing by distance band were also put and both declined.

### 2. Section 18.8 — releasing an anchor

The section said an anchor is *permanent until released* and never authored the release. It is now **free, instant, and taken at will from anywhere**: the exchange is the price of *setting*, not of letting go. The cap is therefore a working set of N rather than N permanent commitments.

### 3. Section 11.1 — every holding carries a Rank

§11.1 authored the Rank of a beast drop, a core and a mined deposit individually and never stated the rule they were instances of. The consequence was live: `EVT-000502`'s **frost-fused plating** — five pieces off a B-Rank instance boss — sat in the inventory for a full campaign span with **no Rank, no stats and no value**, because the System returned no category for it and nothing required it to have one.

**Every holding carries a Rank, defaulting to the Rank of the creature, instance or Gate it came from.** The System returning no category is now a statement about **identification**, not about Rank — the same distinction §19.2 already draws between what an instrument reads and what is true. Where a Rank cannot be traced to any source it is recorded as unranked **and flagged for a ruling**, never left silently blank.

## Procedure for restoring a 1.71 capture

1. **Set `canonical_state.system_state.profile_version` to `"1.72"`.** Nothing else in the character sheet is touched by edges 1 or 2.
2. **Apply edge 3 to the inventory.** For every holding carrying no Rank, assign the Rank of the creature, instance, or Gate it was recovered from. Where the source is recorded, this is mechanical and requires no ruling. Where no source can be traced, record it as unranked and raise it for a ruling; do not guess.
3. **No pool, Stat, level, XP, gold, cash, counter, mastery level, title, or resolved die outcome changes.** No skill line re-renders. No damage preview moves.
4. **Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** A capture taken under 1.71 keeps the state it captured; this migration governs reading it forward, not rewriting it.
5. Run `tools/validate_repository.ps1` and `tools/validate_runtime_configuration.ps1`.

## Campaign application, `gatefall_pendragon_001`

The frost-fused plating of `EVT-000502` is ruled **[B-Rank] × 5** under edge 3 — its source is a B-Rank instance boss and is recorded. Function remains unidentified, which is now the only thing about it that is open.
