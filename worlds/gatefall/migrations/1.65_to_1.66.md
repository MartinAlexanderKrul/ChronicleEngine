## What 1.66 changed

**Three cells in Section 7.3's magnitude-axis ladder described their own arithmetic and had stopped matching it.**

Each cell in the `Rank's magnitude grant` column carries two hand-written annotations: the **dominance ratio** the grant produces at each Rank step, and a word — *thin* or *dominant* — classifying that ratio against Decision 090 point 5's 1.15× threshold. Neither is a rule. Both are consequences of increments authored elsewhere in the same profile, written down for the reader.

**`F-014` predicted this and named the mechanism**: *"a future profile can retune `+0.25` or `+5 points` and leave this table's classification silently stale — a value encoding an arithmetic property of rules that live elsewhere, hand-maintained, with no gate comparing the two."* What actually moved was not an increment but the **ladder's extent**: 1.53 authored A-Rank across all three Section 7.3 tables, adding a fourth reachable step, and the annotations were not extended with it.

| Skill | Stated | Steps that exist | Corrected to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dagger Mastery** | `1.17× / 1.09× / 1.06×` | four (E→D→C→B→A) | `1.17× / 1.09× / 1.06× / 1.05×` |
| **Stone Skin** | `1.10× / 1.07×` | three (D→C→B→A) | `1.10× / 1.07× / 1.00×` |
| **Bulwark** | no ratio; *"reaches the 90% cap at C-Rank"* | four | `1.06× / 1.00× / 1.00× / 1.00×`, cap first binding at **D-Rank Master** |

**Stone Skin's third step is `1.00×` because the Section 7.2 90% cap holds it flat**, not because the grant failed: B-Rank Master computes `30 + 50 + 20 = 100` and A-Rank Novice `30 + 75 = 105`, both clamped to 90. The cell now says so rather than omitting the step.

**Bulwark's cap statement was a Rank late.** D-Rank Master computes `60 + 25 + 20 = 105` and is already clamped, so the cap first binds there; from C-Rank Novice (`60 + 50 = 110`) it binds at every mastery level. *"Reaches the cap at C-Rank"* is defensible as *fully* capped from C, but it reads as the first binding and it is not. The corrected text gives both the arithmetic and the distinction.

**Nothing about the ladders themselves moved.** Every increment, native Rank, mastery step, reduction cap and authored category is exactly what 1.65 held. The Rank Dominance Law's own guarantee is unaffected, and `tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` verified it before and after this edit without a single value changing.

**The class is now gated rather than trusted.** That suite computes each `Rank N Master → Rank N+1 Novice` step from the increments it parses out of Sections 7.3 and 7.4, and compares it to every ratio a cell states, naming the step and both figures on a mismatch. Two skills are excluded by design — **Rupture and Mana Bolt** read `skill_rank_baseline + effective Intelligence` (Section 6.2, Profile 1.56), so their real ratio depends on the Bearer rather than the profile, and the gate instead requires that such a skill state **no** figure it cannot own. Mend is checked, healing being excluded from that fold.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.65 → 1.66.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.66"`.**

**2. Nothing stored changes, and nothing is recomputed.** No pool, Stat, level, XP, System Rank, Card Rank, skill, mastery level, mastery progress, counter, scope floor, magnitude floor, title, item, gold, cash, quest, or candidate is touched. No skill's rendered magnitude, damage preview, or reduction fraction moves, because no increment moved.

**3. Nothing retroactive, and no past ascension is re-adjudicated.** Every Rank ascension resolved under 1.65 or earlier stands exactly as promoted, at exactly the value it was granted. In particular this edge does **not** revisit any ascension whose favourability was assessed against the old annotation: the underlying arithmetic was correct throughout, so no decision made on it was made on a wrong number.

**4. No offer reopens and none is withdrawn.** Ascension eligibility reads the ladders and the Section 7.5 ceiling, neither of which changed. A skill ascension-ready under 1.65 is ascension-ready under 1.66 on identical terms.

**5. No campaign owes a re-audit.** These are reader-facing annotations. Nothing consumed them at resolution time, so nothing derived from them needs recomputation.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.65-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.66 at readiness.
