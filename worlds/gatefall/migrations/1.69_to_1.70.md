## What 1.70 changed

**Dimensional Projection had been ratified for a whole campaign and never once used.** Its live counters read `successful_uses: 0` and `qualifying_scenes_total: 0` from `EVT-000436` onward. That is not a player overlooking a tool; it is the arithmetic being unarguable.

At effective Strength 87, with Dagger Mastery [C-Rank] Expert supplying a ×1.50 chassis and a banked C-Rank blade at weapon power 7:

| Action | Damage | Mana | Range | Combines? | Aftermath |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Ordinary main-hand strike | **164** | **0** | melee | yes | none |
| Rupture | **571** | 9 | 5 m | it *is* the combo skill | none |
| Mana Bolt | **167** | 5 | **unlimited** | no | none |
| **Dimensional Projection** | **141** | 6 | **5 m** at Novice | **no** | weapon on the ground; recovery spends the next exchange's inventory change |

It was beaten by a **free** ordinary strike, beaten by Mana Bolt at worse range for more Mana, worth a quarter of Rupture, and it was the only offensive technique in the profile that disarmed its own user. Its Novice range equalled Rupture's flat band, so it did not even buy reach until mastery. **There was no state in which it was the correct action.**

### The cause

**Profile 1.56 found and fixed this exact defect, and could not reach this skill.** Its finding was stated plainly at the time: *"Rupture dealt 61 for 9 Mana while an ordinary dagger strike, costing nothing, dealt 128 — a signature offensive skill had become strictly worse than swinging the knife."* The structural cause it named was that a fixed Rank baseline cannot keep pace with a weapon strike that compounds with Stats and gear every session, and the fix folded effective Intelligence into `Bearer_skill_damage` (Section 6.2).

**Dimensional Projection resolves on the ordinary *physical* damage formula by construction** — *"one ranged physical strike using Agility to hit and the ordinary Bearer physical-damage formula"* — so the fold never applied to it. Its damage **is** a weapon strike: the same `effective Strength + weapon power`, with a worse weapon than the equipped one, without the equipped Stat bonuses, and with 6 Mana attached. It remained the third case of the 1.56 defect for fourteen versions.

**And it had no damage axis at all.** Both mastery and Rank moved range and cost, and nothing else. Twelve qualifying scenes and four Rank ascensions later, the strike hit for exactly what throwing that weapon by hand hits for.

### The repair

**Projectile count becomes the technique's magnitude.** Mastery launches **1 / 2 / 2 / 3 / 3** objects from Novice through Master, and **each Rank above native adds one more**. Count is a legitimate magnitude axis and it is precedented — Broken Rhythm's mastery axis is scope (1 → 5 beneficiaries), authored at 1.52 for the same reason: a skill whose effect cannot be scaled by a multiplier still needs something that grows.

**Every projectile is its own deployment point, object and resolution, and they split freely across targets in range.** The technique becomes a **named multi-strike** under Section 6.2 — the package type that explicitly supplies its own resolutions, which Twin Fang already uses, so no new machinery is invented. This makes it the Bearer's **only true multi-target offensive action**: Rupture is single-target at 5 m, Mana Bolt is single-target, Twin Fang is one target plus a follow-up. Competing with Rupture on single-target damage is a fight this technique loses at every Rank; a crowd is a fight nothing else in the kit answers.

**Mana is paid once for the action, never per projectile.** Range remains a genuine grant and is no longer the only one.

**Ammunition is governed by arithmetic rather than by a permission list.** The launch is the Bearer's own skill, so it carries his mana under `GTF-OVR-003` and a thrown object wounds a monster where a soldier's rifle would not. But damage reads *that object's* power and chassis: a ranked blade brings its Section 11.5 weapon power and chassis; a crate, a chair or a fistful of crystal brings weapon power 0 and the improvised chassis ×0.5. **Junk is legal and nearly worthless; ranked steel is what hurts.** The consequence is an acquisition track the technique never had — a Bearer holding it stops selling banked weapons, because his armoury is his ammunition.

**Recall stops being a rebate.** The D rung returned the launched weapon without spending the exchange's inventory change — it refunded a cost the skill imposed on itself, where Rupture's *Compound* and Mana Bolt's *Arcing* add capability. Recovering three landed objects for one inventory change is a real grant.

**`tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` now reads the projectile ladder rather than the range ladder.** Both still exist and both drop at an ascension's Novice rung, which is what the `magnitude_floor` ratchet is for; the suite had been verifying the axis that no longer carries the skill's magnitude.

## Migration procedure

**Required migration, 1.69 → 1.70.** It moves live capability: a Bearer holding this technique can do something at adoption that he could not do before it.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.70"`.**

**2. Re-render the technique's `skills_known` entry.** Its stored line states the effect, Mana cost and range. Rewrite it to the 1.70 effect, adding the projectile count for the Bearer's **current** mastery level and Rank. **No counter moves**: `mastery_level`, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, and `qualifying_scenes_total` are all preserved exactly as stored, and the `magnitude_floor` carried from ratification is unchanged.

**3. Projectile count is derived, never stored.** It reads from mastery level and Rank at the moment of use, exactly as range already did. This edge adds no field to any ledger.

**4. Nothing retroactive, and no past exchange is re-resolved.** No resolved strike is recomputed, no damage figure moves, and no scene in which the technique was or was not used is re-adjudicated. In `campaigns/gatefall_pendragon_001/` this is trivially true: the technique has never been used, and its counters stand at zero.

**5. No other skill is touched.** Rupture, Mana Bolt, Twin Fang and every other entry keep their formulas, multipliers, Mana costs, ladders and counters unchanged. Section 6.2's damage formulas are not edited by this edge — Dimensional Projection continues to resolve on the physical formula, and what changed is how many strikes one action supplies.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.69-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.70 at readiness.
