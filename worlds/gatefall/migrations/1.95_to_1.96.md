## What 1.96 changes

**The finding (`F-049`).** Riftwalker's first-listed ability was *"teleport to any point in line of sight; Mana 6, once per exchange... grants +1 modifier step to the immediate follow-up action."* Flash Step, a **D-Rank rune**, is *"close or break line-of-sight distance instantly once, granting +1 modifier step to the follow-up action"* - and at the live Bearer's A-Rank Master it carries a **whole-scene** follow-up window against rift-step's immediate one, with no per-exchange lock. **The class's headline grant was a worse copy of a rune he had ascended past twenty levels earlier**, and the travel it actually sells lived in a side clause capped at one or two anchored steps a scene.

Two ledger drifts made it worse and are corrected here as law rather than as canon repair:

- The live sheet's Flash Step line read **"reach: any point within 100 m, sight or no sight."** The profile grants Flash Step **no metre band at any Rank**; the string `100 m` occurred exactly once in the whole profile, in Riftwalker's own growth table. The skill line had acquired the class ability's reach.
- The same line named its A-Rank category **`Return`**, which appears nowhere in the profile. The authored A category was `Blind step`.

### 1. Flash Step is bound to perception, at every Rank

Its destination must be somewhere he can **see**, or - from C-Rank up, through the `Through the barrier` and `Through the ward` categories - somewhere he holds a **live read** of: a prior look, a resolved Keen Sense, or a relayed Pattern. **There is no Rank at which it crosses to a place he has not perceived, and no distance in metres anywhere in its line.**

**`Blind step` (A) is replaced by `Interpose` (A)**: the step may be taken as a **reaction**, placing him between a resolving attack and another person within reach; he becomes its target. Once per exchange, consumes his reaction, and it grants **no reduction and no defence** - it moves who is hit, never how hard. `Through the barrier` (C), `Through the ward` (B) and `Twice` (S) are unchanged.

### 2. Rift-step splits into two actions

- **The in-scene step** - line of sight, Mana 6, once per exchange, +1 to the immediate follow-up. Unchanged, and deliberately kept: a Riftwalker without it would have to buy a rune to fight.
- **The travelling step** - **any place he has personally stood.** It takes the exchange and **removes him from the scene**; it cannot be spent to leave a fight and return inside it. The one exception is the answering step below.

**"Stood," not "seen."** A place he has looked at, been shown, had described, or watched through another's read is not a destination. *Seen* has no evaluation point and *stood* is already canon - the same `F-002` discipline the rest of this profile is held to. A destination that no longer exists - a cleared Gate's collapsed interior, ground a Scar has shifted - is not a destination.

### 3. Arrival is blind, and that is what anchors are for

He arrives at a remembered place **as he last saw it**: geometry, and nothing else. What is there now resolves without warning him. **An anchor is the exception** - anchor sense has already told him it is disturbed, and at System Rank A which kind - so an anchor is the only ground he arrives at knowing what he is stepping into.

### 4. Mana is priced by container, never by metres

Gatefall tracks no coordinates and the Runtime is not asked to invent any. Each rung asks which **ledgered container** holds both ends: place entities (`210_PLACES.md` and the campaign ledger), **districts** (already tracked state under Section 9.1.3), and metros. Take the smallest container holding both: **6 / 8 / 12 / 20 / 40**. Where the ledger does not place a destination in a district it prices at the metro rung - **ambiguity resolves against the Bearer**, so a vague destination is never the cheap one. An instance's interior prices as the district its mouth stands in.

### 5. No rift-step crosses an instance boundary, at any Rank, either way

Not out of a Gate, a red gate, an instant dungeon, a penalty zone or a class trial, and not into one. **Stated as a hard limit rather than left to the distance table**, because a red gate's seal, a penalty zone's *"no other exit and no retreat"*, the clear window behind Section 13.2's loot declaration, and Section 9.4's committed crews all rest on it. An anchor set inside an instance is reachable while he is inside it, goes quiet when he leaves, and is released by the instance's collapse.

### 6. Anchors become held ground

The cap - `effective Agility / 20` - is unchanged in formula and changed in meaning: **through 1.95 it limited where he could go; it now limits what he can watch and where he can arrive safely.** The growth table drops its reach column, since reach no longer grows, and gains **held ground**: arrival never blind (C), **the answering step** - rift to a disturbed anchor as a reaction, from anywhere, without leaving the scene he is in (B) - **warded ground**, deflection free while standing at an anchor (A), and at S the answering step unlimited, warded ground covering one other person, and an ally he touches sent with him.

## What did not change

**No Rank Health, XP value, threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, break timer, population, loot table, price or title moves.** Flash Step's native Rank, Mana ladder, mastery axis (`Arrival` duration), whole-scene follow-up window, and its C/B/S categories are untouched. Riftwalker's anchor cap formula, free setting and free release, anchor-sense rungs, deflection percentages, and the level-50 Threshold Trial evolution are untouched. Nothing in Sections 8 or 9 moves.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.96** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Correct the Flash Step line, and this is a repair rather than a change.** The live entry carries `reach: any point within 100 m, sight or no sight` and `Category `Return` (A)`. **Neither was ever authored.** Rewrite the reach as *line of sight, or a place he holds a live read of (C/B categories)*, and replace `Return` with **`Interpose` (A)**. The line should also render the `Through the barrier` (C) and `Through the ward` (B) categories it has been omitting - a cumulative ladder shows every category held, as Rupture's line does.

**No use, scene or mastery counter is touched.** `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `mastery_progress`, `mastery_level` and `rank_ascensions` all stand: the skill was used, the scenes happened, and only the description of what it can reach was wrong.

**Scenes resolved under the wrong reach are not re-adjudicated.** `EVT-000631`'s rift-step home and any step taken on the phantom 100 m band stand as ruled. This profile forbids fix-it-later retcons of promoted canon, and the correction is prospective.

**3. Reprice nothing already spent.** Mana already paid on any past step stands. The container ladder applies from adoption forward.

**4. Re-render the class block.** `system_rank_rungs` on the character sheet quotes the old growth table's reach column (*"At A: any point within 100 m sight or no sight"*). Replace it with the 1.96 table, which has no reach column.

**5. Anchors are preserved exactly.** All nine set anchors keep their names, places, `set_event` provenance and disturbance history. The cap is unchanged in formula; only what a slot buys has changed. **The stale `cap_formula` note should be re-derived while the line is open** - it reads *"effective Agility 713 / 20 ... cap 35"* beside a `cap` field of 48, which is correct at the current effective Agility 965.

**6. A capture restored across this edge needs steps 1, 2 and 4 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0120 and earlier keep what they were captured under, including the drifted Flash Step line - that is the record of what the campaign believed at the time.
