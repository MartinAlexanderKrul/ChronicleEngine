## What 1.92 changes

Four changes, all prospective, all against `F-046`. **None of them creates a Gate, raises a reward, softens a number, or lowers a bar.** Every one of them makes an existing mechanism reachable that was authored and then left with nothing to act on.

### 1. Section 6.7 - an automatic defensive effect is standing protection

**The old text excluded on frequency.** `standing_worn_reduction` read *"a once-per-exchange or once-per-scene effect is a tactical resource the Bearer may spend, hold, or waste - not standing protection - and is excluded"* - one sentence covering two unlike things. A **spendable** effect needs a declaration and costs something, and the Bearer can hold it too long or throw it away. An **automatic** effect fires on its own trigger, needs no declaration, costs nothing, and cannot be failed to have.

**The new text excludes on agency.** An effect enters `standing_worn_reduction` when it requires no declaration, spends nothing, and cannot be held or wasted. Automatic effects compose multiplicatively under Section 6.2's standing `total_reduction = 1 - product(1 - r)` rule, and the five-hit band is read at one landed hit per exchange. Everything the Bearer must choose to use stays excluded, unchanged.

**Why it was found.** The live Bearer had bought two A-Rank Warded Vambraces *for the express purpose of moving this axis* - `100_CHARACTER_SHEET.md` records the reason on the item itself - each granting an unconditional -30% on a landed hit with nothing to declare and nothing to spend. The formula that exists to notice investment declined to notice the investment made directly at it.

### 2. Section 7.4 - a combat skill's qualifying test is contest, not danger

**The old text:** *"a combat skill requires genuine dangerous-scene contributions."*

**The new test:** a combat skill qualifies in a scene where something could have made it fail at a cost - an opposed resolution against an opponent capable of beating him, in which a bad result takes something from him. Danger is the ordinary form that takes; it is not the definition. Three conditions, all required: an opponent who could beat him, a real resolution against him, and a cost on failure.

**No settlement is re-opened.** `EVT-000527`, `EVT-000560`, `EVT-000574`, `EVT-000599`, `EVT-000667` and `EVT-000676` all classified no credit on the finding *nothing acted against him and nothing was rolled against him*. That finding fails a contest test exactly as it failed a danger test. All six were correct, all six stay correct, and none is recomputed.

**What it opens** is the practice route a combat skill never had while a non-combat skill always did. Section 6.2.1's pulled strike, authored at 1.87 for `F-042`, already makes contested non-lethal combat fully resolvable; sparring, exhibition bouts, guild trials, arrests and duels become ordinary qualifying scenes wherever the three conditions hold.

### 3. New Section 9.11 - Directed Assignment

Sections 9.1 step 4, 9.3, 9.4 and 12.3 each describe a channel by which A-Rank and S-Rank work reaches an individual high-Rank hunter - state-directed operations, an S-Rank draft, national-asset pay, *never sits on a board*. **None of them authored it**, so the only route the profile actually offered was a board whose own numbers close it: composing Section 9.2's distribution with Section 9.1's take rates puts an A-Rank Gate in a freelancer's reach roughly once per 240 in-fiction days and an S-Rank Gate never.

Section 9.11 authors the channel: a regulator's call list of hunters carded A-Rank or S-Rank, a d100 per assignment against `01-25 NAMED / 26-50 OFFERED / 51-00 NOT THIS ONE` with three stated modifiers, three tiers of refusal ending in the draft, and an explicit statement that it creates no Gate and sizes nothing to the Bearer. It runs on the Section 9.1 tick, in the same pass, and is never narrated at need.

### 4. Section 17 - the proving key

The Combat Tier had **two consumers and both were imposed**: a class trial that is once per campaign and long spent, and a penalty zone that fires only on a failed daily quest. In a campaign sixteen days into an unbroken streak, the profile's one mechanism for sizing content to the Bearer had no live consumer at all.

The **proving key** is a third. It runs a standard Section 17 instant dungeon at the Bearer's Combat Tier, snapshotted at the instant it is **used** rather than created - the section's one named exception to fixed-Rank creation, and a narrow one. It rolls no anomaly, is sold only by the System shop, and costs **twice** the ordinary key price for the tier it will run at. It is chosen and paid for, so Section 6.7's rule that the System owes an honest fight only where it picked the fight is untouched.

New Section 12.5 catalogue row, read as Combat Tiers rather than as Ranks the buyer may pick from: **1,000 / 4,500 / 20,000 / 90,000 / 400,000 / 1,800,000 g**.

## What did not change

**No Rank, XP value, threshold, Rank Health, Rank Mana, damage formula, spawn rate, Rank distribution, break timer, population formula, anomaly table, loot table, title, class, or quest reward moves.** Section 9.2's distribution is untouched and Section 9.11 adds no Gate to it. Section 6.6's rule that a posted or assessed Gate never bends around one hunter is restated rather than weakened. Every ordinary instant-dungeon key keeps its fixed Rank and is still never retroactively hardened.

**Section 6.7's `min(offense_tier, defense_tier)` is unchanged**, and so is its System Rank floor, its snapshot rule, and its exclusion of genuinely spendable effects.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.92** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Re-derive the Combat Tier and correct its render.** The tier is derived, never stored (Section 6.7), but every live ledger *renders* it, and a render is what goes stale. Recompute `offense_tier` and `defense_tier` from the equipped loadout under change 1 above and write the result wherever the tier is rendered.

**This step is not cosmetic, and the live campaign is the worked example.** At the 1.91 -> 1.92 boundary `gatefall_pendragon_001` renders **Combat Tier A** against a ledger that reads:

```text
maximum_health            4,016
total_physical_reduction  ~51.5%  (worn only, five slots, multiplicative)
best standard result      5,823   (Rupture [S-Rank] Expert)
System Rank               A
```

- *Offense.* S-Rank commons hold 4,000 and die to one hit; the ladder has no higher rung. `offense_tier = S`.
- *Defense, worn only, which is the rule as 1.91 already had it.* `incoming(S) = (4,000 / 4) x (1 - 0.515) = 485`, and `4,016 / 485 = 8.3` - five hits stand with room to spare. `defense_tier = S`.
- *Tier.* `max(A, min(S, S)) = S`.

**The rendered A was already stale under 1.91, and this migration is not what changes it.** The tier crossed to S when maximum Health passed roughly 2,700 at the then-current reduction - several levels and two checkpoints before this edge - and nothing re-derived it. The consequence is that the one imposed danger source the campaign still had, the Section 8.3 penalty zone, would have dropped him into an A-Rank instance when its own rule said S. **Change 1 does not create the S tier here.** What it does is make it **durable**: the residual falls from `0.485` to `0.485 x 0.7 x 0.7 = 0.238`, so the axis holds as content Rank climbs instead of sitting one bad exchange above its own threshold.

**This is a rendered derived value that drifted with nothing to disagree with it**, the shape `F-037` and `F-045` were both raised about. Section 6.7 says the tier is derived and never stored, and names an evaluation point for each *consumer* - but the ledger renders it as a standing line and no rule says when that render is recomputed. Raised separately as `F-048`; this migration corrects the instance, not the cause.

**3. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, item, counter, anchor, skill entry, mastery level, `qualifying_scenes_total` or `successful_uses` changes. The contest test under change 2 is prospective from adoption: no past scene is re-scored, and the six no-credit settlements named above stand as ruled.

**4. A capture restored across this edge needs steps 1 and 2 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0119 and earlier keep what they were captured under, including the stale rendered tier, which is the record of what the campaign believed at the time.
