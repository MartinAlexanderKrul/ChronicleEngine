## What 1.42 changed

**Section 13.6's signature-ability requirement gains a Rank floor.** The section previously required a signature ability of *every* named NPC hunter recorded in play. It now requires one of every named NPC hunter at **D-Rank or above**. An E-Rank entry carrying no signature is correct rather than incomplete, and no validator, audit, or Runtime should read the empty field as a gap.

**What the floor does not say.** An E-Rank's gift is not absent. It is **too slight to name, file, or build a reputation on** — which is why the trade's boards record E-Ranks by working type and licence number rather than by power, and why Section 9.4 has most E- and D-Ranks making their living as miners, harvesters, and gear-porters rather than as anybody's firelance. Section 13.6's opening clause is unchanged and still reads *most* awakened manifest a signature, not all; an E-Rank's may be named in play whenever the fiction needs it, authored under the same descriptive, no-magnitude limit as any other signature.

**The Bearer's case is untouched and reads more sharply.** Section 13.6 records that he manifested nothing at awakening — the notorious blank card of the weakest E-Rank. Under 1.42 that remains a distinct and remarkable reading rather than an ordinary one: a blank card is **nothing at all**, not the slight, unfiled gift an E-Rank is now understood to carry. The contrast is why the card was notorious, and the floor preserves it instead of eroding it.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.41 → 1.42 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration.

**No authored signature is removed.** The floor governs what must be authored going forward; it does not strip a signature from any entry that already carries one, at any Rank. If a capture holds an E-Rank NPC with a named signature, that signature stands — it is simply no longer *required*.

**No mechanic moves.** A signature has never carried magnitude: Section 13.6 fixes it as a descriptive fact determining method and reach, and only the mender and warden functions of Section 13.5 attach authored effects, keyed to what a signature *does* rather than to whether one is recorded. An E-Rank entry that gains or loses a signature line therefore changes no damage, no reduction, no pool, no cost, and no party effect. Nothing is re-resolved and no exchange is recomputed.

**In the Pendragon campaign at adoption.** Six D-Rank-and-above hunters are authored a signature in the same pass — Priya Okafor (`ENT-000136`), Ruth (`ENT-000143`), Reggie Calloway (`ENT-000173`), Bhavna Iyer (`ENT-000174`), Foster Nakashima (`ENT-000175`), and Callie Dunmore (`ENT-000176`). Five E-Rank hunters are recorded as correctly carrying none under this floor — Julian Boyd (`ENT-000137`), Renata Marchetti (`ENT-000138`), Denny Osei (`ENT-000179`), Marisol Ruiz (`ENT-000180`), and Milo (`ENT-000168`). Owen Callahan (`ENT-000139`) already carried his, and every figure in `220_NOTABLE_FIGURES.md` who needed one already had it.

**One flag closed rather than filled.** Preston Vaughn (`ENT-000108`) is explicitly not awakened, so Section 13.6 never reached him at any version of this rule; that was settled at `EVT-000249` and 1.42 does not disturb it.

Record adoption as `EVT-000250`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.41-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.42 at readiness.
