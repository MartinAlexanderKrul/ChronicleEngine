## What 1.80 changed, and why it is a correction rather than a buff

**The Bearer's growth was linear and the ladder he was measured against was geometric.**

Every fixed-Rank hunter and creature reads its pools from the Section 6.1 ladder, which multiplies by **2.5 per Rank**: Health 40 / 100 / 250 / 600 / 1,500 / 4,000, with Rank Mana at half of each. The Bearer's pools are linear in his Stats — `Health = 4 × Vitality`, `Mana = 2 × Intelligence` — and his Stat income was flat: `+1` to every Stat per level, plus allocation.

Linear income against a geometric ladder falls behind by construction, and the shortfall compounds at the rate the ladder multiplies. Measured on the live campaign:

| System Rank | Level | A hunter of that Rank has | The Bearer had | Match |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **B** | 30 | 600 Health | **524** | 87% |
| A | 40 | 1,500 | ~1,116 | 74% |
| S | 50 | 4,000 | ~1,804 | **45%** |

Section 6.6 says System Rank *"is not a substitute for his actual body,"* and that remains true and unamended. What was not intended is the size of the gap: a Bearer reaching the S rung with **under half** an S-Rank hunter's body, holding a label the arithmetic could never honour.

**The rung gains are derived, not chosen.** Each is the per-level growth carrying a Stat from one rung's parity value to the next across the ten levels between them, where parity is `Vitality = Rank Health ÷ 4` and `Intelligence = Rank Mana ÷ 2` — the same arithmetic Section 6.1 already uses in the other direction:

| System Rank held | E | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Rung gain, every Stat** | **+2** | **+4** | **+9** | **+23** | **+63** | **+63** |

The gain is read from the System Rank the Bearer **holds as the level-up settles**, not the Rank it reaches. S repeats A because level 50 is the ladder's last rung and there is no parity target above it.

**Automatic growth alone now clears parity, and that is the test the numbers were built to pass.** From the weakest legal creation array (a Stat of 8, Section 4.1): 26 at level 10 against 25, 66 at level 20 against 62, 156 at level 30 against 150, 386 at level 40 against 375, 1,016 at level 50 against 1,000. **The Bearer matches a hunter of his System Rank before allocating a single point; allocation is the margin by which he exceeds one.** Allocation itself is untouched — the +5 per level, the daily-quest reward and the Title Assessment points are unchanged.

## Section 19.2's bands were broken against the same ladder

The re-assessment instrument reads maximum Mana and returns a Rank. Its bands were linear (`+20` per Rank) against the geometric Rank Mana pools, and the failure was not subtle: **an ordinary C-Rank hunter carries 125 maximum Mana, and the old table carded 120+ as S-Rank.** Running the world's own instrument on an unremarkable C-Rank hunter returned S — the table that *assigns* Rank contradicted the table that *defines* it, for every Rank above D.

Each boundary is now the geometric midpoint between the two pools it divides:

| Measured maximum Mana | E | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **1.80 band** | 1–31 | 32–79 | 80–193 | 194–474 | 475–1,224 | 1,225+ |

Section 19.3's reawakening classification, Section 19.4's refusal trade, and Section 19.1's exposure channels are **unchanged and unamended**. What is gone is the possibility of being carded at a Rank the rest of the profile cannot support.

## What did not change

**No Rank ladder, pool formula, XP threshold, allocation rate, skill, mastery, contest rate, roll, band, timer or distribution moves.** Section 6.1 and Section 5.1 are untouched — the ladder was already correct and is what everything else was re-derived *from*. Section 6.6's ladder table and its "not a substitute for his actual body" statement are unamended. Section 6.7's Combat Tier is unamended and continues to size imposed content from the body rather than the label. `Health = 4 × Vitality` and `Mana = 2 × Intelligence` are unchanged.

## Migration procedure

**Required migration, 1.79 → 1.80.** Owner-ruled: **backfill the whole curve.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.80"`.**

**2. Recompute automatic growth from level 1 under the new ladder, and add the delta to every base Stat.** The delta is uniform across all five Stats, because automatic growth is uniform and **allocation is unchanged** — a Bearer allocated what he allocated, and this migration does not revisit a single allocation decision.

```text
old_automatic = 1 × (level − 1)
new_automatic = 2 × (E-rung level-ups) + 4 × (D) + 9 × (C) + 23 × (B) + 63 × (A and above)
delta         = new_automatic − old_automatic          # added to each of the five base Stats
```

At level 30 the level-ups are 9 at the E rung (levels 1→10), 10 at D (10→20) and 10 at C (20→30), giving `new_automatic = 18 + 40 + 90 = 148` against `old_automatic = 29`, so **`delta = +119` to every base Stat.**

**3. Re-derive in order:** effective Stats (base + equipment, equipment unchanged), then `maximum_health` and `maximum_mana`, then every Stat Passive Rank under Section 4.4 with its System Rank ceiling still binding, then every stored damage preview that reads an effective Stat under Section 6.2.

**4. Fill both pools to the new maxima.** A recomputation that raises a ceiling and leaves the Bearer short of it would present as damage he never took. This is the one place the migration touches a current value rather than a maximum, and it is deliberate.

**5. No past Event, roll, damage figure, kill, clear or outcome is retroactively altered.** Every number that resolved in play resolved under the rules in force at the time and stands exactly as recorded. The backfill reconstructs the Bearer's **present** Stats as though the ladder had always applied; it does not re-run history, and no Chronicle entry changes.

**6. Nothing else moves.** No level, XP, Rank, skill, mastery, counter, title, item, gold, cash, quest, class, fragment, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.79-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.80 at readiness.

## The adopting campaign's figures, recorded for audit

`gatefall_pendragon_001` at level 30, System Rank B, creation array Strength 11 / Agility 12 / Vitality 8 / Perception 11 / Intelligence 8:

| | 1.79 | 1.80 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Base Strength | 80 | **199** |
| Base Agility | 68 | **187** |
| Base Vitality | 130 | **249** |
| Base Perception | 69 | **188** |
| Base Intelligence | 112 | **231** |
| Effective Vitality | 131 | **250** |
| Effective Intelligence | 115 | **234** |
| `maximum_health` | 524 | **1,000** |
| `maximum_mana` | 230 | **468** |

Against a B-Rank hunter's 600 Health and 300 Mana, the Bearer now **exceeds parity on both axes**, which is the stated intent. His allocated points are unchanged at 264 across the five Stats and remain exactly where he put them.

**Combat Tier is recomputed and does not move.** Best standard damage rises to 1,583 (Rupture, `(375 + 234) × 2.60`), which lifts `offense_tier` to **S**; `defense_tier` holds at **B**, since `1,000 ÷ ((1,500 ÷ 4) × 0.54) = 4.94` falls just short of the five landed hits A requires. `max(B, min(S, B))` = **B**, unchanged. Section 6.7 takes the lower axis deliberately, and survivability is still what binds.

**A re-assessment now returns B.** Maximum Mana 468 falls in 1.80's B band of 194–474 — matching his System Rank and matching the hunter parity the rungs deliver. Under 1.79's bands the same Bearer measured **S**, which is the defect this migration and the rebanding jointly close.
