## What 1.46 changed

**Section 15 is rewritten. Nothing it reads from moved.** The `/system` window is a view of canonical state (Section 14.1), so changing the view changes no state: no number, cost, timer, capability, pool, price, Rank, counter, quest, item, or resolved outcome is different after this adoption than before it. What changed is the layout the Runtime is obliged to render, and how the command divides.

**The window was authored for a Bearer who no longer exists.** 1.45's Section 15 rendered every section — identity, vitals, stats, quests, skills, titles, equipment, inventory, gold — into one 76-cell framed window, and its normative worked example was a level-3 Bearer with four skills and five inventory lines. At a Bearer's ordinary mid-campaign depth the same template produces well over a hundred rows, most of them wrap-continuations, with an unclaimed reward and a banked antidote rendered at identical weight. The window stopped being readable at exactly the point a Bearer most needs to read it.

**Three tiers replace one window.**

- Bare **`/system`** renders the **Console** (Section 15.2): identity, vitals, stats, unclaimed rewards, active quests, funds, and a counted index naming the panel that holds each remaining body of detail.
- **`/system <panel>`** renders one panel in full (Section 15.3).
- **`/system all`** renders the Console followed by every panel (Section 15.4) — the exhaustive read 1.45's bare `/system` produced, now asked for rather than imposed.
- **`/system <panel> <item>`** (Section 15.3.7) renders an entry's complete canonical line, including the provenance and binding fields no panel column carries.

**Nothing reachable under 1.45 became unreachable.** Every fact the old full window rendered is rendered by some tier of the new one, and the third tier reaches stored fields the old window never showed at all.

**`/system equipment` and `/system inventory` merge into `/system gear`.** Section 12.9 already governed the worn loadout and the dimensional inventory as one surface — a line is the same line whether worn or stored, differing only in whether it is applied (Section 11.5) — so the split across two commands was the profile working against its own model, and it made the ordinary act of comparing a stored piece against the worn one and swapping span two windows. `WORN · TOTALS` now sits at the seam between them. **Both old command names are retained as aliases** and render the merged panel, so no session transcript, campaign file, or player habit is invalidated.

**Every panel now has an authored template.** 1.45 Section 15.2 declared seven focused views and three interactive panels and authored a template for none of them, leaving a focused panel improvised at the table — the exact failure the section's normativity exists to prevent. Sections 15.3.1 through 15.3.6 author `skills`, `gear`, `quests`, `titles`, `shop`, and `log`.

**A render grammar is authored (Section 15.1).** Fixed 78-cell width; a required blank-row spacing law; a five-cell status gutter carrying exactly two glyphs (`!` actionable, `Δ` changed since the most recent promoted checkpoint); fixed-column tables with prose confined to indented continuation rows; no truncation, ever; and `none` beneath an empty group's header so the window's shape is constant.

**Rank always renders bracketed** — `[E-Rank]` through `[S-Rank]`, in every panel, column, and continuation row, including a Card or System Rank and a Stat Passive's next rung. A bare letter is never rendered. This matches the form item lines and skill lines already use throughout the profile.

**Stat Passives get their own group.** 1.45 listed two skill groups and forbade inferring a third, while separately giving Stat Passives a different row grammar — so five rows with different columns sat inside `SKILLS · PASSIVE`. Section 15.3.1 authors `ACTIVE`, `PASSIVE`, and `STAT PASSIVE` as three groups with one row shape each. **No skill is reclassified**: a Stat Passive was a Stat Passive under 1.45 and is one now; only the group it renders in changed.

**Cash and pending payouts render beside gold.** The Console's and `/system gear`'s `FUNDS` block shows System gold, the Bearer's last-known USD balance, and filed-but-unreceived payouts on separate rows. **They are never totalled**, because Section 12.5's no-exchange rule holds and the layout is built to show it: the window offers no conversion between the two currencies. Cash is labeled `last known` — the System holds no bank account, and the row renders what the Bearer knows of his own balance from the campaign ownership ledger, not a live poll. Section 12.9's standing statement that USD is mundane wallet money and never a System good is unamended.

**The `MANA` owner ruling is preserved in its new form.** The 2026-07-30 ruling (Section 20.3) forbids abbreviating a skill's Mana cost to `M<n>`. The skills table carries a column headed `MANA` with the cost beneath it, so the word still appears in full and the abbreviation remains forbidden.

**Stale cross-references are repointed.** An earlier renumber left Sections 14.3, 16, and 18 citing Sections 15.6, 15.7, and 15.8, which have not existed for several versions. Message-block citations now point to Section 14.5, `/system titles` to Section 15.3.4, and the class panel to Section 15.5. **This corrects a citation, not a rule** — the rules cited were always the ones now named.

**Sections outside 15 are otherwise unamended.** Sections 3.9, 4.4, 6.2, 7.2, 7.4, 8.1, 8.4, 11.5, 12.5, 12.9, 14.1, 14.3, 14.4, 14.5, 16, 18, and 20.5 all stand exactly as written in 1.45. Section 7.4's rendering paragraph and Section 8.4.4's panel-order sentence are restated to describe the new columns; the ordering and content law they carry is unchanged.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.45 → 1.46 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration.

**No stored state moves at adoption.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP total, mastery counter, use count, quest counter, deadline, streak, gold balance, cash balance, item, binding, title, or Rank is recomputed, re-derived, re-rolled, or rewritten. `system_state` is byte-equivalent across the adoption except for its `profile_version` string. The panels render the same values they rendered before, in a different arrangement.

**Nothing about the System's behavior changes.** No trigger tier moves between Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3; no notification is added, removed, or reworded; Section 14.4's information boundary is untouched; and the System still never lies, never resolves an action, and never surfaces for anyone but its Bearer. A Bearer restored under 1.46 knows exactly what he knew under 1.45.

**No past render is reissued and no past scene is reread.** A `/system` window rendered in a prior session stands as the record of that moment. This adoption does not re-render it, does not correct it to the new layout, and does not reinterpret anything resolved in the scene around it. Every resolved outcome stands.

**Adoption re-renders nothing on its own.** The next `/system` call after adoption renders under 1.46; no bulk re-render of panels is performed at adoption, and none is licensed from outside play.

**The command surface is additive.** `/system all`, `/system gear`, and `/system <panel> <item>` are new; `/system equipment` and `/system inventory` continue to resolve, now as aliases. No command was removed and no command changed what state it reads.

**Runtime command routing.** A live campaign carrying a `commands` dispatch block in its startup file updates its `/system equipment` and `/system inventory` entries to the merged `/system gear` panel and its dispatch anchors to the new section slugs. This is routing metadata, not campaign canon, and it moves no stored value.

**No engine dependency.** No Persistent Object structure changes, no stored record moves, and no engine decision is required. Data Model 0.1.6 remains unchanged.

Record adoption as `EVT-000276`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.45-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.46 at readiness.
