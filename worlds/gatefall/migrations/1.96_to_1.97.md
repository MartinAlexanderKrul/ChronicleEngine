## What 1.97 changes

**1.96 had the restriction on the wrong half.** It priced every step by ledgered container and laid one blanket instance-boundary rule across all of them, anchors included. The owner's ruling is the better split and it is the one this version implements: **the ordinary step is the constrained one; the anchor is the free one.** Nothing else about the class moves.

### 1. Cost is soft bands, not measured containers

1.96 asked which ledgered container held both ends - place entity, district, metro - and charged 6/8/12/20/40. That is precise and it is the wrong kind of precision: it turns every step into a lookup, and it made the cheapest possible step cost 6.

The bands are now **described, and the Runtime picks the one the fiction is obviously in**:

```text
     line of sight                                         1
     close by      the next room, the far side of the yard 3
     further       across the district                     4
     the city      anywhere in the metro he is standing in 5
     past the metro it doubles at each further step out:  10 / 20 / 40
```

Where two bands could both fit, **the more expensive one governs** - ambiguity resolves against the Bearer, as it did in 1.96. The shape is deliberate: moving inside a fight is nearly free, crossing the world is an undertaking.

### 2. The in-scene step drops from Mana 6 to 1

Its price was never the Mana. A class whose signature move is rationed by pool arithmetic at four figures of Intelligence is rationed by nothing; **the once-per-exchange line is the constraint** and always was. This is a rendering of what the rule already did rather than a loosening of it.

### 3. The ordinary travelling step keeps the walls

It **never crosses an instance boundary in either direction** - not out of a Gate, a Section 9.6 red gate, a Section 17 instant dungeon, a Section 8.3 penalty zone or a Section 18.2 trial, and not into one. Unchanged from 1.96 in substance; what changed is that this is now a rule about *that step* rather than about every step.

### 4. An anchored step is unrestricted and flat

**Flat 5 Mana from any distance** - no band, no doubling, no ceiling - and **not bound by the instance walls above.** It crosses out of a Gate, out of a red gate, out of an instant dungeon, and back in.

**This is a real grant of power and it is stated as one.** A Riftwalker standing in a sealed interior with an anchor outside it is not sealed in, and no other awakened on Earth can say that. Section 9.6 authors the red gate as the anomaly with *no retreat*; this is the profile's only answer to it, and it belongs to a class that spent its trial on positioning.

**Two exceptions, and only two: the Section 8.3 penalty zone and the Section 18.2 class trial.** Neither is a puncture in the world - both are instances the **System itself** builds, and Section 6.7 already names them as the two content systems the System picks *for* him precisely because he cannot walk away. **The forge has no back door.** A penalty zone he could anchor out of stops being a penalty, and it is the campaign's only remaining imposed danger source (`F-046`); a trial he could leave measures nothing. An anchor inside either is unreachable from outside and unusable from within until the instance releases him.

**An anchor inside an ordinary instance still dies with it.** The interior ceases to exist at the clear window's end or the key's expiry (Sections 9.7, 17) and the anchor is released with it, returning the slot.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value, threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, price or title moves.** Flash Step is untouched by this edge - it keeps 1.96's perception binding, its lost metre band, and `Interpose`. Riftwalker's anchor cap formula, free setting and release, blind arrival, the anchor-sense rungs, the answering step, warded ground, deflection percentages, the growth table and the level-50 Threshold Trial are all as 1.96 left them.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.97** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Re-render the class block's costs.** The `RIFTS` panel shows the in-scene step at Mana 1, the travelling step on the band ladder, and a new anchored-step line at flat 5. No counter, anchor, or provenance changes.

**3. Nothing is recomputed and no past step is repriced.** Mana already spent stands. Both the band ladder and the anchored step's flat cost apply from adoption forward. **No scene resolved under 1.96's container pricing is re-adjudicated.**

**4. The nine set anchors are preserved exactly** - names, places, `set_event` provenance, disturbance history. What changed is what a step to one costs and what walls it ignores.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs steps 1 and 2 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0120 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
