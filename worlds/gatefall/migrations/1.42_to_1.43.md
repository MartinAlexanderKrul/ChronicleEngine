## What 1.43 changed

**The first exception to Section 2 since this profile was written.** Gatefall's founding division is that one person grows and nobody else does: an awakened's capability is their fixed Rank, their pools are read from the Section 6.1 table, and nothing they do, earn, train for, or survive will move either. 1.43 leaves all of that standing and adds exactly one hand that can reach it — **the Bearer's**, spending a Section 12.5 stat elixir on another awakened.

New **Section 12.5.1** governs it in full:

- **Vitality and Intelligence only.** Strength, Agility, and Perception cannot be raised this way; Section 13.1 resolves those by Rank and a point has nothing to attach to.
- **Uncapped for a non-Bearer.** Section 12.5's "max 3 lifetime per stat" limits the Bearer's own progression and does not bind what he spends on someone else. Gold is the limit: 5,000 g a point.
- **Flat addition, not a Stat sheet.** `maximum_health = (Rank Health × variance) + 4 × elixir Vitality`; `maximum_mana = (Rank Mana × variance) + 2 × elixir Intelligence`. No Stat sheet is created, no level, XP, skill, mastery, or equipment Stat line follows, and the Section 4.3 domain rating is untouched — the recipient still resolves every contested action at their Rank.
- **Section 14.4 is unmoved, and the recipient still feels it.** No window, no notification, no number, no System voice. But this is the recipient's own body rather than a technique arriving in it, and the change registers immediately as depth — more to spend, and further to the bottom. A mender will know something happened the moment it does without being able to say what.
- **Outside the bracket.** Section 6.1.2's variance is authored once and never recomputed; elixir points add flat on top of the varied pool. The first point past a Rank's bracket ceiling makes the recipient a number no hunter of that Rank can be — real from that instant, invisible until a Flux Sight read at the C rung, a BGM Ranking Bench instrument, or a Section 19.2 re-assessment measures it.
- **True Rank advances; the card does not.** When both totals reach the next Rank's **flat** Section 6.1 values, the recipient's true Rank advances and they resolve at it in full, because Section 13.1 makes Rank the complete description of a body. Their licence still records what BGM measured at Awakening, so Section 9.4's party minimums, contract-Rank ceilings, and sponsor eligibility keep reading the card until re-assessment. Section 19.3's **reawakening** is the only sanctioned explanation available — and it remains a lie, because nothing was mismeasured.
- **The state between Ranks has no name.** Above a bracket ceiling and below the next Rank's floor is neither Rank and not reawakened. The world has no box, no procedure, and no precedent for it. That is deliberate.

**`GTF-OVR-002` and Section 2 are amended to admit it.** The override's scope clause previously read that a fixed-Rank entity's pools "never grow"; it now reads that they never grow *of themselves* and names Section 12.5.1 as the single intervention that moves them. Section 2's scope table carries the same qualification.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.42 → 1.43 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration.

**Nothing has happened yet.** No stat elixir has ever been purchased, transferred, or consumed in any campaign on this profile. There is no recipient, no elixir point, and no altered pool anywhere in the repository at adoption, so there is nothing to migrate and no prior scene to reinterpret. Every pool in every ledger remains exactly its Section 6.1 table value times its Section 6.1.2 variance.

**No past transfer is reread as this rule.** The Bearer has handed System goods to a non-Bearer twice — a Stone Skin rune (`EVT-000178`) and a Standard Mana Potion (`EVT-000180`), both to Owen Callahan (`ENT-000139`). Neither was an elixir, neither moved a pool's maximum, and neither is retroactively an instance of Section 12.5.1. The rune taught a technique under Section 7.1; the potion restored expended capacity without touching capacity itself, which is precisely the distinction `OBJ-18` turns on.

**`OBJ-18` is not resolved by adoption.** The objective records a promise to enlarge Owen Callahan's well and, until now, no world-legal mechanism. 1.43 supplies the mechanism; it does not supply the gold, the decision, or the consequences. The Bearer holds 13,151 g against 5,000 g a point, and the first point alone would carry that recipient past his bracket ceiling into a state the world cannot classify. What was an impossible promise is now an expensive and dangerous one. That is a change in the objective's shape, not a change to any stored value, and no ledger figure moves at adoption.

**The Bearer's own elixir cap is unchanged.** Section 12.5's max 3 lifetime per stat continues to bind the Bearer's own consumption exactly as before. Only the give-away case is uncapped.

Record adoption as `EVT-000251`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.42-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.43 at readiness.
