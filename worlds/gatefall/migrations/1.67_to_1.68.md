## What 1.68 changed

**The five classes were not within reach of one another, and two of them were unfinished.**

Section 18 offers exactly one class per campaign, permanently, at level 25 — the single most consequential irreversible choice the Bearer makes. Audited against its own text, the roster looked like this:

| Class | Acquisition | Scales on | Growth authored? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shadowbinder** | elites + horde, both capped and both binding | **Intelligence** | yes |
| **Devourer** | 5 fragments against a catalog of 5 | level | yes, but the cap never applies |
| **Soulforged Armory** | 5 weapons, of which 1–2 are reachable | level | yes, but the cap grants nothing |
| **Echo Knight** | 3 fixed slots | — | **no** |
| **Riftwalker** | none at all | — | **no** |

**Shadowbinder was the only class with a working acquisition track**, and the only one whose growth read a Stat. Everything else either had a cap that could not bind or no collection to cap.

**Echo Knight and Riftwalker authored no growth.** *"At authored thresholds a slot may capture a critical, the per-echo Mana cost falls, and echo durability rises"* — no thresholds were authored. *"Rift-step range grows past line of sight at higher tiers; deflection strength and pocket capacity rise with allocation and level"* — no tiers, no numbers. Both classes were static across the twenty-five levels they were meant to carry, and the promise of growth was doing the work of growth.

**Soulforged Armory's cap was inert.** The arsenal held `level ÷ 5` bound weapons; the Bearer has two hands, the draw was once per exchange, and a bound ability functioned only while wielded. The fourth and fifth bindings each consumed a beast core to grant precisely nothing. The abilities were flat besides — the section's own worked examples read `×1.5`, `×1.25`, `−1 step` against skills the Bearer already held at `×2.45`.

**Devourer's cap equalled its catalog at the class's earliest acquisition point** (`F-022`). `floor(25 ÷ 5) = 5` slots against exactly five authored fragment kinds, at a class offered only from level 25. Every fragment it would ever hold, on day one, with the escalating cap formula rationing nothing it was written to ration.

### The repair

**Section 18.3.1 is new and states the three tracks a class owes** — acquisition under a cap that binds, magnitude, and a level-50 evolution — with the governing rule that **a cap which cannot bind is not a cap**. A limit of five against a reachable set of five rations nothing; nor does a limit of five on things only one of which can be used at a time.

**Each class's acquisition cap now scales on one Stat, and no two classes share one.** Allocation is the Bearer's only permanent freely-chosen growth, so binding each class to a different Stat makes the class choice and the allocation choice speak to each other, and ensures no build is equally good at all five.

| Class | Stat | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Shadowbinder | Intelligence | elites `INT ÷ 5` · horde `INT × 20` — unchanged |
| Devourer | Vitality | fragments `VIT ÷ 20` |
| Soulforged Armory | Strength | bound weapons `STR ÷ 15` |
| Echo Knight | Perception | echo slots `PER ÷ 20` |
| Riftwalker | Agility | rift anchors `AGI ÷ 20` |

**Devourer's catalog doubles to ten kinds** — Rend, Roar, Spines, Burrow and Bloodfeast join the original five — so the cap rations a real choice, and a held fragment may be **released** outside combat to re-choose as the fights change.

**Soulforged's arsenal grants from the shelf as well as the hand.** Every bound weapon now carries a **standing edge** active while merely stored; edges from different sources compose. A bound ability's multiplier is the **skill-rank baseline at the weapon's Rank**, so it keeps pace with an authored skill instead of being out-grown in a session.

**Echo Knight is repaired against its own signal.** An echo may **intercept one attack aimed at the Bearer**, taking it in full so the damage never reaches his pools, and deploying costs **2 less for each of his own pools below half**. Its rungs are authored against System Rank. The class was offered for Endurance and contained nothing that helped him endure; interception is the repair, and the mechanic already implied it — the section had always said an echo *"persists until a hit would injure it."*

**Riftwalker gains the acquisition track it never had.** **Rift anchors** are set by holding a position for an exchange and stepped to afterwards without line of sight, capped on Agility, permanent until released. Its rungs are authored against System Rank, and its level-50 personal gate now runs between two of his own anchors — the map he spent twenty-five levels making.

**Shadowbinder is untouched.** It was already correct on all three tracks. It is the benchmark the other four were raised to, not a ceiling lowered to meet them.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.67 → 1.68.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.68"`.**

**2. Nothing stored changes, and nothing is recomputed.** No pool, Stat, level, XP, System Rank, Card Rank, skill, mastery, counter, title, item, gold, cash, quest, key, or candidate is touched. Section 18 governs a class, and **no campaign in this repository holds one** — `class: null` on every live character sheet — so this edge has no live subject at all.

**3. Nothing retroactive, and no class is re-adjudicated.** Should a campaign hold a class taken under 1.67 or earlier, it stands exactly as granted with everything it has accumulated; caps are re-read under the new formulas from the Bearer's existing effective Stats, and **anything held above a newly-computed cap is held but inert** under Section 18.3.1 rather than destroyed. Nothing is confiscated by a formula change.

**4. An offered-but-unentered Class Quest is unaffected.** `class_quest.status` does not move on adoption. The trial is sized and structured at entry under Sections 18.2.1–18.2.3 (Profile 1.67), and the class it offers is the 1.68 version of that class.

**5. No campaign owes a re-audit.** No promoted Event, counter, or progression audit derives from Section 18, because no class has ever been held.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.67-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.68 at readiness.
