## What 1.55 changed

Both changes came from the player reading the finished Section 7.3 tables back and asking why two skills appeared to be refused.

**Broken Rhythm's B-Rank rung was named *Denied*.** The name describes what the rung does to an opponent — it denies a telegraphed opening — but it sat in a column whose neighbours legitimately read *deferred*, *unauthored*, and *open question*, so it parsed as the rung itself being withheld. It is now **Cadence break**. **The effect text is unchanged in every particular.** A rung name that collides with the vocabulary of withholding is a rendering defect of the same kind Section 20.2 names: the reader cannot tell an authored thing from an absent one, and that distinction is the entire point of giving every skill a row.

**Dimensional Projection was the one skill in the tables with no Rank ladder.** 1.52 deferred it deliberately — the rungs were to be authored "at ratification, not speculatively now", on the ground that a skill which does not exist yet should not carry them. It is an unformed `progression_candidate`: no Bearer in any Gatefall campaign has demonstrated it.

**The owner ruling goes the other way, and Twin Fang is why.** The ladder is what makes a skill *safe to form*. Twin Fang was made ascension-eligible at 1.52 with no Rank axis whatsoever, and its first ascension would have destroyed 37.5% of its damage — an offer was opened to the Bearer before anyone computed the trade. Dimensional Projection carried the identical defect: its range is authored by mastery alone, so ascension read **×0.25**, twenty metres falling to five. Deferring the rungs did not defer the trap; it only deferred noticing it. Authoring now closes it before the skill can exist rather than after.

- **Range band:** `+3 m for each Rank above native` — E 5–20 m, D 8–23 m, C 11–26 m, B 14–29 m, A 17–32 m, over the existing 5 / 7 / 10 / 15 / 20 mastery ladder. It carries a `magnitude_floor` from ratification.
- **Categories:** **Recall** (D) — the launched weapon returns to hand at the action's end rather than remaining where it falls; **Blind placement** (C) — the deployment point needs a held read rather than an unobstructed line of sight; **Past the wall** (B) — the point may sit beyond a barrier the weapon could not be thrown through, never crossing a Gate boundary, a Section 9.6 seal, or a Section 17 instance wall; **Recall in flight** (A) — the weapon may be recalled mid-flight and re-launched at a second target in the same action.

`tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` verifies it alongside every formed skill, including the check that the magnitude ratchet does not swallow its mastery track.

## Migration procedure

**1.54 → 1.55 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. No stored value is read, written, or re-derived beyond the version pointer.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.55"`.** That is the only stored value this edge touches.

**2. Broken Rhythm is untouched.** Its Rank, mastery level, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `mastery_progress`, `scope_floor`, effect text and Mana cost are all unchanged. Only the **name** of a rung it does not occupy has changed, and no campaign holds that rung. Re-render its `skills_known` line only if that line quotes the old rung name; the Gatefall Pendragon campaign's does not.

**3. Dimensional Projection grants nothing to anyone.** It is an unformed candidate in every campaign — `progression_candidates` status is not `ratified` for it anywhere — so there is no skill to re-derive, no counter to write, and no `magnitude_floor` to store until it forms. Its rungs apply from its ratification forward, whenever that occurs.

**4. Nothing retroactive, nothing else moved.** No past exchange is re-resolved. No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched, and no open ascension offer changes terms.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.54-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.55 at readiness.
