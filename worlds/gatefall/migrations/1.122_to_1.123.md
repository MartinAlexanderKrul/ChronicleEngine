## The finding this version exists for

**Section 9.1.7's region table is authored at tier III and says so in its own column header. The world is at tier VII.** Section 9.2.1 raises the Gate count by 1.364 across that span and the S-Rank share from 0.5% to 2%, so every figure read off that table unadjusted is **27% low on volume and four times low at the top of the ladder**. Restated at tier VII from the same rows and the same depth rules, the world opens **~3,246 Gates a day, ~283 of them A-Rank and ~55 S-Rank**, against the Bible's **~200 living S-Ranks**.

**That is 3.6 days of world S-Rank cover, where 9.1.7 computed twenty, and Section 9.3 gives an S-Rank Gate a two-day timer.** 9.1.7 built its whole treatment of the Jiu Valley on the twenty-day figure — *"the ordinary case is that one is answered."* **At tier VII that sentence is no longer true**, and Asia, holding nearly half the world's S-Ranks, sits at 2.4 days and is already inside the margin.

**Nothing was wrong when 9.1.7 was written.** This is `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 9's geometric curve reaching the rung where the top of the ladder outgrows the people who answer it. The defect is only that no section read the consequence, and the symptom the player reported is its direct result: **the campaign could see ~1.4 A/S events a day, 82% of them Chicago and Prague**, because Sections 9.1.6 and 9.1.7 both filtered on the same question — does this escalate to BGM National? — which was the right question when the protagonist was one man with one agreement and is not the right question for a founder holding fourteen branches, sixty anchors and fourteen tunnels across two continents.

## What 1.123 changes

**Section 9.1.8 (new) - Reach.** A daily A-Rank and S-Rank clock runs in four zones, and everywhere else keeps Section 9.1.7's weekly one:

- a **full-tick city** (Chicago, Prague), unchanged;
- an **operational branch**'s regulator jurisdiction (Section 13.7.5);
- the **locality** of a standing Section 18.8 anchor — a place, not a region;
- whatever a **standing agreement** itself names.

**An anchor buys a place and a branch buys a region**, because a catchment is bought by dispatch infrastructure — an officer, a bench, a filed permit, a movable roster — and an anchor is one doorway.

- **Every S-Rank Gate in reach surfaces**, named and reported, with **no daily budget**. It then rolls Section 9.11 **unchanged**, plus one modifier read off the sited region's own days-of-cover figure (`-30` under 2 days, `-15` at 2 to 4, `0` above 4). Section 9.1.7's **border rule binds without exception**: a foreign S-Rank arrives as an ask through one of its four doors and **never as a draft**.
- **Concurrent S-Rank calls are the ordinary case at tier VII and are triage, not a queue.** He answers what he can reach inside the timer; what he declines or cannot reach resolves without him, and **a new Scar because he was three hundred miles away is a legitimate outcome that must not be routed around.**
- **At most one A-Rank Gate becomes a scene on an ordinary day** — the strongest single Section 9.11 result among the day's A-Ranks in reach. Every other A-Rank that day is a count.
- **A guild's A-Rank response pool answers on a standing posture**, declared once, resolving as counts on Section 13.7.5's terms. Capacity is Section 9.4's arithmetic: 86 A-Ranks and 223 B-Ranks field **19 concurrent 16-hunter A-Rank elements**, each satisfying the `>=1 A` clause — almost exactly the footprint's own ~19.8 A-Rank Gates a day. **Running it at full tilt costs 304 hunter-slots on top of 13.7.5's 882: 1,186 against an 1,812 roster, a 65% duty rate, which is Strained.**

**Section 9.1's step 11.** The reach pass runs here, **beside** the light tick rather than inside it. The light tick rolls exactly what it always rolled.

**Section 9.1.1.** The tick ledger gains a `reach` line; `none` is a result and is written as one.

## What 1.123 supersedes, and how much of it

**Section 9.1.6's filter is superseded in part, and the notice is written on 9.1.6 itself.** *"What the wider map owes is not simulation; it is the three things that reach a national asset"* remains exactly right **for escalation to BGM National**, and its d100, its three event kinds, its band table, its roster, its promotion rule and every one of its fences stand untouched. It is no longer the whole of what reaches him, because reach is now a second and separate road.

**Section 13.7.5's A/S clauses are superseded in part, and the notice is written on 13.7.5 itself.** 13.7.5 said an A or an S in a branch's catchment surfaces and *"becomes a scene"* — and **authored no generator**, because a branch catchment had no A/S source until this version. That promise is now supplied and bounded: S always, A at one scene a day. Its second clause required a fresh on-screen commitment for the A-Rank pool **every time**, which is correct for a guild answering an A-Rank Gate a week and unrunnable for one whose regions open twenty a day.

**13.7.5's fence itself is untouched and is not weakened here.** No background pass resolves an A-Rank or S-Rank Gate at any bench depth. A standing posture is a declaration a person makes on-screen and the work it absorbs is counted, which is not the same thing as a roll deciding an A-Rank Gate's outcome.

## What did not change

**No stored field changes shape or meaning.** No checkpoint is rewritten. No XP, level, pool, gold, cash, Stat, skill or holding moves. No Gate already rolled is re-rolled and no past directed assignment is re-routed.

**Section 9.11 is unchanged** — the same `01-25` / `26-50` / `51-00` bands, the same three existing modifiers, the same three refusal tiers including the draft. 1.123 adds one modifier and routes more Gates through it; it rewrites nothing.

**Section 9.2.1's tiers and 9.2.2's ramp are unchanged**, and the tier still only ever rises. **Section 9.1.7's region table, depth rules and border rule are unchanged** — 9.1.8 restates its rows at tier VII by applying 9.2.1, and authors no second rate.

**Section 6.6 holds in full.** An A-Rank assignment is an A-Rank Gate; nothing here sizes anything to the Bearer.

**No city is authored.** Section 9.1.6's promotion bar is untouched, and a reach zone gains no districts, no board, no pressure value and no agenda advances.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.123** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Regenerate the runtime index** (`tools/generate_runtime_index.ps1`), then confirm with `-Check`.

**3. Validate** with `tools/validate_repository.ps1`.

**4. No reach zone is declared by this migration, and no posture is set.** Adoption declares no branch operational, no anchor a reach zone and no A-Rank standing posture. All three are campaign facts and belong to play. Until a campaign records them, Section 9.1.8 rolls nothing and the tick's `reach` line reads `none`.

## What is owed and not done here

**The adoption Event is campaign canon and belongs to play.** This record lands the world side only. `gatefall_pendragon_001` owes an adoption Event at its next save, covering **both 1.122 and 1.123** if neither has been adopted in play yet.

**Three declarations are owed with it**, and together they are the larger half of the work:

1. **Which branches are operational** (Section 13.7.5's four conditions, per city).
2. **Which anchors count as reach zones** — sixty anchors and fourteen tunnels exist; which of them the campaign treats as standing is not decided here.
3. **Whether the A-Rank pool carries a standing posture**, and in which catchments.

The worked figures throughout 9.1.8 — eight BGM regions, ~220 Gates a day of reach flow, ~19.8 A and ~4.4 S — are an **illustration of the arithmetic**, not a declaration that this campaign's reach is already that wide.

**Open, and deliberately not decided here.**

- **The independent S-Rank network is now load-bearing and is not priced.** The guild's member roster carries zero S-Ranks and a board seat carries no membership, so the only instruments that let anyone but Alexander answer an S-Rank Gate are standing arrangements with S-Ranks who are not his employees. 9.1.8 names this and authors no mechanism for it.
- **Asia is deliberately unreachable.** Forty S-Rank Gates a day open there and none of them surface, because the guild holds no office and he holds no anchor. That silence is the argument for the next branch and is meant to be felt as one; nothing here softens it.
- **The A-Rank posture's interaction with 13.7.5's duty-rate bands is stated but not exercised.** 65% is Strained on 13.7.5's own table; what a guild actually declines at that band is fiction, not a number.
