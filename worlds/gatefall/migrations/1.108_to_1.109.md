## What 1.109 changes

**Section 7.1 authored four acquisition routes and every one of them is solitary.** A rune is found, a book is found, a Stat threshold is crossed, and a formation candidate is noticed by an audit the Bearer does not control. **Nobody in this world teaches anybody anything**, and until this edge that was not a rule written anywhere - it was the unstated consequence of two rules that are each correct on their own. Section 2 leaves exactly one System in existence. Section 13.1 gives every other hunter a fixed Rank and no five-stat sheet, so a hunter who is superb at something is superb *at their Rank*, with nothing underneath it to hand over.

### 1. Why the gap surfaced now

The live campaign reached the state where every other axis is spent: **level 113, System Rank S since level 50, every skill at [S-Rank], five skills at Master reading "no further mastery movement possible", the Section 7.5 ascension ceiling clamped, the rune and skill-book tables exhausted, and 64,964,281 gold against a shop with nothing left to sell him.** Section 12.5 states the wall in its own words - ***gold buys width, never hands*** - and 1.109 is the first route in this profile that offers hands.

**It is deliberately not a larger opponent.** The owner considered and rejected an unlettered content band above S: bigger Rank Health is the same fight with more arithmetic in it, it inflates a Gate ladder that is working, and Section 18.13.2 is right that a rung authored past level 100 answers *"nothing new"* with *"more of what you already have too much of."* The ruling recorded above is narrower and is what unlocks this edge: **content may exceed the Bearer, so other System-bearers may know things he does not** - and what he gains from them is instruction rather than a kill.

### 2. Route 5, and what it is not

| | Routes 1-4 | Route 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Source | an item, or a Stat threshold | a person holding a System |
| Transfer | inscription, instant | demonstration, over scenes |
| Result authored in | this profile's tables | the teacher's own entry |
| Enters at | its item's Rank, or the ceiling | the ceiling, or the teacher's Rank if lower |
| Mastery | Novice | Novice |
| Can be passed on | no | **yes** |

**Only a System-bearer may teach, and only another System-bearer may receive.** That is Section 2 restated rather than a balance decision, and it is what makes the route rare rather than a new shop tab: the teachers it contemplates are the failed champions of `270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4 and nothing else authored.

**A taught technique enters as a candidate on the existing Skill-Formation Audit.** The teacher supplies the signature, so tests 1-3 are satisfied at the first scene rather than argued - including test 3's combination clause, which has always permitted composition and never had anyone to suggest one. The teacher's authored entry supplies the name, Rank, cost, effect and mastery axis, so ratification asks the owner to approve rather than to invent. **Tests 4 and 5 stand unchanged: three distinct qualifying scenes, materially contributing.** Being shown a thing is not holding it.

**Nothing here raises a ceiling.** A taught technique enters at `System Rank + 1` clamped, or lower if the teacher's own entry is lower, and it enters at Novice. Mastery is still three qualifying scenes a level and twelve to Master, in the Bearer's own hands. **Section 12.5's sentence is preserved word for word, and so is the sentence beside it:** mastery is never bought, and it is not taught either.

### 3. Transmissibility, which is the actual grant

A skill from routes 1-4 is an **inscription** - the System wrote a capability into him and he was never shown how it works. A skill from route 5 is a **method he watched somebody perform.** Live state records `acquisition_route`, `transmissible`, and `taught_by` on the skill; `transmissible` is true on route 5 only, fixed at acquisition, and **never granted retroactively to a skill already held.**

He may teach a `transmissible` technique to an already-awakened recipient **with no item consumed**, at `min(his own Rank in it, the recipient's fixed Rank)` - the Consumption Settlement terms with the item removed. It takes scenes rather than a sentence, it cannot awaken anyone, and a Section 13.1 recipient still gains no mastery track and no Stat sheet.

**This is the first mechanism in the profile by which anyone in the world gains a technique the Bearer did not loot for them.** Section 18.12.5 states that a world acquiring one person who can answer anything stops building the capacity to answer it itself; Section 12.5.1 already lets him spend gold on another awakened's **pools**. Route 5 is the **capability** half, and together they are the only way he can put back what his own existence is taking out. It is not free: every transmission is a Section 19 exposure thread, and an awakened performing a method no instructor on Earth teaches is a louder anomaly than a potion in an evidence locker.

### 4. Section 13.1.1 - the teachers had to be credible or route 5 was decoration

**1.109's first draft made the champions teachers and left them priced at Combat Tier equivalent S**, which the live Bearer removes in one standard resolution. **A teacher one-shot mid-lesson is not a teacher**, and the route would have been unusable on arrival.

**The fix is not a larger pool, because that is the thing the owner rejected.** Section 13.1 binds NPC *hunters* - fixed-Rank engine characters, because the world grades bodies and stops there - and **it has never had anything to say about a System-bearer.** Section 6.1 already wrote the governing rule and wrote it in the singular by accident: *"The Bearer is the declared exception. His maximum Health is the direct expression of effective Vitality."* That is a statement about what a System does to the body carrying it, not a courtesy extended to one man.

**So a bearer resolves as a bearer.** Five Stats, `4 x Vitality` and `2 x Intelligence` pools, skills with mastery, Stat Passives, a class where one was taken - and **no Rank, no Rank Health, no Section 13.1 ledger entry.** The population is closed to `270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4 and nobody else; nothing in the world qualifies and no route in this profile makes anyone a bearer.

**Sevrin comes out smaller than the Bearer, deliberately.** Health 7,600 against his 10,620, no equipment line at all, lower Stats across the board - four thousand years of scraps off the Mill against thirty-three days of S-Rank instant dungeons. **A single clean Rupture kills her.** What she has is forty centuries of making sure it is not clean, and an axis he has no answer to: she held a bridge, and what she perfected is **closing a way through.** She can bar an anchored step. On the far side, where Section 2 drains 10% of maximum Mana an hour and the way home is a 5-Mana step, **keeping him somewhere is the lethal outcome and no blow needs to land.** Ilith is smaller still at 4,960 Health, will not fight him at all, and is dangerous because she is faster than he is and wants a door he can leave open by accident.

**Two bearers each carry strikes exceeding the other's pool, so the exchange turns on whether a strike lands rather than on how many are traded.** A Runtime softens that in neither direction.

**`270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4.2 is re-authored under this rule and three figures are added.** Through 1.108 only two of Section 4.1's four positions had a holder, which left the two that matter most to a campaign as paragraphs with nobody standing in them: the position that exists to **help him** and the position that exists to **stop him**.

| Figure | Position | Health | Why they are dangerous |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Sevrin** | Stop it | 7,600 | denial - she can bar an anchored step |
| **Ilith** | Out | 4,960 | will not fight; wants a door he leaves open |
| **Aumry** | Reach the next one | 8,200 | not dangerous at all, and the reason route 5 exists |
| **Orenne** | Let it finish | 4,040 | one clean connection either way |
| **Tolo** | none, deliberately | 6,720 | Perception 2,890 - the only stat here above the Bearer's |

**Every one of them is smaller than the Bearer's 10,620 and none carries an equipment line**, asserted by a gate leg that fails if any authored figure exceeds his pool. Aumry is the worst fighter in the file at Strength 720 and the most valuable person in it - what he teaches is the transmission half of route 5. **Tolo is the figure the Bearer has already wronged**: `EVT-000783` took the Ninth Quiet's entire archive and `EVT-000784` set an anchor inside a long-inhabited dwelling, and Tolo has been reading that library for centuries. Nobody was home because his Perception exceeds the Bearer's and he watched a stranger cross the stratum from a long way off.

**Section 2's resolution rule now names two forms rather than one.** A thing that is not a person states a Combat Tier equivalent; a System-bearer states a sheet. The champions take the second form, because a scale capping at S prices the only people who can teach him on a ruler pinned at its stop.

## What did not change

**No Gate moves and no pool is inflated.** Section 13.1.1 derives a bearer's Health from Vitality on Section 6.1's existing Bearer formula and adds no table, no band and no rung; both named figures come out **smaller than the Bearer**. Section 9.2's distribution, the escalation ramp, every Rank, Rank Health, Rank Mana, kill XP row, milestone, break timer, population formula, anomaly table and archetype are byte-identical. **S remains the ceiling of the Rank ladder in both directions**, and the unlettered content band considered during design was rejected and is authored nowhere.

**No mastery rung is added.** The ladder is still Novice, Practiced, Adept, Expert, Master, 1 through 5. A sixth level was considered and rejected in favour of transmissibility, which delivers the same campaign effect without touching the five-name mapping hardcoded in `tools/validate_repository.ps1` and `tools/test_gatefall_quest_contract.ps1`.

**No existing skill changes.** Section 7.5 ascension, Section 7.4 mastery, every skill entry in Section 7.2, every price in Section 12.5, and the Section 11.3 skill-book table are untouched. `instruction_binding` and the Consumption Settlement branches are unchanged - route 5 sits beside them and replaces none of them.

**Section 13.1 is not relaxed.** An ordinary awakened still has no Stat sheet, no mastery track and no System, and route 5 gives them none. What they gain is one technique at their own Rank, exactly as a rune already gives them.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.109** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. No skill is backfilled.** `transmissible` is written by route 5 alone. Every skill acquired before this edge is non-transmissible by the rule's own terms, and writing `transmissible: false` onto an existing loadout is churn that changes nothing - **absence is the negative case and no counter, Rank or mastery value moves.**

**3. No candidate is re-scored.** A `rejected` formation candidate stays rejected on its recorded reason. Section 7.1's existing clause already reopens one by owner ruling or a materially different signature, and a teacher supplying a signature is the ordinary operation of that clause rather than an exception this edge adds.

**4. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
