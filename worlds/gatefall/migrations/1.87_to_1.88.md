## What 1.88 changes, and the sentence that caused it

**Section 12.9 opened with a sentence that is exhaustive by construction**:

> *"Every item he owns lives there **unless he has chosen to hold or wear it**."*

Two dispositions - **stored** or **carried** - and the world routinely produces a third. An object the Bearer **still owns** can be **physically in a named party's hands**: left with an artificer to work on, lodged as a condition of an arrangement, lent, or deposited. That state had no legal position anywhere in the profile. A grep of 1.87 for `custody` returned **zero**.

**The consequence was deletion, and it is on the record.** `EVT-000636` removed the heartwood core-mass and the frost-fused carapace plating from `100_CHARACTER_SHEET.md` outright, because both had gone into Priscilla Nakamura's physical custody at `EVT-000578` and a two-state model had nowhere to hold them. The removal was the correct repair *given the model* - the sheet had been rendering them as held inventory, which was also false, and one had already misrendered in a `/system gear` panel. **Both readings were wrong because the model only offered two wrong answers.**

**What that cost, stated concretely.** By 1.87 the Bearer's own panel omitted: a **Dormant Core [S-Rank]**, the highest-Rank object in the campaign; a **heartwood core-mass** warm nine hours after its kill with no mechanism for being warm; a **frost-fused carapace plating [B-Rank]**; **three C-Rank Mana Crystals** left as project stock; and a **Seer's Lens [D-Rank] pair** taken out of play by an open fusion. Six holdings, none of them sold, none of them given away, all of them still his - and a Bearer reading `/system gear` would have seen none of it.

## The rule

> **Custody is possession, never title. An owned thing that is somewhere else is still an owned thing.**

New clause at **Section 12.9** and a sixth group, **`IN CUSTODY`**, at **Section 15.3.2**.

**Ownership does not move.** No sale, no transfer, no claim accrues to the holder. The item stays on the Bearer's ledger for as long as the custody stands and renders in its own panel group rather than disappearing.

**A custody record names three things and is invalid without them: who holds it, since when, and what ends it** - a return condition, a completion, or a stated open end. *"Left with someone"* is not a custody; it is an item the ledger has lost, which is the failure this section exists to close.

**The cost is reach, and it is the whole of the mechanic.** A custodied item is **not stored**: it cannot be withdrawn to hand, cannot be launched, and applies no Section 11.5 line. Critically it is **not a legal target for Section 12.9 remote storage** while the custody stands - *even though a core sitting untouched on a bench satisfies that clause's bare wording of "no person is holding, wearing, or carrying."* **He consented to it being there.** Recovering it is an act in the fiction with whatever that costs - turning up, asking, negotiating, or stealing - and never a free thought. **A custody a Bearer can silently reverse is not a custody**, and an artificer who can be robbed by a man thinking about it has no reason to accept work.

**It ends** on its stated condition, on the holder returning it, or on the Bearer recovering it in play; on any of those the item returns to ordinary stored or worn state with nothing else changed. **Destruction, consumption or loss while in custody resolves in the fiction on the holder's terms**, because that risk is exactly what handing an object to somebody else buys and costs.

**The panel group is a location, not a kind.** Section 15.3.2's five kinds classify *what a holding is*; custody records *where it is and who has it*. A custodied item keeps its kind - a core in an artificer's hands is still Materials - and renders in `IN CUSTODY` **instead of** its kind group, never in both, so no holding appears twice and every kind rule's count stays honest.

## What did not change

**No Rank ladder, baseline, rung, category, dividend, XP threshold, price, timer, population, band or distribution moves.** Section 12.9's storage and withdrawal rules are untouched for every item that is actually stored; custody is a state an item can be in, not a change to what storage does. Section 11.5's item lines, Section 12.10's fusion, Section 12.8's commissioning and Section 12.5's shop are all untouched.

**No item changes hands and no ownership moves in either direction.** This migration does not give the Bearer anything and does not take anything from a holder.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.88"`.**

**2. `system_state.inventory` gains a `custody` list.** It initialises **empty** in every campaign. Migrating creates no custody record, because a custody is a fact about the fiction and the migration does not know any.

**3. Reconstructing an existing custody is play, not migration.** A campaign whose fiction already put objects in someone's hands - Gatefall: Pendragon has six such holdings at Priscilla Nakamura's bench as of Profile 1.87 - **restores them to the ledger through a campaign Event at its own anchor**, sourced from the Events that placed them (`EVT-000578`, `EVT-000636`, `EVT-000637`, `EVT-000639`, `EVT-000640`, `EVT-000641`) and from `130_NPCS_AND_FACTIONS.md`'s relationship record. **Do not backfill it here.** Each record needs holder, since, and end condition, and only the campaign's own canon holds those.

**4. No stored value moves.** No level, XP, Stat, Rank, mastery level, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `rank_ascensions`, `dividend_level`, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item line, gold, cash, quest, class, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

**5. No past Event, roll, damage figure, kill, clear or outcome is altered**, including `EVT-000636`. Its deletion stands as the historical record of what was decided under a two-state model; it is superseded on the merits rather than rewritten, and `F-043` carries the finding.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor. Immutable Profile 1.87-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.88 at readiness.

## What this does not close

**It does not author custody in the other direction.** An item *someone else owns* that the **Bearer** is holding - borrowed gear, evidence, something handed to him to look at - has the same shape and is not authored here. The Ironline roster folder he physically carries is the live example, and it is currently modelled as ordinary Special inventory. If that distinction is ever asked for, it is a new ruling.

**It does not author a dispute.** What happens when a holder refuses to return a custodied item, sells it, or denies holding it is fiction resolved in play, not a mechanic. The clause says recovery is an act with a cost; it deliberately does not price that act.

**It does not author insurance, escrow, or a fee for holding.** Section 12.8's commissioning economics are untouched; a custody is not a contract and this section adds none.

**It does not make a custodied item safe.** Destruction or loss resolves on the holder's terms, and nothing here gives the Bearer a claim, a replacement, or a roll to prevent it.
