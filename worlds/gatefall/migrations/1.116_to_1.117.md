## What 1.117 changes

**New Section 7.6.4, Election.** Succession's transcription (7.6.1-7.6.3) is unchanged as a yield, but a technique whose clause removes an option from its own holder — rather than binding only the world around him — is no longer transcribed automatically. The render at the moment of transcription is the only look the Bearer gets; for each entry whose clause binds his own choice, he elects it or does not in the same beat. A declined technique is not acquired: not banked, not stored, not recoverable later by this section. A technique bound in both directions (protecting him from the world *and* binding his own timing or choice) may instead be retained with its clause restated one-directionally, where the fiction supports drawing that line without inventing a new effect.

**Applied immediately in the same ruling, on `gatefall_pendragon_001`'s own protagonist sheet:**

| Technique | Disposition |
|---|---|
| `Plain Answer` [C-Rank] Master — *cannot lie about a fact already asked directly and plainly* | **Declined.** Struck from `100_CHARACTER_SHEET.md`. Not banked; not recoverable. |
| `Procedural Calm` [B-Rank] Master — *cannot be rushed or made to act early* | **Retained, clause restated one-directionally.** Protects him from outside pressure; no longer binds his own timing. Its recorded `EVT-000961` use is preserved. |

Live technique total moves from nineteen (as promoted at `EVT-000959`) to **eighteen**.

## What did not change

**Promoted narration is not rewritten.** `160_CAMPAIGN_CHRONICLE.md` and `170_CHANGELOG.md` keep their count of nineteen at `EVT-000959`, on the same checkpoint discipline `worlds/gatefall/270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4.2 already applies to the Sevrin pronoun correction — the record of what transcribed is accurate to the moment it was written; only live state moves.

**Section 7.6's economics are untouched.** A declined technique still counts against the body for exposure and legal purposes exactly as a taken one does, and `F-065`'s route-5 comparison is unaffected — electing fewer techniques buys back nothing on the instruction side.

**No XP, level, gold, cash, or Stat moves.** This is a ledger-shape change on the skills list alone, exactly as 1.115 -> 1.116's consolidation was.

**Nothing else is swept.** Section 16's assessed titles have not been audited against the election test, and F-068's remaining open design questions (whether the rule belongs one layer up in `012_ENGINE_RUNTIME.md`; whether election should instead happen on a stated window rather than at the kill) are unresolved by this edge — it closes only the profile-layer gap the flag named as open.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.117** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Any technique acquired via `succession` under an earlier capture is tested against Section 7.6.4's rule on restoration.** Where its clause binds only the world (protection), nothing changes. Where its clause binds the holder's own choice and the capture predates this edge, the technique stands as transcribed — this migration does not retroactively re-open an election already played past; it governs new Succession events from adoption forward, exactly as `gatefall_pendragon_001`'s own `EVT-000959` was handled by direct owner ruling rather than by this migration's procedure.

**3. A capture restored across this edge with no Succession-sourced technique present runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
