## What 1.89 changes, and the arithmetic that caused it

**A quest paid less than a Gate, and the most time-critical quest paid least of anything.**

Section 3.4 awards a Gate-clear milestone - E 70, D 150, C 320, B 700, A 1,500, S 3,200 - for killing a Gate's boss, and it pays that **on top of every kill fought through to reach it**. Under 1.88:

| | Formula | E | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gate clear (3.4) | milestone **+ kill XP** | 70+ | 150+ | 320+ | 700+ | 1,500+ | 3,200+ |
| Hidden quest (8.4.3) | milestone, flat | 70 | 150 | 320 | 700 | 1,500 | 3,200 |
| Urgent quest (8.4.2) | 4x common-kill | 40 | 100 | 240 | 600 | 1,600 | 4,000 |

**A Hidden quest paid strictly less than clearing a Gate of its own Rank**, because it drew the milestone figure alone with no kill XP beside it. **An Urgent quest paid less again** at every Rank below A - 100 against 150 at D-Rank - for the one quest class the profile defines by somebody else's life being in immediate danger.

**Against what.** A Gate is roughly two hours. Measured at the moment of this migration, campaign time 2026-08-23 04:15: `warehouse-meridian-ownership` had been attached **sixteen and a half in-fiction days**, `marnie-disappearance` **nine**, `the-sublevel-under-the-cicero-scar` **three**. The Bearer's own record shows the shape plainly - a standing investigation running two weeks paid 150, a Gate cleared in an afternoon paid 150 and every kill inside it.

## The rule

> **A quest is paid at the Gate-clear milestone one Rank above the Bearer's.**

Both Section 8.4.2 and Section 8.4.3 now read from Section 3.4's ladder shifted one rung:

| | E | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Hidden and Urgent reward | 150 | 320 | 700 | 1,500 | 3,200 | 6,800 |

**No figure here is invented.** Every value except one is a row Section 3.4 already authors; the shift reuses the existing ladder rather than introducing a multiplier, which is why the Rank+1 form was chosen over "twice the current figure."

**S-Rank continues the ladder's own construction and is the single derived value.** Section 3.4's rungs double and round up by a step that itself doubles: 70->140->**150**, 150->300->**320**, 320->640->**700**, 700->1,400->**1,500**, 1,500->3,000->**3,200**. Continuing it gives 3,200->6,400->**6,800**. It is named here rather than left implicit because it is the one number a reader cannot check against an existing row.

**Urgent and Hidden now pay the same figure at the same Rank.** That is deliberate: 1.88's split paid the time-critical class *less*, and nothing in either section's design justifies the ordering it had.

## What did not change

**Section 3.4 itself is untouched.** The Gate-clear milestone is what it was; this migration changes what a *quest* reads from it, not the ladder.

**No Rank baseline, rung, category, dividend, XP threshold, price, timer, population, band or distribution moves.** Kill XP (3.3), daily rewards (3.5), award timing (3.6), quest capacity (8.4.1) and slot release (8.4.4) are all untouched.

**Failure, expiry and abandonment still award nothing** and still impose no System penalty, in both sections.

**Reward is still fixed at attachment or acceptance** and still not repriced by later leveling. That clause governs *the Bearer's own advancement* - it is not a promise that the law itself never changes, which is what the repricing below turns on.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.89** on the campaign's live character sheet.

**2. Reprice every quest whose reward has not yet settled**, to the Rank+1 figure for the `reward_rank` already recorded beside it. This is a deliberate one-time exception to both sections' *later leveling does not reprice it*, taken on the owner ruling above: that clause binds the Bearer's advancement, not a change in the law. Applied at this migration:

| Quest | Rank | 1.88 | 1.89 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| `warehouse-meridian-ownership` | D | 150 | 320 |
| `marnie-disappearance` | D | 150 | 320 |
| `the-sublevel-under-the-cicero-scar` | B | 700 | 1,500 |

**3. A quest whose reward has ALREADY SETTLED is not repriced, and this is a limit of the migration rather than an oversight.** `uncontained` completed at 2026-08-22 21:10 and its 1,600 XP settled immediately under Section 3.6. Repricing it would grant 1,600 XP retroactively against a live total of 3,017/4,300, crossing the threshold into **level 44** - and every resolution between that completion and campaign time 2026-08-23 04:15 was made at level 43, on Stats, pools and thresholds derived from it. **A settled award is history, and this migration does not rewrite history.** Recovering that XP, if the owner wants it recovered, is a separate deliberate act with its own recomputation and belongs to a play session, not to a version edge.

**4. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, item, counter or anchor changes. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1, step 2 where an unsettled quest exists, and no other treatment.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0116 and earlier keep the 1.88 figures, which is what they were captured under.
