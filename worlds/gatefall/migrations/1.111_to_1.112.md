## What 1.112 changes

**`F-065` said route 5 was strictly dominated and named the reason it mattered: the far side has no exposure surface at all.** Profile Sections 19 and 20.4 are the whole price of Succession, and every one of their mechanisms is Earth-side - a guild that carded the victim, a body that is evidence, an authority that investigates. Section 18.13.1 already establishes that none of it reaches the Stacks. **Route 6 was unpriced exactly where its yield was largest**, and instruction cost three qualifying scenes to arrive at Novice against one kill arriving at Master.

**The flag offered three remedies and this edge takes two of them together.** The third - narrowing rung two's mastery grant to Adept - was **refused**, because it repairs a pricing gap by retracting a ruling that was made deliberately. **Nothing in 1.111 is walked back.**

### 1. The far side prices a killed champion (`270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4.4)

**The price is not law, not retaliation, and not a bounty. It is that there are six of them and no more are being made.**

- **Notice moves +12**, two full Section 4.3 thresholds at once, on the existing counter and in the existing pattern. Nothing lowers it and nothing rolls.
- **The population is closed and finite**, which the file already said from both directions: Section 4.2 authors all of them, and Section 13.1.1 closes the Earth side. **They have gone tens of thousands of years without a death among them.**
- **Each of the four positions hardens on an authored line**, tabled rather than improvised. **`Reach the next one` closes outright** - Aumry's authored fear is that teaching marks a candidate, and a candidate who kills teachers is that fear arriving with evidence.

### 2. The price is quantified in techniques, not in law

**The first killed champion buys twenty and forecloses eighty.** Section 7.6.2 takes that champion's whole set at their own mastery, and no remaining champion teaches a candidate who has killed one: Aumry closes, Sevrin's condition is failed, Orenne never would, and Ilith trades rather than teaches. **That is the entire cost, and it is paid in the currency 1.111 devalued.**

**Nothing else is charged.** No authority pursues him, nothing is confiscated, and a killed champion still drops nothing - none of them carries an equipment line, and Section 18.13.1's *reachable, not profitable* is unmoved. **A Runtime never assembles them into a response**: Section 4.1's four positions disagree about everything, they do not organise, and no party is authored.

### 3. `transmissible` is raised (Profile Section 7.1)

A slower route needs something the fast one structurally cannot have, and a taken technique records `transmissible: false` permanently and never retroactively. Two grants, both structural rather than numerical:

- **Teaching credits the teacher.** A recipient reaching the technique credits him **one qualifying scene toward his own mastery of it**, once per recipient, never twice with the same person, settled at the ordinary Section 7.4 barrier. **This is not mastery bought or taught** - it is Section 7.1's own *structured practice with real feedback* test, satisfied by the hardest version of that test there is. Route 5 becomes the only route in the profile that **compounds**.
- **Only a `transmissible` technique may be a component of a Section 7.1 test-3 combination candidate.** That test has always admitted combinations and never said what may be a part. **A skill he was shown can be taken apart; an inscription cannot** - a rune wrote a capability into him whole and he has never seen its working. Everything off a drop table, and everything Section 7.6 takes off a body, is an inscription in exactly this sense.

**Together they make route 5 the only road to a technique that does not yet exist.** Route 6 hands him twenty finished things and forecloses the twenty-first; route 5 is fewer, slower, at Novice, and is the only one of the six that can produce something nobody has ever held.

## What did not change

**Section 7.6 is untouched in every particular.** Full absorption, both rungs, the victim's own mastery on rung two, Stat Passives as a floor, class abilities without the class, the Section 7.5 ceiling clamping with the excess lost, and Stats and pools transferring nothing. **1.112 prices the route; it does not narrow it.**

**Section 4.3's notice mechanism is unchanged.** The same counter, the same pattern, the same threshold of 6 and every further 6, the same rule that nothing here makes them hostile. 1.112 adds one row to a table that already existed.

**No figure's sheet, want, fear, secret, voice or agenda moves**, and no technique set changes. The positions harden on new information; the people are the ones Section 4.2 already authored.

**Mastery is still never bought and still not taught.** The teaching credit is a qualifying scene earned by doing the hardest form of structured practice the profile authors, settled at the ordinary barrier like every other scene. No counter is granted, no level is skipped, and Section 12.5's *gold buys width, never hands* is unmoved.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.112** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. `far_side_notice` is not recomputed and gains nothing on adoption.** No champion has been killed. Initialising the counter at all remains campaign canon and belongs to play, exactly as Section 4.3 already states.

**3. No skill gains a teaching credit retroactively.** The credit is written by the barrier that settles a recipient reaching the technique, from adoption forward. Nobody has been taught anything yet.

**4. No existing skill's `transmissible` value changes.** The combination-component rule reads the flag that is already recorded; it does not re-derive one, and Section 7.1's bar on granting transmissibility retroactively is unmoved.

**5. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
