## What 1.105 changes

### 1. The S-Rank rung was closing a gap that was already closed

Section 3.2's rungs are **derived, not chosen**: each is the per-level growth carrying a Stat from one Rank's parity value to the next across the ten levels between them, where parity is `Vitality = Rank Health / 4` and `Intelligence = Rank Mana / 2`. The ladder lands within 2% at every decade, and at level 50 the Bearer stands **at S parity** - automatic 1,008 plus the weakest legal creation Stat of 8 is 1,016 against a target of 1,000.

**Above S there is no parity target, and through 1.104 the S row simply repeated A's +63.** That authored fifty-four further crossings of Rank-closing growth against a horizon already reached. At level 104 the live Bearer stood at **4.4x S parity**, with a standard uncritical Rupture at 17,245 against an S-Rank boss pool of 16,000 - he one-shot the largest thing his own Rank contains, which is verbatim the failure Section 6.7 was written to name.

**Two rungs replace it.** Levels 51-100 gain **+30**: the Rank scale climbs x2.5 (Rank Health 40 -> 4,000), so one further notch is 10,000 Health, a Vitality parity of 2,500, and `(2,500 - 1,000) / 50 = 30`. A Bearer crossing into level 100 arrives at that extended parity exactly as every earlier decade arrived at its own. **Every crossing entered at level 100 or above gains +1**, which is the ladder stopping: Section 18.13.2 already declines to author rungs *"against a horizon the world does not have"*, and five Stats at +1 equals the level-up's own +5 ability-point award, so past 100 the automatic and allocated axes carry equal weight for the first time.

### 2. The far door is re-priced against the Bearer, not the fixed-Rank table

Through 1.104 a far door cost *Mana equal to S-Rank Rank Health - 4,000*. That sized a doorway against the Section 6.1 table rather than against the man paying for it, and it was set while the Bearer's pool was still inflating under the old S rung. With the rungs re-authored, 4,000 became **73% of his pool per crossing** and a round trip stopped fitting in one pool at all.

**It is now 2,500**, anchored to the class's own entry condition: a level-100 Bearer with **nothing allocated** carries `automatic(100) = 2,508` plus a creation Stat of 8, for Intelligence 2,516 and a maximum Mana of **5,032**. A round trip is therefore **one full pool**, and the door is half of it. Opening and closing a far door is the whole of what a fresh Gate Walker can afford in a day.

**Section 5.2.1's drain is unchanged at 10% of maximum Mana per hour.** The re-pricing alone restores the expedition window the drain was tuned for: a live Bearer at 5,484 maximum Mana arrives with 2,984 against 548/hour, or roughly **5.4 hours**, against the 2.7 that 4,000 would have left him.

### 3. The ability-point ledger is re-based, not reconstructed

The live campaign's AP ledger was already internally impossible before this edge: `ability_points_earned` read **371** against **801** of summed allocation, with `unspent_points` rendering 10 on top. Since the `EVT-000597` baseline, earned had grown +48 while allocation grew +478.

**Reconstruction from the authored award sources does not close it either.** Level-ups pay +5 (103 crossings to level 104 = 515), the Title Assessment +2 per title granted (17 titles = 34), and the daily quest +3, or +4 with **Ascetic** equipped, read at claim per the `EVT-000522` ruling (32 completions = 96-128). That is a lifetime of **645-677**, working figure **659** - well under the 801 already allocated.

**So the correction re-bases rather than reconstructs**, exactly as Section 4.2 authorises and exactly as `EVT-000597` itself did: *"A campaign adopting this records each counter's current value as its baseline at the adopting Event; no historical Event gains `counter_deltas`, and no past allocation decision is revisited or second-guessed."*

**Agility, Vitality and Perception are untouched** - 27, 113 and 29, unmoved since the baseline and therefore trustworthy. The excess is entirely in the two counters that were being written, and it is removed **on their own 339:293 ratio** (owner ruling): Strength **339 -> 258**, Intelligence **293 -> 222**. Summed allocation becomes 649; with `unspent_points` 10, `ability_points_earned` becomes **659**, which is the reconstruction's own working figure.

## What did not change

**No XP value or threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, shop price, skill, title, class grant, or cap moves.** Section 3.2's rungs at E, D, C, B and A are byte-identical, as is the level-50 parity landing the whole ladder was built to make. Section 6.6's System Rank ladder is untouched - S is still reached at level 50. Section 5.2.1's drain, Section 5.3's continuation into Health, and Section 12.5's off-world potion degrade are exactly as 1.104 authored them. Every Riftwalker cost is unchanged: the in-scene step is 1, the anchored step a flat 5, micro-rift deflection 8, and the travelling step's distance bands 25/125/625/1,000.

**And every resolved settlement stands.** The Bearer killed the Mill pack for approximately 22,419 at the Strength he held under 1.104. **No resolved Event is recomputed, re-rolled, or re-narrated**, and no damage figure in `160_CAMPAIGN_CHRONICLE.md` is amended. A required migration transforms *stored state*, never the record of what happened.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms stored state and must run.** Every step below is a canon write and belongs to a play session: it is performed at the campaign's **adoption Event**, which that session mints. No identifier is allocated by the engine change that authored this record.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.105** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Re-base the six ability-point counters** at the adoption Event, each with `baseline_as_of` set to that Event and `baseline_value` equal to `current_value`:

| Path | From | To |
|---|---:|---:|
| `stats.strength_allocated` | 339 | **258** |
| `stats.agility_allocated` | 27 | **27** |
| `stats.vitality_allocated` | 113 | **113** |
| `stats.perception_allocated` | 29 | **29** |
| `stats.intelligence_allocated` | 293 | **222** |
| `progression.ability_points_earned` | 371 | **659** |

`unspent_points` renders `659 - 649 = 10`, unchanged.

**3. Recompute every base Stat** from `base_stat = creation_array[stat] + automatic(level) + allocated[stat]`, with `automatic(104) = 1,008 + (50 x 30) + (4 x 1) = 2,512`:

| Stat | Creation | Allocated | Base | Equipment | Effective |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Strength | 11 | 258 | **2,781** | +32 | **2,813** |
| Agility | 12 | 27 | **2,551** | +71 | **2,622** |
| Vitality | 8 | 113 | **2,633** | +1 | **2,634** |
| Perception | 11 | 29 | **2,552** | +5 | **2,557** |
| Intelligence | 8 | 222 | **2,742** | +0 | **2,742** |

**4. Re-derive both pools under Section 4.1's missing-amount rule**, which governs because this is not a level-up or a Status Recovery: `new_current = clamp(new_maximum - (old_maximum - old_current), 0, new_maximum)`.

- **Health:** maximum `4 x 2,634 = 10,536`; missing 25 carries; current **10,511/10,536**.
- **Mana:** maximum `2 x 2,742 = 5,484`; missing 3,928 carries; current **1,556/5,484**.

**5. Re-derive every rendered figure that reads a Stat.** These are renderings, not stored values, and each must be recomputed rather than edited toward the new numbers:

- Both equipped Quickknives: `(2,813 + 22) x 1.85 = 5,245` DMG standard before reduction, at effective Strength 2,813.
- **Rupture:** `(1,000 + 2,742) x 3.05 = 11,413`.
- The five Stat Passive rows, which render the **base** Stat: Overpower 2,781 - Agility passive 2,551 - Shrug Off 2,633 - Flux Sight 2,552 - Conduit 2,742.
- The Riftwalker anchor cap, `effective Agility / 20`: **131** slots, against 13 anchors held. It does not bind.

**6. Nothing else in the campaign is touched.** No Event gains `counter_deltas`, no resolved damage figure is amended, no roll is replayed, and no narrative is rewritten.

**7. A capture restored across this edge runs steps 1-5.** The transformation is deterministic from the values above and needs no roll and no ruling.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Every checkpoint captured under 1.104 or earlier keeps the Stats, pools and counters it was captured with; restoration applies this chain to mutable live state only.
