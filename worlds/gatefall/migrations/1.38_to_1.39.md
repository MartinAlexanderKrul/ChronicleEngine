## Compatibility treatment

**1.38 → 1.39 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration.

The removed rule is Section 7.1's Bearer duplicate-consumption reroll — "the System rerolls on the item's originating skill table until the result is unknown to him or is a genuine Rank upgrade within the ceiling." It never fired. **No consumption-time reroll has been executed in any live Gatefall campaign**, so there is no rerolled item, taught technique, mastery contribution, or counter anywhere in stored state that traces to it and no result to unwind. The one reroll appearing in campaign history is a *generation*-time duplicate filter inside a Daily Premium rotation (Pendragon, historical 2026-07-31 cycle: rune `d10=4` Stone Skin, known → `d10=5` Flash Step), taken under an earlier profile whose Premium tab still filtered against the Bearer's known skills. That draw is resolved provenance, is not reopened or re-drawn, and is untouched by this edge — Profile 1.24 onward already fixes a Premium rune's identity at rotation regardless of what the Bearer knows.

Under 1.39 the Bearer branch resolves without a die: an item teaching *at or below* what he already knows does not re-draw. He chooses **intact** — the item does not consume, keeps its identity, `instruction_binding`, and provenance, and remains transferable instruction under the same NPC branch that taught Owen Callahan Stone Skin (`EVT-000178`) — or **spend for mastery**, consuming it for one qualifying-scene contribution toward one chosen known skill's current mastery level, exactly the Mastery Rune or Mastery Book effect the old rule reached only after exhausting the table. Both outcomes were reachable under 1.38; 1.39 removes the intervening re-draw and makes the choice explicit.

Any instructional item held unconsumed at adoption keeps its stored `teaches`, `teaching_rank`, `instruction_binding`, `provenance`, and `status: unused` byte-for-byte, and is settled under 1.39 whenever it is next consumed. In the Pendragon campaign that is the banked `unbound-awakened` rune teaching Flash Step [D-Rank] (`EVT-000142`), against a Bearer who knows Flash Step at C-Rank: under 1.38 its ledger annotation said Bearer consumption "would trigger a reroll"; under 1.39 it is the intact-or-mastery choice. The item itself does not change, and its already-preferred use — teaching an eligible awakened non-Bearer at D-Rank — was never the reroll branch and is unaffected.

The 1.38 → 1.39 branch relabelling (`known below teaching_rank` / `known at or above teaching_rank`, replacing `known at a lower Rank` / `known at the same or a higher Rank`) is a wording repair with no mechanical content: both readings selected the same branch for the same item, and the new labels only name which side of the comparison governs. Record adoption as `EVT-000230`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.38-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.39 at readiness.
