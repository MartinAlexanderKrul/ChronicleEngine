## What 1.98 changes

**One thing: the travelling step's cost past the city.** Nothing else in the profile moves.

1.97 doubled past the metro - 10, 20, 40 - which against a Bearer whose maximum Mana is **2,016** put an intercontinental step at **2% of his pool** and the far side of the world at the price of five city hops. That is not a distance cost; it is a rounding error, and it made the whole class's reach free in practice.

| Destination | 1.97 | **1.98** |
|---|---:|---:|
| line of sight | 1 | 1 |
| close by | 3 | 3 |
| further | 4 | 4 |
| the city | 5 | 5 |
| another city, same country or state | 10 | **25** |
| another country | 20 | **125** |
| another continent | 40 | **625** |
| the far side of the world | - | **1,000** |

**Inside the city nothing moves.** A positioning class should position freely, and 1, 3, 4 and 5 are unchanged.

**Past the city, every further step out costs five times the last.** The ladder is `5 x 5^n` and is therefore derived rather than invented - 25, 125, 625 - with **1,000 as a deliberate terminal cap** rather than a sixth multiplication, because there is nowhere further than the far side of the world and the ladder should stop saying so.

**What this is worth against the live Bearer's 2,016 maximum Mana:** an intercontinental step is **31% of everything he has** and the last rung is **half**. An unanchored crossing of the world becomes a decision he plans a day around rather than a commute, which is the intent.

**And it is what makes an anchor worth its slot.** The anchored step remains **flat 5 at any range**, so the far side of the world costs 1,000 raw and 5 to somewhere he has held - a **two-hundredfold** difference where 1.97's ladder made it eightfold. The `Agility / 20` cap becomes the class's real strategic decision rather than a bookkeeping limit.

**On the number 625.** The owner's ruling named *635*. The first three rungs the same ruling named - 5, 25, 125 - are an exact x5 ladder, of which the fourth term is 625; 635 continues no pattern and matches no other figure in this profile. It is recorded here as read to be a slip, and the ladder is authored at **625** so the series stays derivable in the way this profile requires of every number it states. **If 635 was deliberate it is a one-character correction** and this note is the record that the question was asked rather than assumed away.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value, threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, price or title moves.** Within Section 18.8: the in-scene step's Mana 1 and once-per-exchange line, the travelling step's *anywhere he has stood* reach, blind arrival, the instance-boundary rule on the ordinary step, the anchored step's flat 5 and its two exceptions, the anchor cap formula, the growth table, deflection, anchor sense, the answering step, warded ground and the Threshold Trial are all as 1.97 left them. Flash Step is untouched.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.98** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Re-render the `RIFTS` panel's travelling-step line** to the new ladder. No counter, anchor, pool or provenance changes.

**3. Nothing is recomputed and no past step is repriced.** Mana already spent stands; the new ladder applies from adoption forward. **No scene resolved under 1.97's doubling ladder is re-adjudicated** - in practice none exists, since the campaign has not left Chicago under either version.

**4. A capture restored across this edge needs steps 1 and 2 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0120 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
