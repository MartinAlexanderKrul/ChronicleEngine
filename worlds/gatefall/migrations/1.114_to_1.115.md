## What 1.115 changes

**Gatefall names two paths for the Data Model 0.1.5 progression-candidate extension instead of one.** `011_ENGINE_DATA_MODEL.md` Section 4.3 expressly permits this — *"a world opts into and names their paths, domains, evidence tests, thresholds, and rendered meanings"* — so **no engine-layer change is involved and the reserved extension shape is untouched.**

| Path | Holds | Loaded |
|---|---|---|
| `canonical_state.system_state.progression_candidates` | `tracking`, `pending-classification`, `pending-ratification` | every readiness gate |
| `canonical_state.system_state.progression_candidates_settled` | `ratified`, `rejected` | deferred, with `progression_settlement` |

**A candidate moves at the moment it becomes terminal**, as the final step of Section 7.1's atomic ratification-or-rejection implementation. Nothing else moves it. An owner ruling reopening a `rejected` candidate returns it to the open path with its evidence intact.

**The uniqueness invariant is unchanged and now spans both paths together:** `domain` plus `key` is unique per subject, and a candidate exists in exactly one path. `tools/validate_repository.ps1` reads both sections into one list precisely so every existing check — malformed shape, duplicated evidence references, the `ratified` and `rejected` required-field rules — continues to span the whole set. Splitting the field without splitting the checks was the failure mode this guards.

## Why

`F-071` measured the Gatefall readiness surface at **39,933 tokens against a 40,000 hard failure** — sixty-seven tokens, behind four prior notes each deferring the trimming. The field-level measurement found the cause: **`progression_candidates` was 5,586 tokens, 55.8% of the protagonist's entire readiness load and 14% of the whole surface**, and **33 of its 36 entries were terminal.**

A readiness load is what a Runtime needs *before the first turn*. Three open candidates are exactly that — Section 7.1's mandatory ratification gate reads them. Thirty-three settled ones are an audit trail whose result already lives in `skills_known`, whose counters already live in `tracked_counters`, and whose reasoning already lives in a canon-bearing Event.

**Measured effect on this campaign: readiness 39,933 -> 35,162 tokens.** Headroom under the ceiling goes from 67 to 4,838, and `failure_tokens` is not raised.

**The general finding is the part worth keeping.** A readiness selector names a **field**, and a field that accumulates settled history grows without bound while its name stays the same size. Nothing anywhere distinguished a field carrying live state from one accumulating history. That distinction is what 1.115 adds; the token saving is its consequence, not its purpose.

## What did not change

**No candidate is edited, re-scored, re-keyed or deleted.** All 36 records survive byte-for-byte; 33 of them are in a different field. Every `status`, `signature`, `evidence` reference, `resolution_event`, `result_ref` and `rejection_reason` is exactly as it was.

**No skill, counter, Rank, mastery level or magnitude moves.** This edge transforms no mechanical value.

**No promoted Event is rewritten**, and **immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** A restored capture runs the procedure below.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.115** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Partition `progression_candidates` by status.** Entries with `ratified` or `rejected` move, unmodified and in their existing order, to a new sibling field `progression_candidates_settled`. Entries with any other status stay. If a capture holds no terminal candidates the new field is omitted rather than written empty.

**3. Move the selector.** In `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`, `canonical_state.system_state.progression_candidates_settled` joins `protagonist_deferred_field_groups.progression_settlement`, beside `tracked_counters` and `progression_audit_baselines`. `progression_candidates` stays in `protagonist_readiness_fields`.

**4. Verify the invariant across both paths** before continuing: `domain` plus `key` unique over the union, and every `ratified` entry still carrying `resolution_event` and `result_ref`.

**A capture restored across this edge runs steps 1-4.** A capture whose candidates are all open runs step 1 and step 3 only.
