## What 1.57 changed

**Two skills had become almost the same spell, and the player said so.** Since 1.56 folded effective Intelligence into Mana damage, **Rupture and Mana Bolt read the identical Section 6.2 formula**:

| | Rupture | Mana Bolt |
|---|---|---|
| Magnitude | `(baseline + effective Intelligence + focus) × multiplier` | *identical* |
| Multiplier band | ×2.00 → ×2.60 | ×1.00 → ×1.60 |
| Base Mana at Novice | 12 | 5 |

The bands are exactly **1.00 apart at every mastery level**. Strip the numbers and nothing distinguished them — and **this predates 1.56**, which only removed the last cosmetic difference. Through 1.55 both already read `(baseline + focus) × multiplier`; the resemblance was structural and had simply never been looked at.

**Delivery is what tells them apart, and 1.57 authors it.** Neither skill had an authored range at all. Mana Bolt was called *a ranged bolt* and Rupture *a heavy strike*, and ninety in-fiction days of play had settled the distinction by convention alone — Rupture always narrated at contact, Mana Bolt from range — with nothing in the profile requiring it.

- **Rupture reaches 5 metres, flat at every Rank.** It is the burst: larger multiplier, higher cost, and a band short enough that using it means being in the fight. Range is deliberately **not** an axis. The skill already grows on magnitude and on four categories, and a restriction that says what a skill *is* should not erode as it ascends. A dagger strike followed by Rupture remains an ordinary combo under Section 6.2, the Bearer being inside 5 metres whenever that combination occurs.
- **Mana Bolt has no limit in distance.** It is the reach: cheaper, weaker per action, bounded by perception rather than by metres. At its native E-Rank the Bearer must **see** the target.

**The ladder relaxes the sense, not the reach, and that is what keeps it a ladder.** The alternative shape considered — writing *vision or sound* into the E-Rank baseline — was rejected on arithmetic rather than taste. It would have handed the baseline two rungs that already exist:

| Rung | Authored text | Under a vision-or-sound baseline |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcing** *(D)* | "a **known position suffices** where a clear line was required" | grants nothing |
| **Homing** *(A)* | "corrects to a target that breaks line of sight, so long as he **still perceives it by any means**" | grants nothing |

That would leave **two Ranks granting nothing on a skill whose magnitude ladder 1.56 had already measured at nothing** — Mana Bolt's E→D step returns zero on both roads. Section 7.2 defines a category as a kind of thing the Rank beneath cannot do at any value of the governing Stat; a rung the baseline already covers is not one. **The Gatefall Pendragon Bearer holds *Arcing* today**, granted at `EVT-000395` as a deferral payment, so the change would also have retroactively emptied a category he had held for one Event.

**A gap in the test is recorded here rather than left for the next reader.** `tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` verifies that a thin magnitude ladder **carries** categories — it does not verify that a category grants what the Rank beneath cannot. A redundant rung passes green. That is the same shape of hole 1.56 found in the *magnitude only, by design* rows, one layer up: a stated design position that no longer holds and that nothing checks.

**Neither skill crosses a Gate boundary, a Section 9.6 seal, or a Section 17 instance wall**, on the clause Dimensional Projection's *Past the wall* already uses. Unlimited distance without that limit would let a Bearer reach into an instance he has not entered.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.56 → 1.57 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.57"`.** That is the only stored value this edge touches.

**2. Re-render the two skills' `skills_known` lines to carry their delivery range.** Rupture reads *within 5 metres*; Mana Bolt reads *no distance limit, line of sight required at native Rank*. This is a rendering change over an authored restriction that did not previously exist in writing — **no counter, magnitude, multiplier, Mana cost, mastery level, or Rank moves**, and no `magnitude_floor` or `scope_floor` is written.

**3. No damage number changes.** Section 6.2 is untouched by this edge. Rupture and Mana Bolt deal exactly what 1.56 left them dealing.

**4. Nothing retroactive.** No past exchange is re-resolved, no resolved strike recomputed, no kill re-credited. **Every prior Rupture in the record was narrated at contact and every prior Mana Bolt at range**, so no resolved action becomes illegal under the new bands; had one, Rules Section 13.2 would forbid reopening it regardless.

**5. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched, and no ascension offer opens or changes terms.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.56-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.57 at readiness.
