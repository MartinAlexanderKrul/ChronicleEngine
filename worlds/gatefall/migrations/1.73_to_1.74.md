## What 1.74 changed

**Two edges. Neither touches stored state**, and no capture taken under 1.73 becomes invalid.

### 1. Section 9.5 — an unconfirmed Gate that breaks unentered rolls its true Rank

§9.5 resolved true Rank **on first entry** and named no other trigger, so a Gate that broke without ever being entered had no Rank at all. Raised as `F-004` on 2026-07-31 and dispositioned *deferred to a play session* — a play session that never arrived, **because breaks never reached the Bearer**.

**It now rolls at the break**, on the same d100, the same table, and the same §9.6 anomaly band. **The roll is discovery, not creation**: a Gate's true Rank exists from the moment it opens, and entry was only the ordinary way of finding out.

**What the world committed to was the assessed Rank.** The board priced it, the institutions staffed the cordon, the responders sized their answer. When the true Rank comes back higher — or lands on the anomaly table — the committed response is the wrong one, and §9.4's economics guarantee this recurs, because cheap jobs sit and the Gates that sit are the ones that break.

**This supersedes the per-instance ruling at `EVT-000270`**, which settled `GB-02` at its assessed D-Rank on §9.10's *no roll* character. That ruling was explicitly for that instance, and its reasoning held while breaks were never near the Bearer — which **Profile 1.73's world tick has just stopped being true**. A break that can only resolve as its assessment is a world event with no surprise in it, which is `F-034`'s defect one section over. **`GB-02`'s historical resolution stands as ruled.**

It also closes the asymmetry `F-004` named: an *entered* unconfirmed Gate could anomaly-roll to a swing an unentered break never could, making entry the only road to the most interesting outcome. Both roads now reach the same table.

### 2. Section 18.3.1 — a grant must grant something

The class-grant analogue of §7.2's ascension-eligibility rule. **Before a class is offered, every grant it names is checked against what a Bearer at that acquisition level plausibly already holds**, and a grant duplicating existing capability is repaired or replaced — not deleted, because a class advertising three grants and delivering two is the same defect in smaller form.

It matters more than the ascension case because **an ascension can be earned again and a class cannot be re-chosen**. Both instances this generalises from — §18.5's standing edge (1.67) and §18.8's standing anchor sense (1.73) — were found by a player standing at a permanent offer, not by any gate.

**A sweep of all eight classes at 1.74 found no third instance.** Shadowbinder, Devourer, Echo Knight, Reliquary, Herald and Seam all bind their caps and pay from every filled slot, and every context-derived cost expression in the section is total.

## Procedure for restoring a 1.73 capture

1. **Set `canonical_state.system_state.profile_version` to `"1.74"`.**
2. **No other state changes.** Neither edge touches a pool, Stat, level, XP, counter, mastery level, title, quest, item, or resolved outcome. No damage preview moves. No skill line re-renders.
3. **Nothing is re-rolled retroactively.** A Gate that broke unentered under 1.73 keeps the Rank it was settled at; the new trigger governs breaks from the restore forward. A back-dated break Rank would be a fabricated one.
4. **Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
5. Run `tools/validate_repository.ps1` and `tools/validate_runtime_configuration.ps1`.
