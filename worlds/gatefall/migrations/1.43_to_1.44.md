## What 1.44 changed

**A deterministic rule became a dispatched one.** Section 9.10's deadline resolution has been discretion-free since 1.31: a tracked posting reaching its deadline settles `staffed`/`held` to cleared, `posted` to a break under Section 9.8, `withdrawn` off the board — "no roll and no Runtime discretion." That section exists specifically so postings do not evaporate when nobody is watching. It nevertheless went three deadlines past without settling in the live campaign, because nothing named the moment at which the Runtime was obliged to consult it.

1.44 adds no rule to Section 9.10 and changes none of it. It adds a **dispatch** for it, in Section 14.3's manifest:

- **`gatefall.board.deadline`**, whose `eligibility_heading` is Section 9.10 and whose settlement kind is **`world_state_settlement`** — the fourth member of the engine's closed trigger-settlement vocabulary, added by Decision 084 for state the world carries on its own behalf rather than presents to the player.
- **Candidate deltas** `clock.advanced`, `supply.advanced`, `commitment.due`, and `outreach.initiated`. The first three are the clock-boundary moments at which the board can have fallen behind; the fourth is the Bearer putting a question to the trade, which settles the board **before** the answer is given and never generates in response to it.
- **Identity** is the posting's stable local board key; **blocked statuses** are `cleared`, `broken`, and `withdrawn`, so a settled posting is never settled twice.

**It is not a System trigger.** The domain rides Section 14.3 because that manifest is the profile's single dispatch index, not because the System is involved. Section 14.4's information boundary is unmoved: the System knows Bearer state, quest state, its own stock, and a Gate's disposition once resolved, and knows nothing about a public board. A posting settling produces no notification, no panel line, and no `progression_audits` entry. A break that fires across the city remains news the Bearer hears about the way anyone does, if at all. Section 14.3's prose says so explicitly so the tier list is not read as reaching it.

**Section 9.10 is unamended.** Its standing state (three to six tracked postings for Chicago), its stored fields, its derived-never-authored deadlines, its four staffing statuses, its institutional-intervention clause, and its closing statement that the Bearer is not obligated to any of it all stand exactly as written in 1.31.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.43 → 1.44 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration.

**No posting's stored state moves at adoption.** No deadline is re-derived, no staffing status changes, no assessed Rank or confidence is revisited, and no board key is minted or retired. The tracked board's contents at adoption are exactly its contents before adoption. What 1.44 changes is *when the board is consulted*, which is a Runtime obligation rather than a stored field.

**Adoption settles nothing retroactively.** A posting whose deadline passed before adoption is not resolved by adopting this version. `gatefall.board.deadline` dispatches forward from adoption; a board carrying unsettled past deadlines carries them still, and clearing them is ordinary campaign work under Section 9.10 in a normal session, resolved at the anchor the campaign actually holds. This record does not perform it and does not license performing it in bulk from outside play. The live Gatefall board is in exactly that condition and is tracked as design flag F-002.

**No past scene is reread.** No Gate that closed, broke, or cleared in any prior scene is reinterpreted, and no posting that left the board is restored to it. Every resolved outcome stands.

**Nothing about the System changes.** No tier moves between Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3; no notification is added, removed, or reworded; the `/system` panels are untouched; and Section 14.4's boundary is unchanged. A Bearer restored under 1.44 sees precisely what he saw under 1.43.

**The engine dependency is Decision 084, not a Data Model change.** `world_state_settlement` is a declarable value admitted to the engine's closed vocabulary; no Persistent Object structure changes and no stored record moves. Data Model 0.1.5 remains unchanged.

Record adoption as `EVT-000268`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.43-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.44 at readiness.
