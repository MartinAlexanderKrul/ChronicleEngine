## What 1.29 changed

Version 1.29 remains the **additive Urgent-eligibility clarification** over frozen 1.28, completing the three-part correction 1.27 and 1.28 began. Section 8.4.2's crisis bar is unchanged and every one of its four criteria still must hold; what changes is that two of them stop being read more broadly than they were written. **First**, criterion 1 required "at least one non-hostile person" without ever saying whether the **Bearer himself** counts. Reading him in would fire an offer in every lethal fight he has; reading him out is what a careful Runtime already does, and the section now says so — danger to the Bearer's own life is combat under Sections 6 and 20.4, never a quest, and the System does not reward him for surviving. **Second**, the blanket exclusion of "routine contracted hunting" was written to stop every contract becoming an Urgent quest, but reads as covering everything that happens during one. It is now scoped to what it meant: the contracted objective, and danger to any person who entered under the **same contract** — a crew facing the Gate's population is doing the insured job it signed for. It does not reach a civilian inside a cordon, a bystander at a break site, an unrelated crew, or an uncovered survey team. **Third**, sealed-instance reachability is stated rather than left to inference: Section 17 permits bringing a party into an instant dungeon and Section 9.6's red gate seals whoever entered, so criterion 1 can hold there — but never for a Bearer who entered **alone**, which makes a solo instant dungeon permanently Urgent-ineligible by construction. **No stored field, magnitude, cost, counter, probability, owned item, completed transaction, or resolved outcome changes**, and the Urgent reward, capacity, acceptance, and lifecycle rules are untouched. Data Model 0.1.5 remains unchanged.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.28 → 1.29 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration. **No Urgent offer is issued retroactively.** No past scene is re-audited against the narrowed exclusion, no completed contract, clear, or resolved crisis is reopened, and no offer is reconstructed for a bystander who was in peril in an earlier session — Section 8.4.4 already forbids a quest completing from conduct predating acceptance, and eligibility applies **prospectively only**, from the first audit after adoption. `non_daily_quests` is structurally unchanged and its `active` and `pending_offers` lists are not seeded. Record the additive-upgrade acknowledgement in mutable live state; immutable Profile 1.28-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the applicable compatibility chain through 1.29 at readiness.
