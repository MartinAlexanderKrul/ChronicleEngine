## What 1.107 changes

**1.106 stopped at the border, and the border is not where the world stops.** Section 9.1.6's light tick covers the United States because that is the geography a **BGM National** agreement covers. That left **roughly 96% of the world's Gates unmodelled** - in a setting whose central mystery is a *worldwide* break rate on a steepening curve, whose one named catastrophe is Romanian, whose #1 hunter is Korean, whose S-Ranks are counted individually as national assets, and whose regulator-tier knowledge explicitly includes *"the true diplomatic cost of borrowing one across a border"* (`200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Sections 1, 3, 5 and 7).

New **Section 9.1.7** authors seven world regions and one weekly roll.

### 1. Why regions run a slower clock than metros

Section 9.1.6 rolls daily because a domestic escalation is a phone call the Bearer can answer inside a Section 9.3 timer. **A foreign escalation is not.** It reaches an American S-Rank as *news* almost every time, and as a *call* only through an authority with standing to make one - which is a diplomatic act, not a dispatch. So Section 9.1.7 rolls **once on the first tick of an in-fiction week**, in the same pass as Section 9.1.4, and its ordinary output is a world fact.

This is also what keeps it affordable. A daily world roll costs as much as the domestic one and mostly produces incidents nobody in the campaign can act on.

### 2. Flow switches proxy at the border, and the switch is the load-bearing decision

**Section 9.1.6 reads licensed-hunter counts** because inside one country the licensing rate is uniform - the United States and the Czech Republic both license ~38% of their awakened - which makes hunter density an honest proxy for Gate density.

**Across borders that proxy inverts the setting.** `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 5 states plainly that *"where states are strong the licensed model holds; where they are weak, Gates go uncleared and breaks are simply endured, and the map of who dies to Gates tracks the map of who was already poor."* A thin licensed population is therefore a **symptom of coverage, never of flow**. Reading it as flow would author a world in which poor countries have fewer Gates, which is the exact opposite of what the Bible says happens to them.

**So regional flow tracks population and licensing becomes the coverage term.** The rate is the United States' own, which 1.106 already derived two independent ways: **~116 Gates a day across ~340 million people is 0.34 Gates per million per day**. The world's ~1.6 million awakened at 1 in 5,000 confirms the same ~8.1 billion denominator.

### 3. The regions

| Region | Population | Gates/day at tier III | Resident S-Ranks | Days of S-Rank cover |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Asia | ~4.3B | ~1,460 | ~80 | 11 |
| Africa | ~1.5B | ~510 | ~15 | **5.9** |
| Europe | ~745M | ~253 | ~45 | 35 |
| North America | ~600M | ~204 | ~25 | 24 |
| Middle East | ~500M | ~170 | ~15 | 18 |
| South America | ~440M | ~150 | ~12 | 16 |
| Oceania | ~45M | ~15 | ~8 | **100** |
| **World** | **~8.1B** | **~2,760** | **~200** | **14** |

**Both columns close.** The resident S-Ranks sum to the Bible's **~200 worldwide**, and the Gate counts sum to **~2,760 a day**, which is 8.1 billion at the one authored rate. Nothing here is a second rate; it is the same rate applied to the rest of the map. The United States' ~116 is the majority of North America's row and remains Section 9.1.6's own - the region is **never sited** by the weekly roll.

**Days of S-Rank cover is what the table exists to produce.** It is `resident S-Ranks / (Gates per day x Section 9.2.1's 0.5% S-Rank share)`: how long a region's roster could answer its own S-Rank Gates at one apiece. It is a **ratio, not a schedule**, and it runs from **Africa's 5.9 to Oceania's 100** - the Bible's sentence about who dies to Gates, stated as arithmetic.

### 4. What the arithmetic produces, said plainly

At ~2,760 Gates a day the world opens **~13.8 S-Rank Gates every day** against 200 living S-Ranks. The overwhelming majority are therefore answered exactly as Section 9.3 already says they are - *"the nearest adequate force plus military containment"*, massed licensed hunters under a cordon, not a famous name. **And in the thin regions many are not answered at all**, which is Section 5's *"quiet scandal the European Gate Council debates and does nothing about"* expressed as a rate rather than a mood.

**This does not demote the Jiu Valley, and the section says so.** The Exclusion is the shared wound of the *profession* because it happened in a well-covered region, to a counted population, with multiple S-Ranks dying in a containment the whole trade watched fail. That losses elsewhere go unnamed is the Bible's point about whose deaths get counted, not a contradiction of it.

### 5. The border rule

**No regulator's call crosses a border, and the draft never does.** Section 9.11's emergency mobilization is the BGM compelling a hunter *it licensed*; `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 3 is explicit that an S-Rank *"cannot be compelled by any institution"*, and no authority on Earth can compel a foreign one. A `96-00` result therefore arrives as an **ask**, through exactly four authored doors: the **IGR**, the **European Gate Council**, **BGM National** lending him out, or **a state buying capacity** on the Gulf pattern.

Only the BGM National door touches his standing, on Section 9.11's standing-cost tier. Reach rolls Section 9.11 with **+20** for a foreign ask against a Section 18.8 anchor's usual **-20**, and **the emergency-mobilization tier is unavailable at every result** - a foreign Gate is never a licensing offense to decline.

## What did not change

**Section 9.1.6 is untouched.** Its bands, its twenty-six-metro roster, its ten BGM regions, its 0.30-a-day stream, its siting table and its promotion rule are byte-identical; 1.107 adds one handoff sentence at its foot and nothing else.

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value or threshold, damage formula, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, shop price, skill, title, class grant, cap, or Mana cost moves.** Sections 9.2.1 and 9.2.2 are byte-identical, and Section 9.1.7 **feeds the ramp nothing** on Section 9.1.6's own reasoning: a foreign break advancing Chicago's tier is the roster-length defect at world scale.

**It authors no city, rolls no Gates, adds no supply, and creates no System quest.** Every Section 9.1.6 fence applies unchanged, and a foreign metro reaches a full tick only through the same promotion rule a domestic one does.

**It is not a travel system.** Reaching a foreign Gate is fiction - a flight, an anchor, someone else's aircraft - and the +20 is the whole of what the profile says about the difficulty of getting there.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.107** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. Past `world_ticks` entries are left exactly as captured.** The `world` line is added to the Section 9.1.1 schema **prospectively**, on the same reasoning 1.106 recorded for `wider_map`: a tick that ran before this edge did not roll Section 9.1.7, and writing one in afterwards authors a roll that never happened.

**3. No region and no foreign metro is promoted by this edge.** Chicago and Prague remain the only full ticks.

**4. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
