## What 1.58 changed

**Section 7.1 route 3 has two endings, and only one of them was ever reachable without stopping play.** A candidate that reaches three qualifying scenes either ratifies automatically — if this profile already authors its complete name, Rank, cost, effect, limits, and mastery progression — or it enters the **mandatory adjudication gate**, where the Runtime may answer rules questions and save, but may not roll, narrate an NPC, advance in-world time, or begin the next scene until the owner authors and names the skill. Through 1.57 exactly one candidate had a pre-authored result: `dimensional_weapon_control`, authored at 1.51 and completed at 1.55. It paid off at `EVT-000436`, where the candidate turned out to have stood at 3/3 since 2026-08-08 and **Dimensional Projection ratified in the same settlement with no queue and no ruling owed.**

**`applied_analytical_reconstruction` is the second candidate to earn one, and it earns it before it forms rather than after.** The Bearer has twice resolved a problem by applying his trained mathematics-and-physics background as the **declared method, explicitly instead of a System skill**, taking the +1 step for a directly relevant established capability each time: plotting four Loomis Street cameras' mounting points and sweep cones by hand, which found a four-foot blind spot along the north wall and reframed the site from *professional security* to *staffed to cover its own blind spot*; and a structural read of the Cicero Scar's unreclaimed core blocks, which established that the sublevel is unreachable without an inner-cordon survey posting. The candidate stands at **2/3**. Under 1.57 a third qualifying scene would have stopped the session at the adjudication gate.

**The ruling is the one 1.55 already made for Dimensional Projection: author the result before the skill can exist rather than after.** 1.55's reasoning was that the ladder is what makes a skill safe to form — Twin Fang was made ascension-eligible with no Rank axis and its first ascension would have destroyed 37.5% of its damage before anyone checked. The same argument applies a step earlier, to the effect itself. A technique authored under time pressure at a gate that has already halted play is authored badly.

**First Principles [E-Rank]**, passive, 0 Mana: **+1 modifier step on any action that reconstructs unobserved structure, coverage, or reach from observed geometry and physical law** — sightlines and sensor cones, load paths and structural extent, trajectory, span, and reachability.

**It derives; it does not perceive.** The boundary is what keeps it distinct from two skills the Bearer already holds. It never reveals what is *present* — that is Keen Sense, which costs Mana and reads a subject — and it establishes **no Pattern**, so it grants no standing exploit and occupies no Exploit Pattern slot. It answers *what must be true of this space, given what I can measure*. Its modifier does not stack with another derived from the same measurements. **A derivation resting on wrong or insufficient measurements is wrong**, and the die resolves that as it resolves anything else: the skill improves the reasoning, never the evidence.

**Its mastery axis is authored rather than left to the closed forms, and that is the whole lesson of Broken Rhythm applied on time.** First Principles is a permanent passive already at the Section 4.3 step cap — no multiplier, no reduction fraction, no duration to lengthen, and Mana 0, so Section 7.4's −10%-per-level rule is inert. **Every closed-form mastery axis is inapplicable at once**, which is precisely the state Broken Rhythm occupied while climbing to a live mastery level and granting nothing at any of them, twelve qualifying scenes for no change of any kind (`F-014`). Nothing detected it, because a closed-form rule with no coverage list cannot report a skill it does not cover. Mastery here grants **1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 beneficiaries** from Novice through Master: at Novice the Bearer alone, each level extending the step to one further person he has walked through the derivation with, who acts on the finding as though he had reached it himself. It carries a `scope_floor` under Section 7.2's ratchet.

**Its Rank ladder joins the Section 7.3 scope-axis table** — the table whose own stated criterion is a skill that gains a further affected subject and an authored category per Rank, which is exactly this one. Rungs: **Inference from absence** (D), the derivation running on what is missing rather than on positive measurements; **Mechanism** (C), extending from where a built system's parts sit to how it behaves under a given input; **From secondhand measure** (B), the reconstruction holding on measurements he did not personally take; **Predictive** (A), deriving the structure's future state before that state exists. No rung adds a modifier step, none changes a Mana cost, and each is a kind of thing the Rank beneath cannot do at any value of the governing Stat.

**It also joins the Section 7.4 per-skill mastery table**, because that section says in its own words that a mastery-tracked skill with no row is not finished.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.57 → 1.58.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.58"`.** That is the only stored value this edge touches.

**2. Nothing is granted and no skill is created.** `applied_analytical_reconstruction` remains a `tracking` candidate at its recorded evidence count. **Ratification still requires a third qualifying scene under Section 7.1's unchanged threshold of three**; this edge changes only what happens when one lands — automatic ratification in the settling Event instead of the mandatory adjudication queue. A Bearer holding this candidate gains nothing at adoption and loses nothing.

**3. No `skills_known` entry is written, and no counter is initialized.** First Principles does not exist on any ledger until a ratification Event creates it. At that point Section 7.1's atomic procedure governs: add the skill at Novice, initialize `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `mastery_progress`, `mastery_level`, `rank_ascensions`, and `scope_floor` at their zero points with that Event as baseline, and set the candidate to `ratified` with its `resolution_event` and `result_ref`.

**4. No existing skill changes.** Keen Sense and Exploit Pattern are untouched in effect, Rank, mastery, scope, and Mana cost; the distinctness boundary above describes what First Principles is *not*, and restricts neither of them. No other skill's ladder, category, magnitude, or axis moves.

**5. Nothing retroactive.** `EVT-000372` and `EVT-000398` remain exactly as promoted, resolved on an uncertain action with a directly relevant established capability and no skill involved. Neither becomes a First Principles use, and no past roll is recomputed. Pre-ratification evidence establishes acquisition only and never backfills use or mastery counters (Section 7.1).

**6. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched, and no ascension offer opens or changes terms.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.57-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.58 at readiness.
