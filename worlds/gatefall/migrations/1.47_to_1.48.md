## What 1.48 changed

**Four Stat Passives had three rungs and stopped.** When the Stat-50 skills were folded into their partners, Section 4.4 authored **Overpower**, **Pre-empt**, **Shrug Off**, and **Multitask** at E, D, and C, and left B, A, and S empty against a threshold table that runs 30 / 36 / 44 / 54 / 66 / 80. Flux Sight alone was authored to S. The consequence was structural rather than cosmetic: a Bearer whose base Stat crossed 54 banked the value against a rung that did not exist, Section 4.4's **authoring clamp** held the Rank down, and the skill's row announced the hold at every checkpoint — working exactly as designed, and reporting a gap the profile had not closed.

**1.48 authors all twelve.** Section 4.4 gains a second rung table covering B, A, and S for the four, and the authoring clamp no longer binds any Stat Passive. It remains in force for any Stat Passive a later version adds, together with its held-rung render.

- **Overpower** — B **Redirection** (a held foe becomes a weapon; shift a creature off its own footing), A **Anchoring** (immovable against force up to the skill's Rank; breaks structures one Rank above it), S **Leverage without purchase** (force applies with no footing, brace, or grip).
- **Pre-empt** — B **First blade** (resolves before any opponent up to the skill's Rank in an exchange he opens), A **Interposition** (once per exchange, intercept an attack aimed at another creature in reach and take its resolution), S **No blind side** (surprise cannot exist against him; an unaware creature grants a free resolution).
- **Shrug Off** — B **Total suppression** (every wound's penalty suppressed, not one; hazards up to the skill's Rank cause no onset), A **Threshold immunity** (nothing more than one Rank below the skill can cross the injury threshold at any damage), S **Continuance** (no wound, condition, exhaustion, or hazard imposes a modifier step at any severity).
- **Multitask** — capacity **5 / 6 / 7** at B / A / S, continuing the ladder it already was. Section 8.4's capacity sentence follows it.

**Every rung is a category, and that is a constraint rather than a flourish.** Section 7.2's Rank Dominance Law requires a Rank to do something the Rank beneath cannot do at all, and its **Stat Passive exemption** removes the count axis: a Stat Passive has no mastery track, so each Rank grants its authored category and nothing else. No rung above adds a modifier step, a Mana cost, a damage multiplier, or a scope count, and none carries a `scope_floor`.

**Death is untouched.** Section 20.4 is not softened at any rung. Shrug Off at S removes every penalty harm imposes; it does not make a killing blow survivable, and no Stat Passive in this profile spares the Bearer a resolved lethal result.

**The System Rank ceiling is untouched and is now the only limit.** Section 7.5's ceiling of System Rank + 1 continues to clamp a Stat Passive's Rank, and Stat value above it is still held intact and still applies the instant System Rank rises.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.47 → 1.48 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption recomputes exactly one thing — the derived Rank of four Stat Passives — and writes its consequences.

**1. Re-derive the four Stat Passives, once, at adoption.** A Stat Passive's Rank is derived state, never stored (Section 4.4): read the highest threshold met by the governing **base** Stat, then clamp by System Rank + 1. The authoring clamp is gone for these four, so any Bearer standing above a previously unauthored threshold **takes that Rank at adoption**, exactly as Section 4.4 promises — "Stat value beyond either limit is held intact and applies the instant the binding limit lifts … the authoring limit when Section 20.3 authors the rung." This is a deferral being paid, not a new grant.

**2. Write the consequences the new Rank carries.** Only Multitask's rung carries a stored number: a Bearer reaching B, A, or S re-derives `system_state.non_daily_quests.multitask_bonus` and `capacity_total` to the new capacity. **No accepted or attached quest is disturbed** — capacity rises, so nothing is evicted, and no quest is created to fill the new slot. Every other rung is a capability that renders in the skill's row and stores nothing.

**3. Re-render the four rows.** Each skill's `skills_known` entry takes its new Rank, its new rung's effect text, and — where the System Rank ceiling now holds a further rung — the ceiling's held state in place of the authoring clamp's `[<Rank>] held: rung unauthored`. The `successful_uses` lifetime count on each is **untouched**; a Rank change is not a use.

**4. Nothing retroactive.** No past exchange is re-resolved, no injury reclassified, no surprise round replayed, and no quest capacity applied backward to a moment a slot was declined. The new rungs apply **from adoption forward**. Rules Section 13.2 forbids reopening what a settlement has spent, and every prior scene resolved correctly under the Rank the Bearer actually held at the time.

**5. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP total, level, System Rank, mastery counter, use count, title, item, gold, cash, deadline, or streak is touched. No skill outside the four changes in any respect, Flux Sight included — it was authored to S already and its Rank is unchanged by this adoption.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.47-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.48 at readiness.
