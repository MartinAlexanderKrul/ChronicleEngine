## What 1.103 changes

**Found at the mouth of the trial it governs, by a Runtime that stopped instead of narrating past it.** The Bearer stood at the entrance to the Section 18.13 Gate Walker trial, six-phase form, terrain rolled Flooded Mine, Combat Tier S. Phase 1 - the folded-in evolution examination that 1.102 had just authored - **could not be opened as written.** Two of the four repairs below are 1.102's own defects rather than inherited ones, and they are recorded as such.

### 1. The deed was authored in retired vocabulary

1.102 wrote the Threshold Trial's condition as *"answer a disturbance at a set anchor from another ledgered container."* **`ledgered container` is Profile 1.96 pricing vocabulary.** 1.97 replaced the container lookup with the soft distance bands, and 1.98 set their multipliers; the phrase's only other surviving occurrence in the whole profile is the 1.97 line that exists to forbid it - *"not metres, and not a table of containers to look up."* A live rule was keyed to a dead lookup.

**This is the `F-049` shape, committed one change after `F-049` was cited in the same session's roadmap.** `F-049` is the flag about a skill line that had quietly borrowed a retired ability's numbers, and its own disposition records that **nothing checks a rendered line against its authored ladder.** That gap is what let a retired phrase into a new rule, and it remains open: the check `F-049` asks for would have caught this at authoring time.

**Repaired** to **another city or further**, which names a scale the 1.98 bands actually author. The deed itself is unchanged - only its unit of distance is now stated in live terms.

### 2. The deed was unperformable where 1.102 asked for it

Section 18.8 bars the anchored step in two instances - the Section 8.3 penalty zone and the Section 18.2 class trial - and through 1.102 stated the bar as: *"An anchor set inside either is unreachable from outside and **unusable from within** until the instance releases him."*

That second clause is absolute, and it made 1.102's Phase 1 self-defeating: **an examination testing the anchor apparatus forbade the anchor apparatus.** Performing the deed against a real external anchor would have required breaching the no-retreat seal the trial exists to enforce; performing it internally was barred by the same sentence.

**The exception is restated as scoped to crossing, which is all its own reasoning ever claimed.** Every argument the paragraph makes is about leaving - *the forge does not have a back door*, *a penalty zone he could anchor out of would stop being a penalty*, *a trial he could leave would measure nothing*. None of it reaches a step that begins and ends inside the seal. From 1.103:

- **No anchored step crosses the wall in either direction.** Unchanged, and this is the whole of the original intent.
- **Within a Section 18.2 trial, an anchored step wholly inside the seal is legal** - to a **trial-local anchor**, taken from inside the same instance.
- A trial-local anchor **exists only inside the instance, costs no slot** against `effective Agility / 20`, and is released with it.
- **The penalty zone admits no such step at all.** It examines nothing, Section 8.3's entire content is that he is in it, and there is nothing there for an internal step to test. The asymmetry is deliberate and is stated rather than left to inference.

### 3. The anchor sense's authoring constraint gets a matching carve-out

Section 18.8's *"The sense reports; it never authors"* forbids surfacing a disturbance at an anchor except where it would have occurred had no anchor been set there. Inside a trial that forbids the phase outright, since the disturbance exists **because** the examination needs one.

**It now does not apply inside a Section 18.2 trial**, and the reason is what a trial is: the System builds the instance and everything standing in it, so a manufactured disturbance at a trial-local anchor **is** the examination rather than a breach. The constraint continues to govern **the world**, where authoring an event because a place is anchored would let the sense create what it claims to detect - which is the defect it was written for and is untouched.

### 4. The distinction underneath all three, now stated

Section 18.3.4 said the additional phase *"is his own evolution's examination"* and left the rest implicit. It was read at the table as **the unlock deed replayed**, which is a fair reading and the wrong one - and the misreading is what surfaced defects 1 and 2 at once.

**A folded-in phase is an examination, not the unlock deed replayed.** The **condition** opens the standalone evolution trial; the **phase** is what stands inside it. A Bearer on the six-phase route **has not satisfied his condition and is never asked to** - the trial carries the evolution precisely because the condition was not met - so the phase tests the class's **axis** under Section 18.2.3 and never requires the deed. **Where testing that axis needs apparatus the seal would ordinarily suppress, the trial builds its own**; trial-local anchors are the authored case and the principle is stated generally, for any later class whose axis is bound by a rule the trial imposes.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value, XP threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, price, skill, title, class grant, cap, or Mana cost moves.** The anchored step still costs a flat 5 Mana at any distance, still crosses ordinary instance walls, and still does not cross a penalty zone's or a class trial's. Section 18.3.4's six-phase ruling, its two condition shapes, and the grant of both lines on passing are untouched. Every other class's hook, and all eight classes' mechanics, are byte-identical to 1.102.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.103** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. No stored state changes.** No anchor, slot, counter, or trial record is read or written. A trial-local anchor is instance-scoped by construction and never enters `riftwalker.anchors` or counts against the cap, so no campaign holding anchors is affected in any way.

**3. The Threshold Trial's lock state is re-tested, and for the live campaign the answer does not move.** The deed's distance clause changed vocabulary rather than scale: `another ledgered container` under 1.102 and `another city or further` under 1.103 both exclude standing at the anchor and both require real separation. **The live record still carries no resolved Event in which he answered an anchor disturbance from that distance in the exchange it reached him**, so the Threshold Trial remains **locked**, exactly as 1.102's step 3 recorded. Nothing is granted, revoked, or re-opened by this edge.

**4. A trial already in progress adopts this edge immediately.** These are repairs to rules that govern the trial's own construction, and a Bearer standing at Phase 1 under 1.102 could not open it. There is no partial state to reconcile - no phase had resolved - so the trial opens under 1.103 with no re-adjudication of anything.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1 only.** There is no state to recompute.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Every checkpoint captured under 1.102 or earlier keeps what it was captured under.
