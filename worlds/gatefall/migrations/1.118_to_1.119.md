## What 1.119 changes

**New Section 13.7, Guild Institutions — Securing Gates and Guild Revenue.** Sections 9.10 and 13.2 priced the trade's mechanics — postings, splits, harvest shares — in detail, but nothing in the profile ever priced the guild sitting on top of them, despite Ironline, Horizon, Karlov, the Coalition, and now Pendragon all being named across the existing text. Four subsections close that gap, generically, for any guild:

- **13.7.1** distinguishes the three posting channels Section 9.10 already listed without separating — the BGM ranking board (regulator-run, no favorites, first-to-qualify), the Coalition's co-op desk (a freelance labor broker, not a regulator and not a guild), and guild dispatch (a guild's own standing permits and contracts, routed internally, never contested) — and states plainly that winning a BGM-board posting is an award, never a purchase: no guild ever pays BGM to be selected. A bid-modifier table governs which guild wins a contested posting when the fiction needs to know.
- **13.7.2** extends Section 13.2's split with a guild house cut (15% of the post-leader pool) where a guild fields the crew, leaving Section 13.2 itself untouched for independent and mixed crews.
- **13.7.3** extends Section 12.3's single guild-salaried B-Rank data point ($240,000/yr) into a full E-through-A salary table, derived against freelance annual income computed properly per-person (not per-pool) for the first time, priced as a real premium (+9% to +25%) rather than a floor, and states explicitly that a guild's own support crew are salaried too — the fix for the harvest-split gap the freelance-only Section 13.2 model left open. A worked example prices guild cost, revenue, and profit margin per Gate at B- and C-Rank.
- **13.7.4** prices a guild-owned appraisal desk's resale margin against the existing Section 12.1/12.8 crystal/core market, with no invented fixed percentage.

**Section 12.3's existing "Guild-salaried B-Rank ≈ $240,000/year" row is marked superseded**, pointing to 13.7.3 rather than left standing as a second, contradictory figure. The $240,000 survives only as 13.7.3's independently-derived freelance-average comparison point — a coincidence of arithmetic, not a retained citation.

## What did not change

**No Bearer-facing mechanic changes.** Section 13.7 governs trade economics the Bearer transacts inside — as a client of it before, and as a guild founder inside it now — never a rule about his own progression, Stats, or System state.

**Section 13.2's independent and mixed-crew terms are untouched.** The guild house cut (13.7.2) and salaried-support treatment (13.7.3) apply only where a guild is actually the leader or the employer; an independent party or a mixed guild/freelance crew still resolves by the unmodified Section 13.2 text.

**No XP, level, gold, cash, or Stat moves.** This is new trade-economics content, not a change to any canonical Bearer state field.

**Nothing about Sections 9.10's staffing-status mechanic, 9.1 step 4's take rates, or 9.11's directed-assignment channel changes.** 13.7.1's bid-modifier table sits beside the existing anonymous d100 roll and is read only when the fiction needs to know which specific guild wins, exactly as Section 9.11 sits beside Section 9.10 without altering it.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.119** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. **This step is owed to the campaign's next play session, not performed by this migration record** — adopting a profile into a live campaign is a canon-bearing Event under save discipline (`160_CAMPAIGN_CHRONICLE.md`, `170_CHANGELOG.md`, `system/ID_REGISTRY.md`), and this record only lands the world side.

**2. Nothing in stored canonical state requires recomputation.** Section 13.7 is net-new content with no prior mechanic to reconcile against; a capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.

**3. A capture that predates 1.119 and references a guild's pre-Pendragon economics (Ironline, Horizon, Karlov, the Coalition) reads unaffected** — nothing about those institutions' prior appearances in play is retroactively changed; 13.7 only supplies numbers where none existed before.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
