## What 1.111 changes

**Section 7.6 has taken exactly one technique off a body since 1.90** - the victim's signature, at their Rank clamped to the ceiling, at Novice - and called itself *"the third acquisition route in this profile."* It was never the third. Section 7.1 opens with a **closed list**: *"A skill enters the Bearer's ledger by one of five routes, and only these."* **Succession was not on it.** The two sections have contradicted each other for twenty-one versions, and the stale ordinal is the fingerprint of when it stopped being true.

### 1. Succession is route 6, and it absorbs fully

Section 7.1's list now names six routes and Section 7.6 authors the sixth in full. **What widened is how much comes across; nothing widened how high.**

| Axis | 1.110 | 1.111 |
|---|---|---|
| Techniques taken | the signature, **one** | **every technique the body held** |
| Arriving Rank | victim's Rank, clamped to `System Rank + 1` | **unchanged** - clamped, excess lost |
| Mastery | Novice always | Novice on rung one; **the victim's own level** on rung two |
| Stat Passives | never | **as a Rank floor** on rung two |
| Class abilities | never | **the ability, never the class** on rung two |
| Stats and pools | nothing | **nothing** |
| Victim class | awakened human | awakened human **or** a Section 13.1.1 System-bearer |

### 2. The two rungs are forced by Section 13.1, not chosen for tidiness

**An ordinary awakened has almost nothing underneath their Rank to take.** Section 13.1 gives them no Stat sheet, no level and no mastery track; Section 13.6 gives them one innate signature *"fixed for life"* plus whatever runes and books added, *"rare enough that a hunter with two tricks is notable and a hunter with three is a story."* Three of the four rulings above therefore have **no referent on an Earth body**: there is no mastery to take, no Stat Passive to take, and no class to take. Rung one's whole widening is **all of their techniques rather than one of them**, which against a typical hunter is one to three entries.

**A Section 13.1.1 bearer is where the ruling has content**, because that section already grants them five Stats, skills with mastery levels, Stat Passives at their thresholds, and a class where one was taken. All four are in scope on rung two, and the population is closed: the failed champions of `270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4 and nobody else.

### 3. Three clauses that had to be authored rather than assumed

**A bearer carries no Rank.** Section 13.1.1 states it plainly - *"No Rank, no Rank Health, no Rank Mana"* - and Section 7.6's arriving-Rank clause had always read the victim's. On rung two a skill enters at **the Rank authored for it on that champion**, and a Runtime reaching an unauthored technique **stops and asks the owner** under `270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 2's standing discipline. Nothing is invented at the body.

**A Stat Passive cannot arrive as a value.** Section 4.4 makes its Rank *"canonical derived state, never a separately stored value"*, read from the holder's own base Stat. Succession writes a **succession floor** instead: the passive renders at the higher of his derived Rank and the floor, the floor clamped by the ceiling, and his own derived Rank takes over silently when it passes. Where the victim's derived Rank is at or below his, **nothing is written**. The Bearer already holds all five Stat Passives and Section 4.4's top threshold is base Stat 80, so this clause is inert against every figure currently authored - it exists so the case is answered rather than improvised.

**A class ability cannot arrive with its class.** Section 18 binds a class to its trial and nothing here reaches a trial, so the named ability arrives and the standing does not: no trial credit, no evolution, no access to the rest of the kit, no change to his own class line. Section 13.6's *"grants its heal but not the full innate-mender package"* is the same rule one layer up.

### 4. What this costs, stated rather than discovered

**Mastery now arrives without qualifying scenes for the first time in this profile.** Section 7.1 and Section 12.5 both hold that mastery is never bought, and 1.109's taught route restated it word for word - *gold buys width, never hands* - and was built around preserving it. Rung two is the exception, ruled deliberately.

**The consequence reaches 1.109 and the file it was written for.** Instruction from a champion is three qualifying scenes and arrives at Novice; killing that same champion is instant and arrives at Master. **Route 5 is therefore the worse trade wherever both are available**, and `270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md` Section 4.1's *Reach the next one* position - the whole reason Aumry was authored - is inverted on the arithmetic. Ilith's *"worth more as a teacher than as a fight"* is no longer true of the numbers, and that line is annotated in place rather than deleted, because it is still true of her.

**This edge does not resolve that.** It is raised as `F-065` in `engine/004_DESIGN_FLAGS.md`, and a Runtime plays the figures as authored until it is ruled.

### 5. The far side's people, authored because route 6 reads them

**Rung two enters a taken skill at the Rank authored on that champion, and there were no authored techniques to read.** Section 4.2's five Standing figures carried a sheet, a want, a fear, a secret, a voice, an agenda - and between them roughly four named capabilities, all of them in prose. Succession against any of them would have hit the stop-and-ask on the first technique, every time, which is a correct stop and a useless one.

**Each of the five now carries twenty named techniques**, with a Rank, a Mana cost and a stated effect on every row. None is shared with the Bearer and none is shared between champions: they came up on different worlds, under different Systems, against different exams, and **the sets are the characterisation**. Sevrin's twenty are almost entirely denial - ten of them do nothing but stop something. Aumry's twenty contain nothing that hurts anybody. Orenne has one offensive line and nineteen techniques for making sure it lands. Ilith has no weapon at all, and **Touch and Go** is her whole agenda expressed as fifteen Mana. Tolo's are twenty ways of finding something out.

**Each set carries exactly one `--` row: a once-ever effect that is an owner ruling and is deliberately not pre-authored.** That is Section 2's discipline applied inside a technique list rather than around it - the secret each figure is carrying has a mechanical slot, and no Runtime fills it.

### 6. The sheets are derived now, and the derivation is the rule

Every figure's base-Stat sum sits **between 85% and 90% of the Bearer's own base-Stat sum**, fixed at **first contact** and never re-derived. The distribution is theirs: **a sum, never a per-Stat scale.** A flat per-Stat 85-90% produces five people who are one person at five sizes, and it deletes the two characterisations this file spends the most words on.

| Figure | Sum | % of Bearer | Health | What they exceed him at |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Sevrin | 12,000 | 88.0% | 9,200 | nothing - she is denial, not numbers |
| Ilith | 11,830 | 86.8% | 5,800 | **Agility 3,900** against 2,581 |
| Aumry | 11,710 | 85.9% | 9,600 | nothing, and that is the point |
| Orenne | 12,050 | 88.4% | 4,600 | **Strength 3,400** against 2,856 |
| Tolo | 12,100 | 88.7% | 8,000 | **Perception 3,900** against 2,582 |

**Two bounds hold.** `maximum_health = 4 x Vitality` and `maximum_mana = 2 x Intelligence` on Section 6.1's Bearer formula, now asserted per figure rather than by two pinned literals; and no figure's pool exceeds the Bearer's, which the gate reads from the live character sheet. **The literal it used to carry read 10,620 against a Bearer standing at 10,948** - the bound was one the campaign had already passed, and a correct rescale would have turned the check red rather than the inflation it was built to catch.

## What did not change

**The Section 7.5 ceiling is byte-identical and still binds acquisition and ascension alike.** `System Rank + 1`, capped at S. A technique above it transcribes at the ceiling and the excess is lost - not banked, not deferred, not raised. Every ladder, threshold and dominance relation reading that ceiling is untouched.

**Stats, ability points and pools transfer nothing.** Section 4.2's derivation - `base_stat - creation_array[stat] - allocated[stat]` equal across all five Stats and equal to `automatic(level)` - is the gate a body-sourced Stat would break, and no Stat is body-sourced. Maximum Health and Mana follow from Vitality and Intelligence and therefore do not move.

**The trigger is unchanged.** The Bearer's own damage, a real death under Section 6.5, never a monster, never an unawakened person, and never a target put to 0 by pulled damage under Section 6.2.1. Only the victim *class* widened, to admit a Section 13.1.1 bearer.

**A taken technique is never `transmissible`.** Route 5 remains the only route that grants it, and Section 7.1's rule that it is never granted retroactively holds without exception. A teacher performs a method; a body performs nothing.

**Where he already holds the technique, nothing is taken** - unchanged, and the below-Rank case still settles on Section 7.5's ordinary rune-ascension comparison at the rune route's mastery, never the victim's.

**No figure's want, fear, secret, voice, agenda or position moves.** Every characterisation in Section 4.2 is the one it had; the Stats under them changed and the people did not. Sevrin is still smaller than him, Ilith still will not fight him, Aumry still cannot hurt anyone, Orenne would still rather talk, and Tolo still has no position.

**Corrin Hale and the Spent are untouched and deliberately excluded.** Corrin Hale is `Continued` and beyond the ladder - a Runtime never resolves an engagement with it as combat, so a technique list would be a scale for something the file says has none. The Spent are a population rather than people, authored at need, and none of them teaches because teaching is effort.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.111** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. No skill already held is re-derived.** Every entry in `skills_known` keeps its Rank, mastery level, counters and acquisition route exactly as recorded. Nothing acquired under 1.110 or earlier is re-examined against the wider rule.

**3. No past kill is re-settled.** A Succession that took one technique under the narrower rule took the right thing under the law in force, and this profile does not reopen a resolved Event to hand over the rest. The rule governs forward.

**4. No entry gains `acquisition_route`, `transmissible`, `taken_from` or `succession_event` retroactively.** These are written by the Events that create entries from adoption forward. A skill with no recorded route is not defective; the route that created it is what the chronicle already says it was.

**5. No `succession_floor` exists on adoption**, on any Stat Passive. All five render from the Bearer's own base Stat exactly as they did under 1.110.

**6. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
