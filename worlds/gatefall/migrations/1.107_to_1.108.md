## What 1.108 changes

**1.106 and 1.107 varied how many Gates a place opens and left the Rank distribution flat everywhere.** A minor metro holding under 150 licensed hunters rolled the same 0.5% S-Rank share as Chicago, and a continent with the thinnest agency reach on Earth rolled it too. **Owner ruling: that is backwards. Fewer hunters means less mana, which means fewer and lower Gates.**

### 1. The rule, and where it was already written

**A Gate is a puncture, and a puncture is made by pressure.** Section 9.3 already reads a Gate's break timer off *"its mana pressure - the same reading that Ranks it"*, and `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 2 puts hunters, Gates and loot on **one measured scale**, so that *"a monster, a hunter, and a sword can be spoken of in the same breath."* Those are not three coincidences on a shared vocabulary; they are one quantity read three ways.

**So where the ambient mana is thin the world cannot open high Gates**, and no run of bad luck produces one. That is why a region's S-Rank Gates and its S-Rank hunters are found in the same places rather than being a cruel accident of distribution.

**Hunter population is the visible measure**, and the Bible reports it twice already: guild strength *"scales with home-country size and Gate density"*, small nations *"import clearance capacity"*, and East Asia is at once the densest Gate region on Earth and the region that produced the early S-Ranks. The measure is honest at the top in particular, because a state may license whom it likes but **cannot license anyone into S-Rank** - the ~200 are counted individually, by name.

**The one place the measure lies is the Gulf**, which imports S-Ranks *"like any other luxury"*. A bought roster says what a state can pay, not what its ground carries, which is why the Middle East reads **ordinary** while carrying a resident count that would otherwise argue for deep.

### 2. Three depths at region scale

| Mana depth | Flow | Rank ceiling |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep** | full 0.34 per million per day | Full Section 9.2.1 distribution, S included |
| **Ordinary** | x0.7 | S at half share, the freed 0.25% into A |
| **Shallow** | x0.45 | **No S-Rank Gate at all**; S into A, that A halved, the freed share into B |

**Depth scales the count as well as the ceiling**, because the ruling is that thin ground opens fewer punctures *and* lower ones.

| Region | Depth | Gates/day 1.107 -> 1.108 | S-Rank Gates/day 1.107 -> 1.108 | Resident S-Ranks | Days of cover |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Asia | deep | 1,460 -> **1,460** | 7.3 -> **7.3** | 95 | **13** |
| Africa | shallow | 510 -> **230** | 2.55 -> **none** | 8 | - |

**The resident-S-Rank column is re-allocated within the Bible's ~200**, which 1.107 had distributed on population alone. It now follows depth: Asia 95, Europe 42, North America 26 (holding the Bible's fixed 11 US), South America 14, Middle East 12, Africa 8, Oceania 3. **The Bible fixes only the total, the US count and the Czech count**, and all three are preserved.

**A shallow region reads `-` for cover because it has no S-Rank traffic to cover**, which is a different statement from having no cover.

### 3. Why this is the repair and not a softening

Read without the ceiling, **Africa carried 2.55 S-Rank Gates a day against a roster of fifteen**: under six days of cover, a continent losing a valley most months, and a world that could not contain exactly one Exclusion. **Read with it, thin ground has no S-Rank Gates at all**, and what kills people there is what the Bible actually describes - *"under-ranked Gates and thin agency coverage"* at C-Rank and B-Rank, breaks *"simply endured"*, and a real toll far above the reported one.

**Under-ranked is an assessment failure, not a Rank ceiling.** The two are different and both are real: Section 9.5's confidence swing is where the first lives, and thin appraiser coverage makes it worse. Nothing in this edge touches it.

**The Jiu Valley is not demoted but explained.** An S-Rank Gate opens on deep ground, and deep ground is where the S-Ranks live, so the ordinary case is that one is answered. Romania is Europe: deep, thirty-three days of cover, and the Exclusion happened anyway because the timer beat the response into a valley with one road. That is a covered region failing on its worst day, which is why the whole profession remembers it and why there is exactly one.

### 4. Band IV at metro scale, and the two arithmetic consequences

Section 9.1.6's **Band IV** - under 150 licensed hunters - takes the shallow ceiling: `E 50 / D 25 / C 15 / B 8.5 / A 1.5 / S 0`.

- **The wider map's S-Rank traffic falls 0.55 -> 0.31 a day**, because it is now Bands I-III only: `(22 + 22.5 + 18) x 0.5%`. Band IV's forty-eight Gates a day contribute none.
- **The red-gate escalation rate falls 3% -> 2.4%**, the A+S tail being 3% across Bands I-III and 1.5% in Band IV, weighted across the whole wider map.
- **The break line does not move.** The redistribution preserves the C-and-above mass at exactly **25%** - `15 + 8.5 + 1.5` against the baseline's `15 + 7 + 2.5 + 0.5` - so the 25% escalation rate is still asserted against a distribution that produces it.

The daily stream therefore composes to **0.23** rather than 0.30, and the roll table opens `01-77` rather than `01-70`. **An S-Rank result may not be sited in Band IV or off-roster**; it re-rolls across Bands I-III. A ceiling that a siting table can route around is not a ceiling.

## What did not change

**No authored city moves.** Bands I, II and III are untouched, so **Chicago and Prague roll Section 9.2.1 exactly as they always have** - the ceiling only ever binds on places this profile has not authored, and applying a band rule back onto the two cities that supplied the band derivation would rewrite resolved law from its own output.

**Section 9.2.1 and Section 9.2.2 are byte-identical.** The world's tier distribution is unchanged and remains what Chicago rolls; depth re-weights a *local* application of it and never edits the table. The escalation ramp is untouched and the light ticks still credit it nothing.

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value or threshold, damage formula, break timer, population, loot table, shop price, skill, title, class grant, cap, or Mana cost moves.** Section 9.5's confidence table and Section 9.6's anomaly table are untouched - a red gate is still 51-80 of the anomaly band wherever the anomaly is rolled.

**Section 9.1.7's border rule, its four doors, its weekly clock and its `+20`/`-20` reach modifiers are unchanged.** Its siting table is re-derived against the new flow, which moves Asia 40 -> 48 and Africa 26 -> 24 in a table that was always explicitly rounded.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.108** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. No past roll is replayed.** A `world_ticks` entry written under 1.106 or 1.107 recorded what was rolled at the time and stands. **The ceiling governs forward**, on Section 9.5's own precedent for a rule that changes what a later roll produces without reaching back into a resolved one.

**3. No Gate in play is re-Ranked.** Any Gate already detected, posted, entered or resolved keeps the Rank it was assessed and resolved at, wherever it sits.

**4. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
