## What 1.110 changes

**Section 12.8 has always said a core sets a ceiling.** *"The commissioner supplies the core - the core's Rank sets the ceiling of the piece's Rank."* **Nothing in this profile ever explained why that may happen exactly once**, at forging, and never again to a piece that already exists.

### 1. The stop that had no author

Seamwork's `Raise` rung carries a piece **to** its authored ceiling, and the clause past it read ***"a [C-Rank] piece whose ceiling is reached cannot be raised by him or by anyone."*** That second half - *or by anyone* - was never a rule anybody wrote down as a design position. It was a bound on **one skill**, stated as a bound on **the world**.

**The campaign found it the expensive way.** `EVT-000813` spent **two S-Rank Beast Cores and a plain Quickknife [S]** on a power-ceiling working "for no result." That was not a bad roll: the working had nothing to reach from the moment it was attempted, and **nothing in the profile said so in advance** - Section 4.4's own ceiling-read grant was never consulted, because it did not return the thing that mattered. Raised as `F-058`.

### 2. Re-coring (Section 12.11)

**An artificer reseats an existing piece around a higher-Rank core, and the core sets a new ceiling** - the origination rule of Section 12.8 applied to an object that already exists. It is **artificer work**: Section 12.8's *"the signature originates; the hands may still work"* is unmoved, and this is the signature's half.

**It raises the ceiling and never the piece.** Two costs, both paid:

1. the artificer re-cores, and the ceiling moves;
2. ordinary **Seamwork** carries the piece up to it - `Seat` at B spending material per step, `Raise` at S in one working - or a further commission does.

**Section 12.8's *ceilings are why he still shops* therefore stands**: the way to a higher ceiling is still a higher-Rank core, and cores are killed for.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Eligible** | functional; **standing at its current ceiling**; offered core **above** that ceiling; not a named artifact, class-bound item, or instruction; not equipped |
| **Material** | **one core at the target Rank, or four at the Rank below**, consumed |
| **Fee** | the Section 12.8 anchor - about the market price of a same-Rank core |
| **Bench** | 1 day to C, 2 at B, 3 at A, **5 at S** |
| **Resolution** | d100 on the Section 12.10 bands, **01-05 destroys the piece** at every modifier |

**A fused piece is eligible and a re-cored piece may be re-cored again.** Section 12.10's once-ever rule is about seams already opened and rejoined; this is a different working on a different part of the object, and **each step costs its own core**, which is the whole of what bounds the ladder.

**The four-at-the-Rank-below clause is the one that matters in a mature campaign.** Banked low-Rank cores are Section 12.5's refused goods with no outlet but an artificer; the live campaign holds **38 C-Rank and 14 B-Rank cores** recorded as *"banked, unsold, unworked."* This is the outlet.

### 3. The Ascension (Section 12.11.1), and why it is not a tier

**S is the top of the Rank ladder and 1.110 does not move it.** Section 11.5's tables end at S, Section 6.1's Rank Health ends at S, and **no equipment Rank above S exists or is authored anywhere.**

A piece at an S ceiling, at S, has no higher Rank to be given - so it is given a **name**. Section 11.6 already authors objects outside the ladder: *"each one-of-a-kind, each carrying a property ordinary ranked gear does not,"* *"worth far past its Rank on any market,"* entering play *"only where a file authors it doing so."* **An Ascension is that authoring, performed at a bench.**

- **Every number stays put.** Stat budget, weapon power, physical reduction and chassis line are unchanged, and the piece remains S-Rank under Section 11.5.
- **It gains one named effect and a recorded provenance**, and **the artificer names it and marks it** - the widest Section 19 exposure surface an object can carry.
- **It is not rolled.** Section 11.6 forbids generating a named unique at random, so an Ascension resolves on an **owner ruling**; the material conditions make the attempt possible and the result is authored at that moment. A Runtime reaching one with no authored result **stops and asks**.

**A rung above S was considered and refused.** `F-058` offered four remedies and this is the second: *a named-artifact-only forging route*. The first - a new tier - would inflate one side of every equation the world is balanced on, against the owner's standing ruling this version that bigger numbers are not progression. **An Ascension changes what a piece is, not how large it is**, and it does it through a person, with materials, over a relationship - the same shape 1.109's route 5 authors for skills.

### 4. Flux Sight answers the question that cost two cores

Section 4.4's ceiling read returns whether a piece stands at its ceiling; **from 1.110 it also returns whether that ceiling is re-corable and at what Rank a core would have to stand.** That is `F-058`'s own second question, and it is what would have stopped `EVT-000813` before material was spent.

## What did not change

**No Rank moves anywhere, in either direction.** Section 11.5's four stat-budget tables, its armor reduction table, every chassis line, Section 6.1's Rank Health and Rank Mana, Section 12.5's every price, and the whole Gate apparatus are byte-identical. **There is no S+1 for equipment, monsters, Gates, or anything else.**

**Section 12.10 fusion is untouched.** It still moves an effect and never a magnitude, its once-ever rule still governs fusion, its bands and costs are unchanged. Re-coring is a separate working that neither replaces nor feeds it.

**Seamwork's own bound is unchanged.** He still cannot lift a ceiling with his own hands - that is the signature's half of the trade and this skill has never held one. What changed is that the clause stating it stopped claiming to speak for the whole world.

**No existing item changes.** No ceiling is recomputed, no piece is re-Ranked, and nothing in any loadout moves on adoption.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.**

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.110** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. No ceiling is backfilled or recomputed.** Every piece keeps the ceiling it already had. A ceiling that was reached stays reached; what changes is that an operation now exists which can move it, and that operation is a scene rather than a recalculation.

**3. `EVT-000813` is not re-settled.** The two S-Rank cores and the Quickknife [S] are spent and stay spent. The working failed correctly under the law in force at the time, and this profile does not refund a resolved Event - Section 9.5's precedent for a rule that governs forward without reaching back into a settled one applies unchanged.

**4. No artificer gains a capability retroactively.** Whether Priscilla Nakamura or Corwin Ashvale has ever completed a re-core is campaign canon and belongs to play; the `-1` step for an artificer who has not is the ordinary case on adoption, for everyone.

**5. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
