## What 1.49 changed

**Multitask granted a ceiling nobody reached.** Its entire effect was the cap on concurrent non-daily quests — 2/3/4 at E/D/C, extended to 5/6/7 at 1.48. Across the whole of Gatefall's play record that cap never once bound. The live Bearer sat at capacity 4 holding **two** attached Hidden pointers, with **no** Urgent quest ever accepted in ninety in-fiction days, and the skill's own `successful_uses` counter stood at **0** while the other four Stat Passives read 8, 4, 2, and 1. The binding constraint on concurrent quests is **supply** — Section 8.4.2's crisis criteria and Section 8.4.3's concealed-discovery and proximity requirements — and never the ceiling. A Rank step that raises an unreached cap grants nothing a Bearer can notice, which is Section 7.2's Rank Dominance Law failing on its own terms: the higher Rank could do nothing the lower Rank could not.

**Capacity moves off the skill and onto System Rank.** Section 8.4 now derives it directly: **2 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 7** at E / D / C / B / A / S (Section 6.6). No skill, allocation, item, or title raises it. Capacity still grows with progression — System Rank grows — it simply stops pretending to be a capability. `system_state.non_daily_quests` accordingly drops `base_capacity`, `multitask_bonus`, and `analyst_bonus` and keeps `capacity_total`, `active`, and `pending_offers`.

**Conduit takes the Intelligence slot.** One category, escalating across all six rungs the way Flux Sight's reading widens and Shrug Off's endurance deepens: **+5 / +10 / +15 / +20 / +25 / +30 percentage points** added to both of Section 5.2's recovery rates. At B-Rank that reads 30% active and 45% resting against a 10/25 baseline.

**It composes in a fixed order and cannot reach inside a fight.** Base rate, then Conduit's points, then any environmental halving (a Frozen Gallery halves the improved rate, not the baseline), then any multiplier a title or item grants — the **Wellspring** title's out-of-combat doubling lands last. Recovery is settled from elapsed fictional time and a combat exchange is not an hour, so **no Conduit rung refills a pool mid-fight or softens Section 5.3's exhaustion**; the −1 step at 0 Mana and the drained-boss tactic it supports are untouched. Nothing here alters a skill's Mana cost, which remains mastery's axis alone (Section 7.2).

**Two 1.48 rungs are corrected to their own category.** The same standard that condemned Multitask condemns a rung that changes subject:

- **Overpower A** was *Anchoring* (immovability), which is defence; the category is dominating above your own Rank. It becomes **overpower or restrain up to three Ranks above System Rank, and break structures one Rank above the skill** — E's one Rank and D's two, continued.
- **Pre-empt A** was *Interposition* (taking a hit for another), which is protection; the category is acting ahead of the beat. It becomes **shared first blade** — every ally within his call resolves before any opponent up to the skill's Rank in an exchange he opens, as C extended E from himself to those who can hear him.

Shrug Off's and Overpower's other rungs stand. **No Bearer had reached A-Rank on any Stat Passive at adoption**, so neither correction takes away a capability anyone held.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.48 → 1.49 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Retire Multitask and author Conduit.** Remove Multitask's `skills_known` entry and its `skills.multitask.successful_uses` tracked counter, and author **Conduit** in its place with `successful_uses` at **0**. The retired counter transfers nothing: Multitask's lifetime count was 0 in the only campaign that held it, so no history is lost and none is invented. Conduit's Rank derives immediately under Section 4.4 from base Intelligence, clamped by System Rank + 1 as every Stat Passive is.

**2. Re-derive quest capacity from System Rank.** Set `capacity_total` from the Section 8.4 ladder and delete `base_capacity`, `multitask_bonus`, and `analyst_bonus`. **Where the new capacity is lower than the old, no quest is evicted, cancelled, or displaced**: an over-capacity Bearer simply attaches nothing new until he is back under his ceiling, exactly as Section 8.4 already handles a full slate. Nothing was silently displaced when capacity rose at 1.48 and nothing is silently displaced when it falls here.

**3. Apply the two rung corrections prospectively.** Overpower's and Pre-empt's A rungs change text only. No Bearer stands at A-Rank on either — the System Rank + 1 ceiling puts A out of reach below System Rank A — so nothing is withdrawn from anyone, and no past exchange resolved under the superseded wording.

**4. Nothing retroactive, and no recovery is recomputed.** Conduit applies **from adoption forward only**. Every prior Mana settlement stands exactly as resolved: no elapsed span is re-settled at the new rate, no `mana_recovery_remainder_units` is recomputed, and no pool is topped up to what it would have held had Conduit existed earlier. Rules Section 13.2 forbids reopening what a settlement has spent, and Section 5.2's determinism depends on a settled span staying settled.

**5. Nothing else moves.** No pool maximum, Stat, XP total, level, System Rank, mastery counter, title, item, gold, cash, deadline, or streak is touched. The other four Stat Passives keep their Ranks and their `successful_uses`. No skill outside Multitask changes identity, and Flux Sight is untouched in every respect.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.48-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.49 at readiness.
