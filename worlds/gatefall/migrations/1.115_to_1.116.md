## What 1.116 changes

**New Section 7.7, Consolidation.** Two or more held techniques sharing a functional role may fold into one, carrying every constituent's distinct behavior forward as a selectable tier or mode rather than flattening any of them away. No roll (Rules Section 4.2's automatic-success standard — a Bearer already holds and understands everything going in). No Mana/gold/action cost and no Section 7.6-style world-facing exposure price, since consolidation compresses existing capability rather than acquiring new capability. A Stat Passive is never eligible (Section 4.4's derivation from a base Stat cannot be folded into a flat grant). The merged entry sums `successful_uses`/`qualifying_scenes_total` from its constituents, takes the highest constituent mastery level, and stays `transmissible: false` if any constituent was.

**Applied immediately in the same ruling, five consolidations on `gatefall_pendragon_001`'s own protagonist sheet** (all Succession-sourced, all `transmissible: false`, none Stat Passives):

| Merged | Constituents struck | Rank / cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron Frame** | Unbreaking, Iron Hide, Granite Hide | [S-Rank] Master, Passive |
| **Full Assessment** | Threat Assessment, Cold Read, Reading the Room | [S-Rank] Master, tiered: Passive / Mana 3 / Mana 4 |
| **Bounding Seal** | Lockdown Field, Restraining Field | [S-Rank] Master, two modes: Mana 35 (area) / 20 (single-target) |
| **Binding Grip** | Chain Lash, Earthbind, Subdue | [A-Rank] Master, three modes: Mana 16 (Root) / 18 (Bind) / 20 (Subdue) |
| **Field Sense** | Tremor Sense, Echo Sense | [B-Rank] Master, Passive |

Ninety-six known techniques become eighty-eight. Category totals move with them (Offense 22→19, Defense 20→18, Utility 54→51), sum unchanged at 88.

## What did not change

**No behavior any constituent carried disappears.** Full Assessment's free ranged read, Bounding Seal's area-mode self-blocking clause, and Binding Grip's three distinct restraint shapes (movement-only, full immobilize, non-lethal takedown) are each still reachable, named, in the merged entry — the completeness test Section 7.7 states explicitly. **Iron Frame is the one place a real number is authored that did not exist before**: none of Unbreaking, Iron Hide, or Granite Hide ever had a quantified magnitude, so consolidating them is also the first time this specific grant gets one, not a loss of three different numbers down to one.

**No mastery, use-history, or provenance is discarded.** Merged `successful_uses` sum their constituents' counts (Full Assessment carries the 1 prior use from Cold Read at `EVT-000961`; Bounding Seal carries Lockdown Field's 3 uses this checkpoint, all in area mode). `taken_from` history for every struck constituent is preserved in the merged entry's own provenance note.

**No XP, level, gold, cash, or Stat moves.** This is a ledger-shape change on the skills list alone.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.116** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Strike the thirteen named constituent entries and write the five merged entries in their place**, each carrying the summed counters, highest mastery, and preserved `taken_from`/`transmissible` flags per Section 7.7.

**3. A capture restored across this edge with none of the thirteen named constituents present runs step 1 only** — the merge has nothing to do if the constituents were never held.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
