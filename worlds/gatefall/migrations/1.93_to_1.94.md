## What 1.94 changes

**Three rates, all of them 1.93's, all of them calibrated against the wrong clock.** 1.93 authored the escalation from `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 9 correctly and then sized every interval against the Bible's **decade-scale 2016-2026 timeline**. The campaign it governs covered **33 in-fiction days**, level 1 to 48, an unawakened E-Rank card to a carded S-Rank, and the entire System Rank ladder. Every one of 1.93's three rates was therefore slower than the story it was running inside, and the practical effect was a world that escalated on paper and never once in play.

**No distribution changes. No Rank, reward, timer, population, price or table moves.** This edge only retimes what 1.93 already authored.

### 1. The ramp's schedule: 184 days to 14

| | 1.93 | 1.94 |
|---|---|---|
| Tier IV | 2027-02-24 (184 days) | **2026-09-07 (14 days)** |
| Tier V | 2027-05-24 (+89) | **2026-09-14 (+7)** |
| Ramp's end | 2027-07-05 (+42) | **2026-09-18 (+4)** |

The halving structure is unchanged; only its unit is. **A first rung 184 days out sat more than five times the campaign's entire length past the end of it**, which is a countdown that expires after the story does. The Bible's curve is geometric rather than calendar-bound, and the interval that matters is the one the protagonist experiences: a world that produces a champion in a month is one the Assay is escalating against in weeks.

### 2. Escalation credit: every break counts

1.93 credited only losses - a Strained district, a Cordon, a Scar, an A-Rank break - on the reasoning that a contained break is the system working. **It is, and it is also the signal.** The Bible says *"breaks grow more frequent"* and its planted clue-line is a chart of break **frequency**, not of districts lost. Crediting only catastrophes meant the ramp read everything except the one series the world is actually measuring.

**And the line it did price was unreachable.** An A-Rank posting is taken by the trade at 90% a day against a three-day timer, so it survives to break at 0.1% - about **once per seven thousand days** at tier III. That is the `F-002` shape: a clause whose trigger cannot fire.

The new table credits `+1` for any break, `+2` more at C-Rank or above, and raises Cordon from +3 to +5. At Chicago's own take rates that is roughly **1.1 breaks a day, about +1.2 credit a day, and 20 credit in about 17 days** against a 14-day clock floor - **the two routes are now the same order of magnitude**, which is what "the clock leads and pressure pulls it forward" was supposed to mean and did not.

### 3. An audition always rolls the anomaly table

1.93 had a Section 9.12 audition roll Section 9.6 **against the tier's band** - 8% at tier III - which put a red gate roughly **150 days** from a Bearer at Combat Tier S. The Bible does not describe an audition as a Gate that might be strange; it names *"red-gate anomalies"* and *"crucibles built to look survivable and not be"* as **what an audition is**. Every audition now rolls Section 9.6.

At the Bearer's own audition rate that is an anomalous, Bearer-sized Gate roughly every three or four days, of which about **three in ten seal behind him**. A red gate moves from one per ~150 days to one per ~12.

**One new clause, because the anomaly table can run out of ladder.** A `01-50` *population one Rank above* on an audition whose true Rank is already S resolves as **doubled population at the true Rank** - twice the `3d6` commons and twice the `1d2` elites, boss unchanged. Nothing else on the table needs a cap.

## What did not change

**Section 9.2.1's tier table is untouched** - the same five tiers, the same distributions, the same day-counts, the same anomaly bands. Tier III remains byte-identical to the pre-ramp constant.

**Section 9.12's keying, share table and switch-off are untouched**: Combat Tier and nothing else, the same 15/25/33/50 shares, the same on-route restriction, and the same rule that a party meeting the Section 9.4 legal minimum suppresses the audition entirely.

**Section 9.11, Section 9.5's tier-read band, Section 9.6's table and its tier V confluence, and the whole of 1.92 are unchanged.** No Rank Health, XP value, threshold, damage formula, break timer, population formula, archetype, loot table, price, title, class or quest rule moves.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.94** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. If `escalation_tier` and `escalation_credit` are already initialised, leave them.** 1.93's step 2 sets them to `III` and `0`; this edge does not move either. If the 1.93 adoption has not yet been played, initialise them there as that record says.

**3. The new dates are absolute and are measured from the 1.93 adoption anchor, not from this edge.** Tier IV lands no later than 2026-09-07 whether the campaign adopts 1.94 today or a week from now; retiming the schedule does not restart it.

**4. Nothing else moves, and nothing is recomputed.** No Gate already rolled changes Rank, no anomaly already resolved is re-rolled, no break already settled is credited retroactively, and no audition already resolved is re-anomalied. All three changes are prospective from adoption. Credit still starts from whatever the ledger holds and is not backdated over the campaign's existing break history - that history was settled under profiles with no ramp to feed, and crediting it now would advance the tier on evidence gathered before the rule existed.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0120 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
