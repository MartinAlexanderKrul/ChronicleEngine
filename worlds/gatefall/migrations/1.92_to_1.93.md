## What 1.93 changes

**This implements the World Bible rather than adding to it**, and that distinction governs how every claim below should be read.

`200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 9 has always stated the escalation directly - *"The break rate is not a trend. It is a countdown. The Assay escalates on a fixed schedule... Gates get denser, Ranks climb, breaks grow more frequent - all on a geometric curve with an asymptote."* Its 2026 timeline row reads *"the worldwide break rate is at an all-time high and still climbing."* Its second planted clue-line is a leaked analyst chart showing break frequency **doubling on a fixed interval**.

Profile Section 9.2 implemented that as a **flat constant**, and Section 9.1's Chicago rate had not moved once in a hundred in-fiction days. The clue-line was unplayable: there was no doubling to chart. **1.93 is that correction.**

### 1. Section 9.2 - one distribution becomes five tiers

The Rank distribution and the metro day-counts are read from the world's **escalation tier**. **Tier III is exactly the 1.92 numbers** - Chicago 1d4+3, Prague 1d2, 50/25/15/7/2.5/0.5 - so nothing about the present moment moves on adoption. Tiers I and II are the retroactive explanation of the Bible's 2016-2026 timeline; tiers IV and V are where the curve goes.

**What doubles is the top, not the total.** Chicago's daily count rises 5.5 -> 6.5 -> 7.5 across tiers III to V (about 36%), while A-Rank density goes 0.14 -> 0.33 -> 0.68 per day and S-Rank 0.028 -> 0.065 -> 0.15, roughly x2.4 and x2.3 a tier. E-Rank density is nearly flat. The bottom of the ladder stays the job it always was; the top arrives twice as often each rung.

### 2. Section 9.2.2 - what advances it

**The clock is a floor and is fixed**, at halving intervals in campaign time: tier IV no later than **2027-02-24**, tier V by **2027-05-24**, and the ramp's end at **2027-07-05**. Beyond tier V this profile authors no distribution; that is owner-authored campaign canon.

**Pressure pulls it forward and never pushes it back.** `escalation_credit` accrues on the world's losses - Strained +1, Cordon +3, an A-Rank-or-above break +5, a new Scar +8, a Section 9.1.4 escalation-elsewhere +3, an unprecedented event +5 - and at **20** the next tier arrives and the credit resets. An ordinary contained break credits nothing directly; Section 9.1.3's pressure track already prices repetition, and what credits here is the city **losing ground**.

**The ramp is keyed to the clock and to the world, never to the Bearer's Rank, and this was a deliberate ruling.** A distribution that read his System Rank would make levelling worsen the world and make the optimal play *stop levelling* - a perverse incentive pointed directly against the campaign. The Bible's own wording is *"a fixed schedule keyed to how long a world has resisted producing a viable champion"*: time, not the candidate.

### 3. Section 9.5 - the anomaly band widens with the tier

The true-Rank table's anomaly threshold `A` is read from the tier: **97 / 95 / 93 / 90 / 86** across tiers I to V. At the present tier III it is 93, so the table reads exactly as it always has. The *one Rank higher* band absorbs the difference; the ladder's shape is unchanged and only the tail thickens.

### 4. Section 9.6 - a tier-V entry

At escalation tier V a 96-00 anomaly becomes a **confluence**: two interiors joined at a seam, each with its own d8 archetype and its own full Section 9.7 population, sharing one mouth, and **the mouth does not close on the first boss**. The clear window begins only when both are dead. This is the first thing the world will meet that no protocol was written against.

### 5. New Section 9.12 - the audition

A share of Gates sited **on his route** are sized to him. Keyed to **Combat Tier** (Section 6.7) and to nothing else - not System Rank, not the BGM card: 15% at C, 25% at B, 33% at A, 50% at S, and none below C.

An audition Gate is **assessed on the ordinary distribution**, so the board, the contract and the legal minimum all carry a number that is wrong. Its **true Rank is read from the Bearer on first entry** - d100, `01-70` his Combat Tier and `71-00` one Rank above, capped at S - replacing Section 9.5's table. And it **rolls Section 9.6 regardless of confidence**, which is the only route by which a red gate reaches a Bearer who does not walk into unconfirmed public postings.

**It switches off entirely if he crosses the threshold with a party meeting the Gate's Section 9.4 legal minimum.** Being tested is the price of working light, and that is the section's whole risk economy. The check is made once, at the threshold, on who actually crosses.

**The cause is owner-facing and stays there.** `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 9 authors it - *"engineers auditions - Gates seeded to mislabel, red-gate anomalies, crucibles built to look survivable and not be"* - together with the third planted clue-line by which a player may work it out. The profile carries mechanics only; a Runtime never names an audition or hints the category exists.

## What did not change

**No Rank Health, Rank Mana, XP value, threshold, damage formula, break timer, population formula, archetype, loot table, price, title, class or quest rule moves.** The anomaly *table* is unchanged below tier V. Section 6.6's rule that a posted or assessed Gate never bends around one hunter is untouched: an audition does not resize a Gate around him, it is a Gate that was **already** what it is and whose assessment is wrong - which Section 9.5 has described since 1.74.

**No supply is created anywhere.** An audition reclassifies an on-route Gate the tick already rolled; the ramp changes the composition of what opens, not the fact that the tick rolls it.

**Section 9.11 directed assignment, and the whole of 1.92, are unchanged.**

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.93** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Initialise the escalation state on the world ledger.** `110_WORLD_LEDGER.md` gains two tracked values, on the Section 9.1.3 pattern:

```text
escalation_tier    III
escalation_credit  0
```

**Tier III is the correct starting value and is not a judgement call**: it is the tier whose distribution is byte-identical to what 1.92 already ran, so initialising there means no Gate rolled before this edge was rolled on the wrong table and nothing is recomputed. `escalation_credit` starts at 0 because the breaks and pressure already settled in this campaign were settled under a profile with no ramp to feed; **crediting them retroactively would advance the tier on evidence gathered before the rule existed**, which is the retcon this repository forbids.

**3. The clock's dates are absolute and are not rebased.** Tier IV lands no later than 2027-02-24 regardless of when this migration runs. A capture restored from an earlier point does not restart the schedule.

**4. Nothing else moves.** No Gate, posting, district pressure value, Scar, break record, pool, Stat, item, counter, skill entry or anchor changes. Every Gate already rolled keeps the Rank it was rolled at. The audition is prospective from adoption: no past Gate is reclassified, and in particular the campaign's two A-Rank instant dungeons and every public Gate in the record stand exactly as resolved.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs steps 1 and 2 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0120 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
