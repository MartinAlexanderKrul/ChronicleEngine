## What 1.121 changes

**Section 7.2, Sprint's S rung.** `No ground needed` gains its figure: sustained cross-country travel runs at **850 km/h, terrain allowing**. Ratified at the figure the Paris to Amstelveen run implied (about 500 km in about 35 minutes).

**Section 4.4, Flux Sight.** A one-paragraph clause states that the E-rung Gate read fires from outside, on the Gate entering perception, without the Bearer entering it. The rung's wording implied this; it now says it. Player ruling C.

**Section 6.2, damage.** One general paragraph admits a named per-exchange multiplier as **its own factor in the damage product**, outside the net +/-3 cap because it is not a modifier step, resetting when the fight ends.

**`270_THE_FAR_SIDE.md`, The Count.** The row read *+1 to his damage step, uncapped*, and "damage step" was never defined anywhere. It now reads **+10% damage per exchange already fought**, as the Section 6.2 factor above, uncapped, resetting when the fight ends, applied identically to whoever holds it. This is the one place the transcription replaces a stated value rather than adding text, and it does so because the old value could not be applied.

**Section 12.1.1 (new), unbound-awakened runes.** Value is read against the combined crystal and core haul of a Gate **two Ranks above the rune's own**, **1x** for defensive and utility techniques and **1.5x** for offensive ones.

**Section 13.6, signatures.** A signature may carry **any number of earned branches**, each earned in play by diagnosis, real Mana and real risk, none compounding, none adding a magnitude beyond Rank and the three Section 13.5 effects.

## What did not change

**No stored field changes shape or meaning.** No checkpoint is rewritten. No XP, level, pool, gold, cash, Stat, skill or holding moves.

**No mechanic is newly invented.** Every clause restates a ruling the player already made, and the campaign has been playing under each since it was ruled. Where the ruling was silent, the text says so and stops; see the last section.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.121** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Regenerate the runtime index** (`tools/generate_runtime_index.ps1`), then confirm with `-Check`.

**3. Validate** with `tools/validate_repository.ps1`.

## What is owed and not done here

The campaign's `provisional_mechanics` entry for unbound-rune pricing still carries its `owes:` line; closing it is a canon write, so it waits for that same save.

**Open, and deliberately not decided here.**

- **Ruling C's citation.** The chronicle cites it as recorded at `EVT-001052`, a promotion-barrier settlement, and describes it as elected earlier in the session. The exact originating exchange was not located; the citation above is the one the chronicle itself gives.
- **`The Count` as held by the Bearer.** The row now applies to whoever holds it. Whether the Bearer's own copy is a separate authored entry was not checked against his skill list.
