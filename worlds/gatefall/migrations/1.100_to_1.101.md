## What 1.101 changes

**The defect is arithmetic, not narrative.** Section 4.3 caps the stat-margin contribution at +2 steps specifically so skill, title, and circumstance retain room inside the engine's ordinary net ±3 cap - and a Bearer whose effective Stat clears the opposing domain rating by 20 or more, which is every roll once base Stats pass roughly 80, takes that capped +2 automatically. That leaves exactly **one** step of headroom per roll, shared by every skill and title that might apply to it. Five Section 7 skill passives (Broken Rhythm, Exploit Pattern, Silent Step, Sprint, Keen Sense) already compete for that one step in their own domains. A Section 16 title whose entire passive read `+1 modifier step` therefore paid out only on the minority of rolls where nothing else had already claimed the slot - and two titles (`Vanguard`, `The Unmeasured`) stated a *two*-step grant against a budget that has never had two steps of headroom to give, so a fraction of even their nominal value was structurally unspendable from the day they were authored.

This was raised in play, not diagnosed from the text: the live Bearer's Stats (all four figures against a domain-rating ceiling of 60) made the crowding total rather than occasional, and a title-by-title audit turned up the same shape repeated across the catalog.

### 1. Section 16.2 and 16.4 - every step-only passive rebased

**No earning criterion, grade, or point grant changes.** Only the "Passive (while equipped)" cell changes, and only where the passive was pure step. Titles that already paid out in a resource, reduction, multiplier, or capability (`Red Survivor`, `Centurion`, `Prospector`, `Ascetic`, `Deathless`, `Executioner`, `Wellspring`, `Unbroken`, and the purely informational grants) are untouched.

**Common:** `Loner` (step -> 10% damage reduction while alone), `Untouchable` (step -> 50% reduced damage on the first hit landed each combat), `Break-Breaker` (step -> 15% reduced damage to what he defends), `Underdog` (step -> +15% damage vs. orange/red-designated creatures), `Diehard` (step -> 15% reduced damage at low Health), `Kingmaker` (step -> +15% damage on the relayed opening, still landing on the ally's own roll), `Penitent` (step -> 20% reduced Mana cost in an unopened seal), `Giantslayer` (step -> +15% damage vs. bosses).

**Rare:** `Cartographer`'s twist bonus (step -> +15% damage or effect), `Warden` (step -> 15% reduced damage, both the Bearer's own and the shared ally grant), `Vanguard` (two-step -> +30% damage or effect on the opening action, the double conversion rate matching the double step it replaces).

**Singular:** `The Unmeasured`'s concealment clause (two-step -> automatic success against anything but a named, direct System capability).

**Ascendant:** `Sealbreaker` (step -> 15% reduced damage in any sealed instance), `Cordonholder` (step -> 15% reduced Mana cost in a Strained-or-worse district), `The Standing Name` (two-step -> +30% effect on standing/negotiation/testimony actions, plus one penalty-free retry per week).

**Absolute:** `Before the Confluence` (step -> immunity to the first anomaly-imposed condition or penalty each triggering exchange).

**Section 16.3's composition rule is updated to match**: a percentage effect composes multiplicatively under Section 7.3 and shares no budget with the Section 4.3 step cap, so a Bearer may hold several percentage-paying titles at once without any of them crowding another - which is the entire point of moving them off the step economy.

### 1b. `Grounded` added — one title deliberately kept on the step economy

**Owner direction mid-migration: keep at least one Section 16 title paying out as a modifier step.** The rest of this migration's argument stands - a step-only passive is crowded out on most rolls by the Bearer's own stat margin and five Section 7 skills - but the catalog loses something if it never shows what a step-shaped title looks like at all. Rather than reverting one of the eleven rebased entries and re-narrowing its trigger condition, a new Rare title is added: **`Grounded`**, earned by a boss kill credited inside a Gate *and* a kill of a creature at or above his own System Rank credited entirely outside any Gate, sealed instance, or instant dungeon - proof the same danger meets him wherever he stands. Its passive is **+1 modifier step on all actions, without regard to whether the ground is a Gate**: no trigger condition at all, the broadest a step grant can be, and correspondingly the one most likely to actually be the thing claiming the roll's single open slot rather than losing it to a narrower title or an already-active skill. Rare grade fits exactly: Section 16.2's own grade table already defines a Rare passive's character as *"a step that applies broadly."*

**Catalog counts move:** Rare 6 -> 7, first catalog 21 -> 22, full catalog 32 -> 33. `The Ledger's End`'s criterion and Section 16.3's slot-count prose are updated to match. No other title's criterion, grade, or point grant is touched by this addition.

### 2. `Godslayer` replaced, not rebased

`Godslayer`'s passive was never a step, so it survives the rebasing pass on its own terms - and fails a different one. Section 16.2's own grade table requires a Singular to grant *"a capability the profile grants nowhere else."* Flux Sight's S-Rank rung already renders a complete read of any perceived subject, which necessarily includes exact Health, and Section 14.5.4 already renders the designation colour on the same line automatically. A Bearer holding Flux Sight at S gained nothing from equipping `Godslayer` that Section 4.4 was not already giving him for free - confirmed in the live campaign, where the title sat earned-but-unequipped by the Bearer's own choice before this migration existed. The passive is replaced with a boss-focused damage and XP grant: **+10% damage against a boss, and double Kill XP on a boss kill** - a capability nothing else in the profile states.

### 3. A second `Ledger's End` foreclosure, documented

Section 16.4's closing paragraph already names one way `The Ledger's End` can never be completed: `The Unmeasured` forecloses the moment a Bearer cards up under the Section 19.4 trade. A second, independent foreclosure existed in the rules text all along and was never written down: `Executioner` requires kills of creatures standing *above* the Bearer's own System Rank, and System Rank S is the profile's ceiling. A Bearer who reaches S without already holding fifty such kills can never bank another one - `Executioner`, and by inheritance `The Ledger's End`, foreclosed regardless of whether the Section 19.4 trade was ever taken. This is documentation, not a rule change: nothing about `Executioner`'s criterion, grade, or points moves. The live Bearer carded S-Rank at `EVT-000683` and was independently frozen on `Executioner` at roughly 20-25/50 before this migration was written; the note exists so a future table discovers the ceiling in the rules, not in play.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value, XP threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, price, skill, or class ability moves.** No *existing* title's earning criterion, grade, or point grant changes; `Grounded` is a wholly new catalog entry, not a change to one. Section 16.1's assessment procedure, schedule, settlement invariant, render block, and stored-state shape are untouched; only the derived catalog and Rare counts move, from the new entry. Section 18 is untouched. The only Section 16 titles whose passive text changes are the eleven named above plus `Godslayer`; `Grounded` is new; every other entry in both catalogs is byte-for-byte what 1.100 left it.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.101** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. No earned-title stored state changes.** `system_state.titles` (earned titles, grades, granting assessment, points) and `system_state.title` (the equipped-loadout list) are untouched by this migration - a held title's name, grade, and slot cost are exactly what they were. Only what a title's passive *does* when equipped changes, which is prospective rules text, not stored state.

**3. `Grounded` is not granted retroactively.** Section 16.1 governs unchanged: a title is tested and granted at an assessment. `Grounded` is simply a new row in the catalog the next assessment tests against, exactly like any title authored before a Bearer reached the level that first evaluates it. A rendered `catalog_size` (32 under 1.100) is derived from the active profile and reads 33 the moment 1.101 is adopted, independent of whether the Bearer has earned or will ever earn `Grounded`.

**4. A dormant title's effect is unaffected until equipped.** Any title held but not in a slot (e.g., a Bearer holding `Godslayer` unequipped) carries its new 1.101 passive the next time it is equipped; nothing about it fires retroactively and no past exchange is re-resolved.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1 only.** There is no earned-title state to recompute; a rendered `catalog_size` simply reads from the newly active profile.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Every checkpoint captured under 1.100 or earlier keeps what it was captured under; a Runtime resolving a title's effect for an action already recorded in one of them reads that checkpoint's own profile version, not the live one.
