## What 1.73 changed

**Five edges, on an owner critique the record bore out.** After ninety in-fiction days the campaign had issued **two** non-daily quests, both `hidden`, both attached to something the Bearer had already touched; **two of four slots had never been used**; **four Gate breaks had occurred and none had reached him**; **no red gate had ever rolled**; and the tracked board had decayed to **zero postings** with nothing to regenerate it. The owner's objection was that he should not have to author what he wants to be surprised by, and that the Runtime should take initiative. He was right, and the profile itself was part of the cause.

### 1. Section 9.1 — the daily world tick

The line replaced read: *"These are aggregate rates across the metro, not a schedule; the Runtime distributes them into the fiction as needed and does not roll a fixed calendar."* That sentence made every world event conditional on the Runtime choosing to author one.

**At each in-fiction day boundary the world is rolled**, before and independently of anything the Bearer does: Gate count (Chicago `1d4+3`, Prague `1d2`), each Gate's true Rank on §9.2, **rarity rolled every time** rather than when a scene wants one, §9.10 board timers and breaks, posting regeneration, and agenda initiations. **Results are binding, including inconvenient ones.**

### 2. Siting is rolled — proximity is an input, not a veto

Each Gate and each break is sited `d100` against the Bearer's recorded day: **01–10 on his route**, **11–30 within reach**, **31–00 elsewhere**. §8.4.2's Urgent criteria were never wrong; they had simply become the reason nothing was ever near him, because nothing generated nearness. The tick generates it.

### 3. Both quest triggers gain a push channel

A quest may **originate in the world and arrive unbidden**. The tick feeds §8.4 directly, and the mirror-only behaviour — quests attaching solely to things the player already touched — is closed.

### 4. Cost expressions must be total (`F-032` question 2)

Any authored cost naming a context-derived term must state a fallback for the contexts the same rule advertises, or state that the ability does not function without it.

### 5. Section 18.8 — the pocket cache is replaced by a standing anchor sense (`F-033`)

The cache duplicated two containers a Riftwalker already carries, and anchors three through five were inert in any scene where the Bearer stepped twice — the same defect 1.67 repaired in Soulforged Armory. The sense pays **from every set anchor at once**, is **stored state with a lifecycle**, and carries an enforceable firing obligation and a **reports-never-authors** constraint on the Runtime.

## Procedure for restoring a 1.72 capture

1. **Set `canonical_state.system_state.profile_version` to `"1.73"`.**
2. **Initialise `canonical_state.world_clock`.** `last_ticked` is set to the capture's `campaign_time`; the world is not retroactively rolled for elapsed days, and no back-dated Gate, break, posting, or agenda advance is invented. **The tick begins at the next day boundary after restore.**
3. **Relocate any pocket cache contents to §12.9 remote storage.** Nothing is lost — §12.9 is reachable from everywhere the cache was. Replace the `pocket_cache` state with `anchor_sense`, and give every set anchor `disturbance: none`.
4. **No pool, Stat, level, XP, gold, cash, counter, mastery level, title, quest, or resolved die outcome changes.** No damage preview moves.
5. **Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
6. Run `tools/validate_repository.ps1` and `tools/validate_runtime_configuration.ps1`.

## Campaign application, `gatefall_pendragon_001`

The pocket cache was granted **empty** at `EVT-000519` and never used, so edge 5's relocation is a no-op and only the state shape changes. `world_clock.last_ticked` initialises to **2026-08-19T14:05:00-05:00**, the current anchor; **the first tick falls at 2026-08-20 06:00**, which is also the next daily-quest issue and the Daily Premium rotation. No Gate, break, posting, or agenda advance is back-dated for the ninety days already played.
