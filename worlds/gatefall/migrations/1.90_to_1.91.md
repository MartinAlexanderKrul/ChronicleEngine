## What 1.91 changes

One change, prospective. **No stored value moves**, which is why `touched_state_paths` is empty and the classification is compatibility-treatment.

### Section 16.2's common titles table - Loner's passive condition

**The old text bound the effect to the earning ground.** Loner's criterion has always been a Gate cleared alone, and its passive read *+1 modifier step on all actions while no allied hunter shares the Gate* — wording that switches the bonus off the instant the Bearer stands anywhere that is not one. That reading held for every session before this one because nothing had put him anywhere else for long enough to notice.

**`ENT-000217` did.** An anomaly this same span establishes is expressly not a Gate, not an instance, and not the city — and Alexander stood in it, alone, for the whole scene. Nothing about *why* Loner is earned turns on the ground being a Gate; the criterion proves solitude, and the passive should track the same fact the criterion proves.

**The new text:** *+1 modifier step on all actions while no allied hunter is with him*, unconditioned on whether the ground is a Gate. **Scope note carried into the row itself:** an *allied hunter* is another awakened working with him; a nearby civilian, an NPC he is not fighting alongside, or an empty room does not switch it off, exactly as before.

**What this predictably touches.** Every roll made solo outside a Gate — which, by volume, is most of this campaign's actual play — now carries the same +1 the character has always carried inside one. It is applied prospectively from adoption; rolls resolved before this migration are not recomputed.

## What did not change

**The earning criterion is untouched.** A Gate cleared alone, its boss killed with no other hunter inside, Section 17 instant dungeons and Section 8.3 penalty zones counting as real clears — all exactly as 1.90 left it. Alexander's own Loner title (`EVT-000097`) is not re-proved and needs no re-proving.

**No other title's passive, no Rank, no XP threshold, no price, no timer, no population, band, or distribution moves.**

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.91** on the campaign's live character sheet.

**2. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, item, counter, anchor, quest reward, or skill entry changes. The widened passive is prospective from adoption forward: a roll resolved under 1.90's narrower reading is not retroactively recomputed.

**3. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0117 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
