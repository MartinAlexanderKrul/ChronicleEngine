## What 1.28 changed

Version 1.28 remains the **additive Gate-persistence advance** over frozen 1.27, closing the companion half of the defect 1.27 opened on. Section 9.3 gives every Gate a break countdown and Section 9.8 authors the disaster the entire regulatory apparatus exists to prevent, but nothing ever counted: a Gate the Bearer was contracted to had a live clock because the contract put it in front of him, and every other detection in the metro entered a scene as a line on a board and stopped existing when the scene ended. No Gate could therefore break anywhere except on the Bearer's own contract, which also left Section 8.4.2 — whose eligible crisis is a Gate break, a Gate creature, or a perceived sealed-instance hazard — with almost no input stream that was not excluded as routine contracted hunting. New Section 9.10 makes a detection that has entered the fiction **persist**: it stores its district, detection timestamp, assessed Rank and confidence, a break deadline **derived** from Section 9.3 rather than authored, its Section 9.4 posting floor, and a staffing status, and it resolves at its deadline **deterministically** — `staffed` or `held` clears off-screen as the ordinary case, `withdrawn` leaves the board, and only `posted` (still under its floor when the clock runs out) breaks under Section 9.8. This is **persistence, not simulation**: Section 9.1's aggregate rates remain explicitly not a calendar, the Runtime still never rolls the metro's ~35 weekly detections, and the board holds only the three-to-six postings the fiction has actually surfaced. **No stored field, magnitude, cost, counter, probability, owned item, completed transaction, or resolved outcome changes**, and Section 8.4.2's four criteria are untouched. Data Model 0.1.5 remains unchanged.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.27 → 1.28 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration. **No Gate breaks retroactively.** Every Gate named in a past scene is resolved exactly as that scene resolved it, no earlier posting is re-derived against Section 9.10, and no completed clear, filed split, or closed contract is reopened — the Red Line Corridor contract of `EVT-000051` was cleared on its deadline at `EVT-000059`/`EVT-000060` and stays closed. The board begins at adoption holding only postings live in the fiction at the adoption anchor, and grows only as ordinary channels surface new ones. Deadline resolution applies **prospectively only**, from the first tracked deadline that falls after adoption. Record the additive-upgrade acknowledgement in mutable live state; immutable Profile 1.27-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the applicable compatibility chain through 1.28 at readiness.
