## What 1.83 changed, and the third time this defect has been fixed

**A mastery level costs three qualifying scenes. Nine of sixteen skills were charging that and returning nothing.**

Section 7.4 has been amended for this twice. `F-014` found Broken Rhythm at a live mastery level having granted **nothing at any level**. Profile 1.52 answered it by giving every mastery-tracked skill an authored axis and a row in a table.

**Both fixes gave every skill an axis. Neither asked whether the axis could pay.** Measured on the live campaign at System Rank A:

| Route | Skills | Why the level returned nothing |
|---|---|---|
| **Magnitude pinned by its own floor** | Rupture, Mana Bolt | A **Rank-baseline** skill's multiplier band is identical at every Rank — Rank moves the baseline, not the band — so `magnitude_floor` lands equal to the **new Rank's Master value**. Rupture at [S] Adept authors ×2.30 under a floor of ×2.60; six scenes return it to exactly ×2.60. |
| **Duration already saturated** | Sprint, Flash Step | Section 7.2 saturates duration one Rank above native. A skill whose mastery axis *is* duration stops responding the moment it ascends once. |
| **A count of other people** | Keen Sense, Silent Step, Broken Rhythm, Resonance Extraction, Field Command | The axis counted allies, beneficiaries, recipients or miners. A Bearer who clears content alone held **twenty-two such slots and filled none**, at three qualifying scenes each. |

**Keen Sense is the case that makes the third route concrete.** 85 successful uses and 57 qualifying scenes — the most-exercised utility skill in the campaign — and its entire mastery axis bought companions the Bearer never brought.

**`tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` has a leg for the first route and it does not fire.** It asks whether Rank *N+1* Master beats Rank *N* Master, and it does, because the baseline grew 375 → 1,000. It never asks whether climbing mastery **within** the current Rank beats the floor already in force. The gate is not wrong; it answers a different question, and that question had been mistaken for this one.

## The rule

> **A mastery level must return something to the Bearer, in the play he is actually in.** Where its authored axis cannot, the level pays on the first axis below that can.

**Clause 1 — a binding floor ratchets with mastery.** While `magnitude_floor` binds, a mastery advance raises it by that skill's own increment, as a `counter_deltas` change. Rupture at [S] reads **×2.60 / ×2.75 / ×2.90**. The floor becomes a floor rather than a ceiling.

**Clause 2 — a saturated duration is not a mastery axis**, applied consistently across every skill on that axis.

**Clause 3 — a shared count belongs to Rank.** Every count of other people grows on Rank alone:

| | E | D | C | B | A | S |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Further subjects per Rank above native** | +0 | +1 | +2 | +3 | +4 | **+8** |

**Nothing is deleted and nobody loses reach.** Sharing survives everywhere it exists; it stops costing a mastery level. The level buys a five-rung self-facing **dividend** instead.

**Clause 4 — anything left pays Mana.**

## The seven dividends

Each is exactly five rungs. None is longer than another. All obey the division of labour: **Rank sells what a skill can reach; mastery sells how well it resolves.**

| Skill | Dividend |
|---|---|
| **Keen Sense** | `Resolution` — Direction · Number and distance · Discrimination · Live · Confidence |
| **Silent Step** | `Composure` — Re-establishes · Graceful failure · At speed · Under load · Unhurried |
| **Broken Rhythm** | `Tempo` — Free answer · Second answer · Answer with anything · Refund · Every commit |
| **Resonance Extraction** | `Yield` — More · Faster · Clean · Grade · Past spent |
| **Sprint** | `Control` — Standing start · Turn · Stop · Carry · Recovery |
| **Flash Step** | `Arrival` — Facing · Momentum · Silence · Precision · Composure |
| **Field Command** | `Conduction` — a sustained step · a sustained reduction · a passive · a window · two at once |

**`Tempo` is the reaction economy, and nothing else in this profile governs it.** Reactions appear throughout — Bulwark's `Anticipated`, Twin Fang's `Answer`, Micro-rift deflection by definition — and no skill has ever said how many a Bearer gets. Broken Rhythm is cadence, which is the beat of an exchange rather than perception or armour, so the economy is its ground. It compounds with the defensive kit instead of duplicating it, and it pays hardest exactly where a solo Bearer suffers most: swarmed.

**`Conduction` makes Field Command the profile's one sharing skill**, and it is bounded — recipients are its own Rank-derived count, no additional Mana, it ends when his own effect does, and it never shares a skill carrying its own scope count.

**Exploit Pattern is excluded from clause 3 and gains no dividend.** Its count is *concurrent Patterns* — his own reads, not people — and it already pays him in full alone.

## Retention, stated once

Section 7.2 carried this principle twice as two ratchets and once implicitly as the practice of listing every category a skill ever gained. It is one rule:

> **Nothing a skill has earned is ever taken back.** A category earned by Rank, a dividend rung earned by mastery, a scope count and a magnitude are all permanent — at every Rank, at every mastery level, through every ascension on either road.

The scope and magnitude ratchets are that rule applied to the two values that can otherwise *fall*. `dividend_level` is monotonic and needs no floor. **Categories were never at risk and were never floored, which is why nothing said so** — that silence is closed.

## Broken Rhythm's categories, ratified

The live campaign has rendered **`Cold read`** (D) and **`Shared cadence`** (C) since its rune ascension. This profile authored *Unseen cadence* and *Read the room*. Two pairs, neither aware of the other, with the Bearer holding the pair that was never written down.

**Ratified under Section 7.1 by owner ruling**, and recorded as a choice rather than allowed to stand by survival — `EVT-000590` withdrew improvised rungs of exactly this class three days earlier, and the difference is that this one was decided. *Unseen cadence* and *Read the room* are struck. **Nothing the Bearer holds is withdrawn.**

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.83"`.**

**2. Set every shared count's floor to what it holds today**, so clause 3 takes nothing. In the adopting campaign: Silent Step `scope_floor` 0 → **5**, Broken Rhythm 0 → **3**, Field Command 0 → **3**. Keen Sense (9) and Resonance Extraction (5) already hold theirs.

**3. Apply clause 2 where saturation has already occurred**, correcting any rendering that disagrees. A duration-axis skill more than one Rank above native renders **whole scene**, from the Rank at which it saturated. A rendering correction, not a grant.

**4. Repay every level ever climbed on a dead axis, capped at five rungs, overflow to Mana.** Each such level credits one rung of the skill's dividend, or one increment of a binding `magnitude_floor`. Beyond the fifth rung the level pays the standing −10% Mana reduction, minimum 1; beyond that it is spent and returns nothing, and the row below says so.

**5. `dividend_level` is created at the repaid value** and never decrements thereafter.

**6. No past Event, roll, damage figure, kill, clear or outcome is retroactively altered.**

**7. Nothing else moves.** No level, XP, Stat, Rank, mastery level, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `rank_ascensions`, title, item, gold, cash, quest, class, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor. Immutable Profile 1.82-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.83 at readiness.

## What this does not close

**It does not gate clause 1.** `test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` still asks its own question correctly; a leg asserting that mastery beats a binding floor **within** a Rank is delivered in `tools/test_gatefall_mastery_dividend.ps1` instead.

**It does not make Field Command pay a solo Bearer**, and does not pretend to. `Conduction` is excellent with a crew and inert without one. That is what the skill is.
