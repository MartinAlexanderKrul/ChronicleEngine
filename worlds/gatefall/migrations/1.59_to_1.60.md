## What 1.60 changed

**1.58 pre-authored a skill that should never have been authored, and 1.60 retires it.** `applied_analytical_reconstruction` was opened at `EVT-000436` at 2/3, given a complete result at 1.58 (**First Principles [E-Rank]**, passive) so that a third qualifying scene would ratify automatically rather than halting play at Section 7.1's adjudication gate. The candidate is now **`rejected`** and First Principles is removed from Sections 7.2, 7.3 and 7.4 and from the trigger manifest's `pre_authored_result_keys`.

**The native effect duplicated a modifier the Bearer already receives.** Both evidence scenes — `EVT-000372`'s Loomis Street camera geometry and `EVT-000398`'s Cicero Scar structural read — resolved on the engine's standing **established-capability** step, recorded in `EVT-000372`'s own text as *"+1 step for the directly relevant established capability."* That step fires for competence a character actually holds and requires no skill whatsoever; `EVT-000189` shows the same shape on a different domain (*"net +1 (established capability at reading people, no specific skill applies)"*). The Bearer is a university-trained mathematician and physicist. Sightlines, load paths, sweep cones and structural extent are what that training **is**.

**So the skill would have granted nothing.** Section 4.3 caps the net modifier at +1 and Section 7.2's non-stacking rule forbids a second modifier derived from the same finding, so on the exact scenes that formed the candidate, First Principles would have changed no roll, no margin, and no resolved outcome. **This is the Rank Dominance Law failing at acquisition rather than at ascension**: the law asks whether a higher Rank can do something the Rank beneath cannot, and here the *skill* could do nothing its own absence could not.

**Every mastery axis failed the same test from the other direction**, which is how the defect was finally isolated rather than patched:

- **Beneficiaries** (1.58's choice) priced a solitary analytical method as a group capability, extending a derivation to allies who had not made it.
- **Concurrent held derivations** rebuilt **Multitask's** retired defect exactly — a capacity ladder no play ever reaches, which 1.49 removed on the finding that *"a Rank step that raises an unreached cap grants nothing a Bearer can notice."*
- **Scale**, starting at *one wall*, asserted a floor **beneath** what the Bearer had already demonstrated **with no skill at all**: `EVT-000372` mapped four cameras' cones across a whole building and found a four-foot gap. Ratification would have been a downgrade dressed as progression.

Three independent axes, three failures, all tracing to the same root: there was no capability to grow because there was no capability being granted.

**The general rule this leaves behind.** A System skill is a capability the world does not otherwise grant. Where a Bearer's own background already resolves an action, the **established-capability step is the correct and complete pricing of it**, and Section 7.1's third test — *distinct capability*, excluding **generic competence** — is what keeps it out of formation. **Education is capability, not a skill.** It carries no Rank, no Mana cost, no mastery track and no System Rank ceiling, and it applies whenever it is directly relevant — strictly better than any E-Rank passive this profile could have authored for it. The Bearer loses nothing by this rejection; he keeps the thing that was always doing the work.

**Section 7.2 keeps the reasoning rather than deleting it.** A rejected candidate that leaves no trace invites the next reader to open it again on the same evidence, which is how a settled question becomes a recurring one.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.59 → 1.60.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.60"`.**

**2. Set the `applied_analytical_reconstruction` candidate to `rejected`** in `progression_candidates`, recording the owner-authored reason and the rejection's canon-bearing Event under Section 7.1. **Its evidence references are preserved unchanged** — `EVT-000372` and `EVT-000398` remain exactly as promoted. A campaign that never opened this candidate has nothing to change and this step is inert.

**3. No skill is removed from any ledger, because none was ever added.** First Principles existed only as authored profile text between 1.58 and 1.59; no Bearer in any campaign held it, no `skills_known` entry named it, and no `tracked_counters` row was ever created for it. There is no counter to retire, no mastery to unwind, and no Mana, Rank, or scope value to recompute.

**4. Nothing retroactive.** `EVT-000372` and `EVT-000398` are unchanged and remain valid exactly as resolved — on an uncertain action with the established-capability step, which is unaffected by this edge and continues to apply whenever the Bearer's background is directly relevant. No past roll is recomputed and no margin moves.

**5. The trigger manifest loses one key.** `pre_authored_result_keys` returns to `dimensional_weapon_control` alone. `dimensional_weapon_control` is untouched and **Dimensional Projection remains ratified and live** (`EVT-000436`); this edge reaches neither.

**6. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, skill, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched, and no ascension offer opens or changes terms.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.59-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.60 at readiness.
