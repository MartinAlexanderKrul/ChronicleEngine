## What 1.122 changes

**Section 13.7.5 (new) - Standing Branch Operations.** Sections 13.7.1 to 13.7.4 priced a guild's margins and never its throughput. That gap costs nothing while a guild is one bench in one metro and becomes unplayable the moment it is not: every Gate the institution answers still had to arrive one at a time through Section 9.1's tick or Section 9.11's channel, so a guild large enough to matter was a guild that could never be shown working. 13.7.5 closes it.

- **An office becomes a rate rather than a scene** once four things hold: a named officer who dispatches, round-the-clock cover, a bench it can legally crew from at the Ranks it dispatches (Section 9.4), and a filed standing relationship with its jurisdiction's regulator. Below that bar it is `standing up` and its Gates are narrated individually, exactly as before.
- **Catchment is the regulator's whole jurisdiction**, and response time is the only reason. Section 9.3's timers are what fence a bench; a guild holding a zero-latency transport network between its own offices has had that fence removed, and a guild without one reads its own metro's band and stops.
- **A daily dispatch per operational branch, rolled inside Section 9.1's step 4, for counts rather than Gates**: `1d4+1` at E-Rank and `1d3` at D-Rank every branch every day, `1d3-1` at C and `1d2-1` at B. Nothing here mints a posting, a per-Gate record, a break timer or an anomaly roll.
- **No background pass ever resolves an A-Rank or S-Rank Gate.** Those surface - through Section 9.11, through the branch officer's own call, or as news heard late - and become scenes.
- **The table scales to the bench** (halved between 60 and 125 hunters per operational branch, E and D only below 60), and **duty rate is tracked deterministically**, with an over-70% band that forces a branch to refuse work or file a roster it cannot staff.
- **Attrition is a call before it is a casualty.** One d100 per branch-day; `00` is a crew in distress, which surfaces and is answerable; only an unanswered call rolls for a death, and **no guild buries more than one member per 150 days**. The ceiling is lifted for a branch that dispatched under Section 9.4's floor.
- **Revenue composes from existing prices** - Section 12.3's bands at their midpoints, 13.7.3's 15% guild-backed premium, 13.7.3's salaried-roster rule - and authors none.
- **A background clear mints no identifier and pays the Bearer nothing.** No `EVT-`, `ENT-` or `REC-`; no XP, skill use, mastery counter, title progress or loot.

**Section 9.1, step 4.** The contest step gains the clause that routes 13.7.5's dispatch through it, and states plainly that the step's existing take rates are unchanged and still govern the board - what 13.7.5 adds is who, by name, was doing the taking.

**Section 9.1.1.** The tick's `world_ticks` entry gains a `guild_ops` line, on the same discipline as every other: `none` is a result and is written as one.

**Section 13.7.3, the D-Rank row.** The per-clear pool, crew size and non-leader share cells were blank (`-`) through 1.121. They are filled at **$11,500 / 8 / $1,294**, the midpoint of Section 12.3's own authored `$8,000-15,000` D-Rank band and the same arithmetic the table's E, C and B rows were always derived by. **This was a gap in 13.7.3's table, not in the economy**, and filling it invents nothing. The freelance-average column is untouched and remains a harvest estimate.

## What 1.122 supersedes, and how much of it

**Section 13.7.3's worked example is superseded in part, and the part is its cadence.** Its `1.25x` bid-edge multiplier, its `~14 clears/year` at B-Rank, and the `~9.3%` and `~10.5%` margins that follow are **still correct for a guild whose crews can only reach their own metro** - which was every guild in this world when that example was written, and is still most of them. They do **not** describe a guild holding a zero-latency transport network: that guild's B-Rank crews clear nearer 90 a year than 14, and its margin is several times the figure because the cost side did not move with the reach.

**Everything else in 13.7.3 stands untouched** - the 10% leader's share, the 15% house cut, the 76.5/23.5 split, the salary table, the salaried-support rule that closes the Section 13.2 harvest gap, and the refusal to price A/S clears. The notice is written on 13.7.3 itself, in place, rather than only here.

## What did not change

**No stored field changes shape or meaning.** No checkpoint is rewritten. No XP, level, pool, gold, cash, Stat, skill or holding moves. No Gate already rolled is re-rolled, no break already settled is re-settled, and no clear already paid is re-priced.

**Section 9.1's take rates are unchanged** (E 15 / D 25 / C 55 / B 75 / A 90 per day) and still govern the board and its break arithmetic. **Section 9.2.1's tiers and Section 9.2.2's ramp are unchanged**, and the tier still only ever rises: 13.7.5 can slow the pressure route to nothing and cannot reverse a rung.

**Section 9.1.6's light tick still rolls no Gates.** 13.7.5 reads the band table as a rate, which is what that table is for; it gives no metro a per-Gate record, a tracked posting, a break timer, a district `pressure` value or an agenda advance.

**No new price is authored anywhere.** Section 12.1, 12.3's bands, and 13.7.3's salary table are all read as they stand.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.122** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Regenerate the runtime index** (`tools/generate_runtime_index.ps1`), then confirm with `-Check`.

**3. Validate** with `tools/validate_repository.ps1`.

**4. No branch is promoted by this migration.** Adoption does not declare any office `operational`; that is a campaign fact about named premises, named officers and filed regulator relationships, and it belongs to play. Until a campaign records which of its branches meet 13.7.5's four conditions, the section rolls nothing and the tick's `guild_ops` line reads `none`.

## What is owed and not done here

**The adoption Event is campaign canon and belongs to play.** This record lands the world side only. `gatefall_pendragon_001` owes an adoption Event at its next save, per every prior profile adoption.

**The operational-branch declaration is owed with it.** `gatefall_pendragon_001` holds fourteen offices and fourteen standing tunnels (`EVT-001107`) and a 1,812-member roster, but **which branches satisfy 13.7.5's four conditions is not decided here** - it is a canon write naming officers, cover and regulator filings per city, and it waits for that same save. The worked figures in 13.7.5 (fourteen branches, ~98 clears a day, 48.7% duty) are an illustration of the arithmetic, not a declaration that this campaign's branches are all operational.

**Open, and deliberately not decided here.**

- **A foreign-market factor outside Prague.** Section 12.1's `~70%` is Prague's. This profile authors no factor for Paris, Frankfurt, Amsterdam or any other foreign market, and 13.7.5 says a Runtime needing one takes an owner ruling rather than generalizing Prague's across a continent.
- **Institutional overhead is unpriced.** 13.7.5's revenue figure is gross of leases, the headquarters, liability and hunter insurance, the appraisal desk, legal and per-branch regulator filings - the same basis 13.7.3's own worked example used, stated rather than quietly widened.
- **Rival guild bench depths are not authored.** The section binds Ironline, Horizon, Karlov and the Coalition generically, and their rosters are campaign facts nobody has written down. A Runtime running their dispatch reads whatever the campaign's own ledgers hold.
