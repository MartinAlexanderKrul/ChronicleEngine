## What 1.61 changed

**Flux Sight had no authorization to fire, and no template to fire into. 1.61 gives it both.** Player observation from the two-Crypt session recorded as `F-017`, and the finding on inspection is sharper than the report: the passive being withheld across an entire combat session as though it needed an active check was **the profile's instruction, not a lapse.**

**The authorization hole.** Section 14.3's trigger tiers are the whole authorization list for what the System says unprompted, and Tier 3 forbids firing *"with no declared precondition at all."* Tier 1 named the **designation color**. Tier 2 named the **B-rung item appraisal**. The D rung (monsters — Rank, Health, Mana) and the C rung (awakened humans — the same read) appeared in **no tier**. A Runtime reading the tiers strictly found no authorization to fire them and a prohibition against firing anyway. Naming exactly one rung, and naming it under *bounded discretion*, is also what made the whole passive read as conditional — the one rung any tier mentioned was the one that looked like it needed a precondition to hold.

**The template hole.** Section 4.4 authored *what* Flux Sight reads. Nothing authored *how* it renders. Every other System output in this profile has an exact normative template; this one had none, so it was re-invented at each use. Three shapes from one campaign:

```text
**Flux Sight read on introduction (Section 4.4, C rung — awakened humans, no roll):** **B-Rank, Health 600…**
**Flux Sight** read Owen and both monsters through the fight (…D and C rungs, no roll): elite #2 **200/200**, boss **400/400**
Flux Sight on sight: **[C-Rank boss], 1,000/1,000 HP.**
```

Three formats, the rule citation leaking into the fiction twice, and **Mana dropped in two of three** — against a D rung that authors *"Rank, current/maximum Health, and current/maximum Mana"* as one read. A rung's values are not a menu, and 1.61 says so.

**The read and the designation color are one line now.** They fire at the same instant, on the same subject, at first perception, both without a roll, both Bearer-only — and the color is *computed from the very Rank the read states* (Section 14.6). Two lines reported one perception twice. Where Flux Sight does not cover a creature whose true Rank is resolved, the color still fires alone on its unchanged Tier-1 terms: the color is the floor, the read is the floor plus what the rung adds.

**One grammar, two weights, across every inline notification.** Section 14.5 was three example lines and a sentence; it is now the contract. Every inline message is built from the same parts in the same order — subject, bracketed Rank, delta, `before → current/maximum`, parenthesized source. The Bearer's own pools and XP render **bare**, because they move several times an exchange; anything carrying a subject or more than a number takes **`[SYSTEM]`**. Section 8.2 is restated as an explicit mirror of 14.5 rather than a parallel authority.

**The XP line names its source.** `XP +50 · 40 → 90/300 (elite ×2)` states the award and the arithmetic that produced it, checkable against Section 3.3 without reconstructing the fight. `F-017`'s third item is an entire instant dungeon's XP going unrendered and the running total then resuming from a figure that matched no derivable sum; a total that names no source is unauditable the moment anyone doubts it.

**A target's pools re-render when the Bearer's action changes them.** Full read on first perception; thereafter that subject's pools render only in an exchange his own resolved action moved them, as a bare line carrying the subject. The designation color is fixed for the scene and is not repeated — but a pool is the number the fight is *about*, and leaving it in conversational memory across sixty exchanges is precisely the drift `F-017` records. Rendering the change when it happens is what makes a wrong figure visible while correcting it is still cheap.

**A label vocabulary that was inconsistent with itself.** Through 1.60 the inline contract read `XP:`, `Mana:`, `HP:` — one word spelled out between two abbreviations, in the same three-line block. The pool labels are now **`HP`, `MP`, `XP` on every surface**, matching Section 15.1's bar labels, and they join its fixed-vocabulary list. The 2026-07-30 owner ruling that *"Mana is never abbreviated"* is **scoped, not overturned**: it governs the **skills table's cost column**, which is the surface it was made about and the surface its own next sentence names, and `M<n>` remains forbidden there. A cost is written `Mana 8`; a pool is written `MP 92/98`.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.60 → 1.61.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.61"`.**

**2. Nothing stored changes, and nothing is recomputed.** No pool, maximum, Stat, level, XP, System Rank, Card Rank, skill, skill Rank, mastery level, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `mastery_progress`, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, objective, or candidate is touched. **Flux Sight's own Rank is unchanged** — it is derived from base Perception under Section 4.4 and this edge reaches neither the thresholds nor the clamps.

**3. The rules are prospective and apply from adoption forward.** From the adopting campaign's current anchor, every covered subject entering the Bearer's perception fires the Section 14.5.4 read, and every inline notification renders in the Section 14.5.1 grammar.

**4. Nothing retroactive, and the reason is worth stating.** No promoted Event is rewritten, no past exchange is re-resolved, and no roll, modifier step, damage figure, or margin moves — **a Flux Sight read reveals information and settles nothing.** It grants no modifier step, costs no Mana, and has never been an input to any resolution. Scenes played while the read was withheld remain valid exactly as resolved; what the Bearer might have *chosen* differently had he seen a boss's exact Health is a player decision, not a mechanical outcome, and this profile does not re-litigate one.

**5. Prior renderings are not corrected in place.** Chronicle and Event prose written under the old improvised shapes stays as promoted. A record of what was rendered at the time is not a defect to repair; rewriting it would destroy the evidence `F-017` rests on.

**6. Section 8.2 becomes a mirror.** Where a Runtime finds 8.2 and 14.5 disagreeing on any future edit, **14.5 governs** and 8.2 is corrected to it.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.60-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.61 at readiness.
