## What 1.90 changes

Two independent changes, both prospective. **No stored value moves**, which is why `touched_state_paths` is empty and the classification is compatibility-treatment.

### 1. Section 8.4.2 - the Bearer may be the imperilled person outside a Gate

**The old rule was written whole.** *"The Bearer is never the imperilled person"* excluded him everywhere, on the reasoning that danger to a hunter's own life is the job he signed for. That reasoning is exactly right inside an aperture and has no force at all on a street, where nobody hired him to be there and nothing about the danger is the work.

**The new scoping:**

| Where | Criterion 1 |
|---|---|
| Inside a Gate, a Section 9.6 red gate, a Section 17 instant dungeon | unchanged - the imperilled person must be someone **other than** the Bearer |
| Anywhere else | the **Bearer himself** satisfies criterion 1 |

**Criterion 2 widens for that case only.** Where the imperilled person is the Bearer and the place is not an instance, the qualifying threat is any immediate lethal threat he directly perceives - the old Gate creature and active break, and now also **an awakened human acting to kill him**. It does not widen for a third party and never widens inside an instance.

**Two guards carry the weight, and neither is new text:**

- **A danger the Bearer deliberately created still disqualifies.** Under 1.89 that clause was nearly decorative; under 1.90 it is the whole anti-farm. He cannot open a lethal exchange and collect a milestone for winning it. The test is who initiated the lethal intent, not who struck first.
- **The contract exclusion now reaches him.** Danger on the work he was hired to do is excluded whether the imperilled party is a crewmate or the Bearer.

**Reward is untouched** - the Section 3.4 milestone one Rank above his System Rank at acceptance (1.89's ladder), fixed at acceptance, not repriced by later leveling. At System Rank A that is 3,200 XP.

**What this predictably does to supply.** Section 8.4.6's telemetry recorded twenty-nine dry days before the campaign's first Urgent offer, and named the cause precisely: the Bearer fights alone in private instances, where criterion 1 can never hold. **1.90 does not touch that case** - a solo instant dungeon still produces no Urgent quest, deliberately. What it opens is the ordinary world, which is where this campaign's actual danger has been accumulating.

### 2. New Section 7.6 - Succession

**A personally killed awakened human leaves one transcribable technique.** Signature only; never a Stat Passive, never a class ability; the Bearer's own damage must have crossed the Section 6.5 threshold, and a target incapacitated by pulled damage under 6.2.1 is alive and leaves nothing.

**It enters at the victim's Rank clamped to Section 7.5's ceiling** (System Rank + 1), at **Novice**, counters at zero, Mana cost read from the arriving Rank's ladder, categories at or below the arriving Rank held. Where the Bearer already holds the technique, nothing is taken - no duplicate, no ascension, no credit.

**No number here is invented.** The Rank clamp is Section 7.5's existing ceiling, the entry condition is Section 7.1's existing reset, and the cost ladder is Section 7.2's. What 1.90 adds is a **route**, not a magnitude.

**The design consequence, stated plainly rather than discovered later.** This makes killing awakened humans mechanically rewarding, and it is the first mechanic in this profile that does. That is the owner's intent and it is not hedged here. The counterweight is deliberately **not mechanical**: Sections 19 and 20.4 already author exposure and law, a licensed hunter's death is investigable, and a transcribed technique is itself evidence sitting in the killer's own ledger. A Runtime running 1.90 is never obliged to let the world fail to notice.

## What did not change

**Sections 8.4.1, 8.4.3, 8.4.4, 8.4.5 and 8.4.6 are untouched** - capacity, Hidden quests, settlement order, the promotion ritual, and telemetry all read as they did under 1.89.

**Section 7.1, 7.2, 7.4 and 7.5 are untouched.** Succession is a new subsection beside them and amends none of them; the three-scene evidence route, the mastery ladder, the dividend, and the ascension ceiling are exactly what they were.

**No Rank baseline, rung, category, dividend, XP threshold, price, timer, population, band or distribution moves.**

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.90** on the campaign's live character sheet.

**2. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, item, counter, anchor, quest reward, or skill entry changes. Both rules are prospective from adoption forward: an Urgent quest that did not fire under 1.89 is not retroactively owed, and an awakened killed before adoption transcribes nothing.

**3. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0116 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
