## Profile 1.26: Skill Rank Ascension (`EVT-000158`, no fictional time consumed)

**The defect corrected.** A skill's Rank used to be fixed the moment it was learned, rising only if loot happened to deliver the same technique at a higher Rank, while the shop explicitly refused to sell instruction for a skill Alexander already knew. Against the Section 7.2 Rank baselines (10/25/62/150/375/1000), a whole mastery track is worth only ×1.3–×1.6 while one Rank step is worth ~×2.5 — so learning a skill early was strictly worse than waiting for higher-Rank instruction. **Rupture is the proof:** E-Rank at Master, fourteen lifetime qualifying scenes, dealing **26** on a standard success, against **124** for a C-Rank Rupture learned that same morning by a stranger.

**Two offered routes (Section 7.5).** **Breakthrough** — a skill reaching Master while below the ceiling is offered +1 Rank and settles at **Adept**, keeping three of five mastery levels. **Rune ascension** — consuming instruction teaching a known technique above its current Rank raises it to that Rank and resets mastery to **Novice**. Both present exact before-and-after values on every axis, and neither consumes an item nor settles a change unless accepted; a declined rune stays intact and owned. The earned road is the stronger result at the same Rank (Rupture ×2.30 against ×2.00); the bought road is instant, needs no mastery prerequisite, and can cross several Ranks at once.

**The ceiling.** No skill exceeds **System Rank + 1** — currently **C-Rank**, Alexander being System Rank D. The ceiling binds acquisition as well as ascension: instruction above it does not consume and waits intact until his System Rank rises. This is what removes the incentive to postpone learning.

**The Rank Dominance Law (Section 7.2).** Utility Rank scales are sized so that a skill at Novice one Rank higher is stronger than the same skill at Master one Rank lower — the behaviour damage and healing already had. Reduction skills gain **+25 points** per Rank above native against mastery's +20 across a whole track; passive multipliers **+0.25** against mastery's +0.20; modifier-step skills saturate duration at whole-scene one Rank above native and spend further Ranks on **scope** (additional subjects, allies, patterns, concurrent instances), because whole-scene is mastery's absolute ceiling. The single-skill reduction cap rises **75% → 90%**; at the cap further Rank steps convert to duration. Rank never changes Mana cost.

**Rune economy (Section 12.5).** The catalogue now stocks runes for known skills at Ranks above their current one, up to the ceiling, at the C–S anchors (20,000 / 90,000 / 400,000 / 1,800,000 g). Gold can buy a Rank; it can never buy mastery.
