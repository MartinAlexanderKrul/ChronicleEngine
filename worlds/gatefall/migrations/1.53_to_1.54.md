## What 1.54 changed

**Section 7.5 has stated an ordering since 1.6 and nothing ever checked it.** The law reads `Rank N Master < Rank N+1 Novice (rune) < Rank N+1 Adept (breakthrough)`, and Section 7.2's Rank Dominance Law promises every trade this profile offers is a net gain. Computed at every rung of every skill on both ascension roads, that was **false in two places**.

| Skill | Rank N Master → Rank N+1 Novice |
|---|---:|
| Rupture | ×1.92 |
| Mend, Mana Bolt | ×1.56 |
| Dagger Mastery | ×1.17 falling to ×1.04 |
| Stone Skin, Bulwark | ×1.10 falling to **×1.00 at the 90% cap** |
| **Twin Fang** | **×0.625** |
| **Dimensional Projection** | **×0.25** (range 20 m → 5 m) |

**Twin Fang lost 37.5% of the damage it exists to deal.** Its strikes read weapon power rather than a Rank baseline (Section 7.2), so its whole follow-up multiplier was authored by mastery — and ascension resets mastery. The 1.52 magnitude-axis ladder made the skill *ascension-eligible* by authoring a category for it, without anyone checking whether the trade it now permitted was favourable. **An offer had already been opened to the Bearer on those terms** and described as costing damage, which Section 7.5 does not permit an offer to do. Dimensional Projection carried the identical defect in its range track, unformed and waiting.

At the **90% reduction cap** the stated `<` was also wrong in a milder way: a higher-Rank Novice merely *equals* the lower-Rank Master, and Section 7.2 already routes the gain to a further exchange of duration there.

**Fix 1 — Twin Fang gains a per-Rank base.** Its multiplier becomes `1.00 + 0.35 × ranks_above_native + 0.15 × (mastery_level − 1)`, giving E ×1.00–×1.60, D ×1.35–×1.95, C ×1.70–×2.30, B ×2.05–×2.65, A ×2.40–×3.00. The increment is deliberately modest rather than the ~2.5× a Rank baseline carries: Twin Fang's damage already grows with effective Strength and with every weapon equipped, and multiplying that by a Rank step as well would outrun every skill in this profile. **Its value at native E-Rank Master is ×1.60 — exactly what it was under 1.53.**

**Fix 2 — Section 7.2 gains the magnitude ratchet**, the exact counterpart of the scope ratchet it has carried since 1.31:

```text
magnitude = max( the value authored at the current Rank and mastery level,
                 magnitude_floor )

magnitude_floor := the skill's own authored magnitude immediately before its
                   last ascension (0 for a skill that has never ascended)
```

It is a `tracked_counters` entry written as a `counter_deltas` change on the ascension Event, like `scope_floor`, and binds every magnitude a skill owns — multiplier, reduction fraction, or authored range. Where Rank already dominates the axis it never binds and costs nothing.

**The law is restated as what it actually is.** `Rank N Master ≤ Rank N+1 Novice (rune) < Rank N+1 Adept (breakthrough)`: **no axis ever falls, and at least one thing is gained** — a larger number, a further exchange of duration at the cap, or an authored category. Equality plus a new kind is a real ascension; a smaller number is not.

**A new contract test makes it permanent.** `tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` verifies every reachable rung of every skill on both roads, reads the Rank baseline table and the authored ceiling from the profile rather than restating them, ties each ladder constant back to the sentence that authors it, and catches the further trap the ratchet itself creates: a floor that swallows an entire mastery track, so that climbing Novice to Master at the new Rank buys nothing — the `F-014` defect reappearing on the magnitude axis, and invisible to a fall-detection check because the floor has already hidden it.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.53 → 1.54 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption changes no live number.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.54"`.**

**2. Write a `magnitude_floor` counter for each skill that can bind one.** A skill whose Rank grant on the magnitude axis is **absent or thin** — Twin Fang, Dimensional Projection, Dagger Mastery, Stone Skin, Bulwark — carries a `magnitude_floor` `tracked_counters` entry at its acquiring or baseline Event, with `current_value: 0` for any skill that has never ascended. **This stores a value; it changes none.** A skill whose Rank grant dominates its axis (Rupture, Mend, Mana Bolt) may carry one, and it will never bind.

**3. Re-derive no multiplier.** Twin Fang stands at **native E-Rank**, so `ranks_above_native` is 0 and the new base contributes nothing: its Master multiplier is ×1.60 before and after, its Mana cost 4 before and after, and every resolved strike in the record stands. No other skill's formula changes at all.

**4. Re-price any open ascension offer.** An offer surfaced under 1.53 and still unanswered is restated at 1.54's values before the Bearer answers it, because Section 7.5 entitles him to the exact before-and-after on every axis and the earlier figure was wrong. In the Gatefall Pendragon campaign this is **Twin Fang, E-Rank Master → D-Rank Adept**, re-priced from ×1.30 to **×1.65**. The offer itself is neither withdrawn nor accepted: it remains open and unanswered, and remains the Bearer's to take or decline.

**5. Nothing retroactive.** No past exchange is re-resolved and no strike recomputed. Rules Section 13.2 governs as always.

**6. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, `scope_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.53-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.54 at readiness.
