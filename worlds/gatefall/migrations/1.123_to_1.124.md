## The finding this version exists for

**Two techniques were being used with no rule behind them.** The Bearer coached Elena Cho's control of her own telekinesis in graded sessions with real feedback, and earlier reshaped Elias Kane's Standfast by aiming Overpower at his live field, priced at 400 Mana with a real risk of breaking it for the scene. The first was recorded as an open provisional mechanic owed to Section 7.1's third test (distinctness from the rejected `formation_instruction`, `EVT-000465`); the second was ratified as three standing signature branches under Section 13.6 (`EVT-001079`) but never gave the Bearer a skill that earns the next one. **Neither was a rule, which is Section 20.2's defect: a live mechanic resting in campaign state with no rule behind it.**

## What 1.124 changes

**Section 7.2 gains Wrought Hand** as a ratified earned technique, S-native, and Section 7.4 gains its row in the mastery axis table and its `Temper` ladder in the dividend table.

- **Coaching** is a structured session on an awakened person's own technique; it costs no Mana and gives a Section 13.1 subject fictional control gains and no number.
- **Reshaping** costs **400 Mana**, needs **no consent**, and resolves as **one ordinary uncertain action** with the subject's resistance and Rank read as ordinary signed steps. A success gives the technique a branch under Section 13.6's limits, unchanged: method or reach only, permanent, never a magnitude. A failure breaks the technique for the scene and no longer.
- **Mastery is Mana** (400 / 360 / 320 / 280 / 240) with **one subject at every level**. Section 7.4 clause 3 puts every count of other people on Rank, and an S-native skill has no Rank above native, so the count cannot grow. This corrects a first draft that gave mastery a 1-to-5 student count.
- **`Temper`** is authored with five rungs and no new number.

**Section 13.6** gains one sentence naming Wrought Hand as the Bearer's own way of earning a branch.

## What did not change

**Kane's three Standfast branches stand exactly as ratified** (`EVT-001079`); Section 13.6's limits are unchanged. **No counter is backfilled** from `EVT-000952`, `EVT-001014`, `EVT-001070`, or Elena's lessons (Section 7.1). No XP, level, pool, gold, Stat, item or holding moves. No checkpoint is rewritten.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.124** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Regenerate the runtime index** (`tools/generate_runtime_index.ps1`), then confirm with `-Check`.

**3. Validate** with `tools/validate_repository.ps1`.

**4. A campaign that had coaching or reshaping recorded as a provisional grant closes it against this version**, records its `skills_known` entry at Novice with counters at zero, and moves its candidate to `ratified`. A campaign with neither has nothing to do.

## What is owed and not done here

**The ratification is campaign canon and belongs to play.** This record lands the world side only. `gatefall_pendragon_001` owes the ratification Event, its `skills_known` entry, its counters, the candidate's move to the settled path, and the close of its provisional mechanic; in that campaign they land in the same session.

**Open, and deliberately not decided here.** Reshaping an unwilling subject is unpriced beyond the roll: what a hostile subject does with a branch they did not choose is fiction, not a resolved outcome, and no consequence is authored for it here.
