## What 1.85 adds, and the question that had no rule

**A player asked an artificer to merge two finished items into one, and the profile had nothing to say.**

Section 12.8 authors **commissioning** — a core plus materials plus a fee becomes a new piece. Section 11.5 authors what a finished piece *is*. Section 7.1 authors instruction. Nowhere does any section address **two completed pieces going onto a bench and one coming off**, and the gap is not an oversight of detail: it is a whole trade the world's own premise implies and never wrote down.

**The world already entails it.** Ranked gear is forged from what Gates yield, by awakened whose signature ability works mana into matter (Section 12.8). A trade that can put a mana line *into* matter can take one *out* of it, and an artificer good enough to read another artificer's mid-process corrections — which this campaign has established happens — is an artificer who can see where two seams would have to meet. Nothing in Section 2, Section 11, or Section 12 forbids it. It simply had never been asked for.

**What was not permissible was inventing it at the table.** Section 20.2 and the Resident Core draw the line in the same place: *a fact about the world* is the Runtime's to author, and *a mechanical value a later resolution reads* is not. Fusion is entirely the second kind — an eligibility test, a cost, a resolution table, and a permanent change to an item line. So the scene stopped, the artificer said plainly that she did not know what it would cost or what would come off the bench, and the mechanic came here.

## The rule

> **Fusion unmakes two finished pieces and re-seats them as one. It moves an effect; it never moves a magnitude.**

That second sentence is the whole of the balance, and it is load-bearing rather than decorative.

**Why it has to be there.** This campaign's binding constraint is worn physical reduction: Combat Tier S needs ≈60% against 46.2% held, and the Ceiling Register (`EVT-000592`) established that two worn pieces cannot be improved by anyone and three can. A fusion rule that let two armor pieces pool their reduction into one slot would dissolve that constraint in an afternoon, retire an authored ceiling the campaign had just spent a scene discovering, and do it without a single Rank being climbed. **Fusion must not be a Rank ladder wearing a different hat.** It buys a *slot*, and the slot is the entire prize.

### 12.10 Fusion — Two Finished Pieces, One Chassis

*Landed as Section 12.10 of the active profile, after 12.9. It authors no template and adds no row to any panel other than the item line described at the end. The text below is the reasoning; the section itself is authoritative.*

**The fourth trade.** Identification reads a thing. Commissioning makes one from raw material. Teaching is a third thing and is billed as one. **Fusion is the fourth, and the rarest** — it is the only artificer work whose ordinary failure destroys the material it was handed. Most licensed artificers have never performed one and will say so; an artificer who quotes a fusion confidently without having done one is worth less trust than one who does not.

**Eligibility.** All of the following are checked before any cost is paid:

1. Both pieces occupy the **same equipment slot class** — two accessories, two armor pieces of the same slot, or two weapons of the same chassis (Section 11.5).
2. Both are **functional**: Excellent, Good, Worn, or Damaged. Broken and Destroyed cannot be fused.
3. Neither is a **named artifact** (Section 11.6), a **class-bound** item, or **instruction** of any binding (Section 7.1).
4. Neither is **equipped**; fusion takes both out of play for its duration.
5. The two pieces stand **within one Rank of each other**. A gap of two Ranks or more cannot be bridged.
6. **Neither piece has been fused before.** A fused piece is never again eligible as frame or donor.

**A piece at its Rank ceiling is a legal frame.** Fusion does not travel the Rank axis, so a Flux Sight reading of `REACHED` does not bind it — the two questions are unrelated, and a piece that can never be improved can still be the one that survives.

**The frame and the donor.** The commissioner nominates one piece the **frame** and one the **donor** before work begins. **The nomination is irreversible.**

- The **frame** survives. The result carries the frame's Rank, chassis, slot, stat line, weapon power or protection, and combat line **unchanged**.
- The **donor** is **consumed whole, at every outcome including every failure.**

**What the result gains.** On a successful fusion the result carries, in addition to the frame's complete line, **one named effect of the donor's**, chosen by the commissioner at nomination.

It gains **no stat points, no weapon power, and no physical reduction.** A donor whose whole value is a stat line has nothing to give and cannot be a donor; a donor with no named effect cannot be one either. Where the carried effect is the same effect the frame already has, the two run as **independent instances** under Section 11.5's duplicate-accessory rule and compose multiplicatively under Section 7.3 — which is what the frame's two-vambrace case already establishes, and no stack ever reaches immunity.

**Cost.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Material** | the donor, consumed; plus **one beast core at the result's Rank, or four at the Rank below** |
| **Fee** | the Section 12.8 commissioning anchor — about the **market price of a same-Rank core** (Section 12.1). A named artificer's work trades above every anchor and the exact offer is authored for that transaction, never rolled. |
| **Bench time** | **one day** at C-Rank and below · **two** at B · **three** at A · **five** at S. Fusion is never same-day work. |

**Resolution.** One d100 by the artificer, read against the engine's standard bands (Rules Section 4.6). Steps come from established circumstances only (Section 4.3), never from balancing:

- **+1** — the two pieces are identical models at identical Rank; the seams already agree.
- **+1** — the commissioner assists with demonstrated infusion or diagnostic capability established in play.
- **−1** — the artificer has never completed a fusion.
- **−1** — either piece is unprovenanced or was never fully read.
- **−1 per Rank** — the artificer's established working Rank stands below the result's.

| Effective | Band | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **01–05** | catastrophic | **Both pieces destroyed.** Core spent, fee owed. |
| **06–20** | major failure | Donor destroyed; the frame **drops one condition grade** — a frame already at Damaged becomes **Broken**. Core spent. |
| **21–40** | failure | Donor destroyed. Frame returned **intact and unchanged**. Core spent. |
| **41–60** | partial | Fusion holds, the carried effect **diminished**: once per *scene* where it read once per exchange, or **half** its stated magnitude where it states one. |
| **61–80** | success | The carried effect operates at **full stated strength**. |
| **81–95** | strong | As success, and the result's condition rises **one grade**, to a maximum of Excellent. |
| **96–99** | exceptional | As strong, and **the core is not consumed** — it comes off the bench unspent. |
| **100** | legendary | As exceptional, and the result carries **every** named effect the donor held, not only the nominated one. |

**The critical tails stay live** (Rules Section 4.6). No modifier removes the 01–05, which is the whole reason a careful artificer states the risk aloud before taking the commission.

**Provenance.** A System-shop piece has no licensed provenance (Section 12.8.1), and handing one to a world artificer to be unmade is a Section 19 exposure thread like any other — the artificer holds a piece with no market record, in pieces, on their own bench.

**Rendering.** A fused piece renders its own line under `/system gear` with the carried effect on a continuation row, tagged `fused`. It is one item in one slot and occupies one slot; nothing about the panel's geometry changes.

## What did not change

**No Rank ladder, baseline, rung, category, dividend, XP threshold, price, timer, population, band or distribution moves.** Section 7 is untouched end to end. Section 11.5's stat budgets and reduction table are untouched. Section 12.5's shop, prices, rotation and surcharge are untouched. Section 12.8's commissioning is untouched — fusion borrows its fee anchor and changes nothing about it.

**Worn physical reduction is untouched, deliberately and by design.** No fusion adds a percentage point of reduction to anything, so the Combat Tier S threshold and the Ceiling Register's finding both stand exactly as `EVT-000592` left them.

**Section 15 authors no new panel and no new template.** The item line already carries named effects on continuation rows; a fused effect is one of those.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.85"`.**

**2. Nothing else moves.** No level, XP, Stat, Rank, mastery level, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `rank_ascensions`, `dividend_level`, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, item line, gold, cash, quest, class, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

**3. No item is retroactively fused, and no past Event, roll, damage figure, kill, clear or outcome is altered.** Fusion is available from adoption forward and reaches nothing behind it.

**4. No holding becomes ineligible.** Every piece in every inventory is unfused at adoption and therefore a legal frame or donor on its own terms.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor. Immutable Profile 1.84-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.85 at readiness.

## What this does not close

**It does not author fusion across slot classes.** A coif and a vambrace remain two different questions and this section answers neither.

**It does not author unfusing.** A fused piece is finished in the fusion sense, permanently, and nothing here provides a route back.

**It does not price a named artificer's fusion.** Section 12.8's rule governs — the offer is authored for the transaction, and an artificer who has never done one may quote whatever their own uncertainty is worth to them.

**It does not establish any artificer's working Rank.** The `−1 per Rank below the result` step reads an established value; where a campaign has not established one, the step does not apply and the omission is not a defect.
