## What 1.86 changes, and the ruling nobody had taken

**A sentence in the economy section was doing work nobody had authorised it to do.** Section 12.8 closes with *"There is no player crafting subsystem: the Bearer commissions or buys like every other hunter — the forge is someone else's signature."* It was written to say the obvious thing about **gear supply**: there is no recipe menu, no crafting minigame, and the Bearer does not manufacture his own equipment.

It was read as something much larger — that a Bearer may never do artificer work of any kind — and on that reading `EVT-000485`'s `progression-batch-settlement` closed the infusion formation candidate as `none`. **No owner ruling was taken and none is cited.** The entry then added that it was *"recorded explicitly so a later session does not re-litigate whether repetition alone might eventually open a candidate it structurally cannot"* — a Runtime conclusion granting itself the standing of a ruling and then sealing itself against the review that standing requires. Four days later a session read it back to the player as settled canon. **The player asked who had ruled it. Nobody had.** That defect is recorded independently as `F-039`; this migration is the substance.

**The profile's own text was already against the wide reading.** Section 13.6:

> *"The Bearer is the sole exception to all of it: he manifested nothing at awakening... and everything he gains, the System governs. In a world where every hunter is born as exactly one power, the man who can **accumulate** them is a walking impossibility."*

And the precedent for the exact shape needed was already authored, one paragraph down:

> *"Learned **Mend** grants its heal but not the full innate-mender package of three field touches and one post-fight injury-tier reduction."*

Menders and artificers are named as parallel professions in Section 12.8's own sentence — *"rare on the order of menders and paid on the same logic."* A learned technique delivering a profession's **function** without its **innate package** is therefore not a new mechanism; it is the mechanism this profile already runs for healing, applied to the other rare trade.

## The rule

> **The signature originates; the hands may still work.**

**Seamwork** is authored at Section 7.3 as an earned technique (Section 7.1 route 3), native **C-Rank**, **Mana 3**, on a four-rung ladder: **Read and open · Seat · Lift · Raise.** The Rank ladder sells what he can reach; **mastery sells `Hand` — the cost of being wrong**: at Novice a failed working spends its material and closes the piece to him for the scene, and at Master a failure spends nothing and returns why it failed. **Mastery deliberately does not sell depth of read**, because the native rung already grants the maker's corrections and no mastery level may withhold what a Rank rung has granted.

**It is native at C-Rank because Section 18.3.1 forbids a grant that returns nothing the Bearer already lacks.** An earned technique is authored from the evidence that earned it, and Section 7.1 puts **Rank** among the things ratification authors rather than assumes. The qualifying evidence shows a Bearer reading another maker's mid-process corrections unprompted and opening a finished working without damaging the piece — so an E-Rank entry rung reading *detect that a working is present* would author **two Ranks of nothing** beneath capability already on the record. Reading, reading the hand, and opening are folded into the native rung where the evidence puts them.

**The boundary is origination, and only origination.** Seamwork **never creates an item.** Matter carrying no mana line — a bar of steel, a hide, an unworked blank — is **inert to it**, and the Mana is refused before it is spent. Everything else it does, it does to an object the Bearer already owns and that already carries a working: read it, open it, seat material into it, lift a working out of it, carry it toward what it could become.

**It does not forbid improvement, and an earlier draft of this record got that backwards.** That draft read *"cannot create something from nothing"* as *"cannot add anything"* and authored a ladder whose rungs changed nothing about a piece — an over-tight rule that passes every gate exactly as quietly as a loose one, because nothing in a test suite reports a rule that is merely useless. It is recorded rather than silently replaced, because the failure mode is worth naming: **the restrictive misreading of an owner's ruling is still a misreading.**

**Three bounds keep Section 12.8's economy intact, and none of them is new.**

- **An item's authored ceiling binds absolutely.** Seamwork carries a piece toward its ceiling and never past it. A piece already at its ceiling cannot be raised by him or by anyone; a [B-Rank] piece that can reach [A] reaches [A] and stops. **The ceiling is why he still shops** — a higher ceiling is a higher-Rank object, and those are bought, commissioned, or killed for.
- **Material is spent at this profile's own anchor.** Adding to a piece consumes crystals or a core exactly as commissioning does, and cores remain artificer material rather than shop goods.
- **His own Seamwork Rank gates the ladder**, so a piece of a Rank he cannot yet read is a piece he cannot yet work.

**Every item he will ever own is still acquired, never made.** Shop, commission and loot are untouched as the only routes to equipment. What changes is that a piece already in his hands stops being finished business the moment he acquires it.

**Section 12.10's assist step now reads the skill.** The `+1` for a commissioner assisting a fusion previously read "demonstrated infusion or diagnostic capability established in play," which is an impression. It now requires **Seamwork at C-Rank or above** — the rung at which a working can actually be opened under him — and Section 4.4's no-double-counting clause is cited so one assistance cannot be described twice into two steps.

**What Seamwork still does not confer.** He is not a licensed artificer. He takes no commissions, holds none of the trade's standing or reach, and cannot originate a piece. Section 12.10's fusion still resolves on the artificer performing it, with their own `−1` for never having completed one — **assisting is not performing.**

## What did not change

**No Rank ladder, baseline, rung, category, dividend, XP threshold, price, timer, population, band or distribution outside the new skill moves.** Section 12.10's eligibility, costs, bands and outcomes are untouched apart from the assist step's grounding. Section 12.5's shop, prices, rotation and surcharge are untouched. Section 12.8's commissioning economics — core supplied, fee at the same-Rank core anchor, B-Rank-and-above as guild work — are untouched. Section 13.5's three authored support effects and Section 13.6's signature rules are untouched; 1.86 applies the Mend precedent rather than amending it.

**No existing skill gains or loses anything**, and Section 7.4's sixteen existing axis rows are unchanged. Seamwork adds a seventeenth.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.86"`.**

**2. No skill is granted by migrating.** Seamwork enters a Bearer's ledger only through Section 7.1's ordinary ratification, on evidence, with an owner ruling — never by adoption. A campaign adopting 1.86 holds exactly the skills it held under 1.85.

**3. No stored value moves.** No level, XP, Stat, Rank, mastery level, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `rank_ascensions`, `dividend_level`, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, item line, gold, cash, quest, class, tracked posting, district pressure, supply source or standing need value is touched.

**4. No past Event, roll, damage figure, kill, clear or outcome is altered**, including `EVT-000485`. Its audit stands as the historical record of what was decided and by whom; it is superseded on the merits rather than rewritten, and `F-039` carries the procedural finding.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor. Immutable Profile 1.85-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.86 at readiness.

## What this does not close

**It does not ratify anything.** The adopting campaign's infusion candidate was closed as `none` by `EVT-000485` and this profile does not reopen it — Section 7.1 reopens a closed candidate **only by owner ruling**, and that ruling belongs in play, with the candidate re-formed on its evidence and Seamwork ratified through the mandatory adjudication gate like any other technique.

**It does not author what a fusion attempted by a non-artificer does**, because Section 12.10 does not admit one. If that question is ever asked, it is a new ruling and not an inference from this one.

**It does not author the exact magnitude a Seat or a Raise delivers.** The ladder authors what the Bearer can *reach* and the ceiling authors where he must *stop*; what one working moves a piece by, on what die and at what material cost, is resolved at the Section 12.10 step vocabulary and settled per attempt. A profile-level table for it is owed if the operation becomes routine.

**It does not resolve `F-039`.** The structural defect — a Runtime audit reaching ruling authority by assertion and sealing itself against review — is untouched by fixing the one conclusion it produced.
