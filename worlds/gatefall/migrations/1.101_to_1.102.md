## What 1.102 changes

**Raised as `F-052`, from an out-of-character question at session open.** The Bearer stands at level 102 with **both** class trials open and unentered - the level-50 Threshold Trial (Section 18.8) and the level-100 Gate Walker trial (Section 18.13) - and the player asked what the difference between them is. The profile could not answer, and the attempt surfaced three separate gaps in the same seam of Section 18.

### 1. Two of eight evolution hooks named no unlock condition

Six classes state a deed in the same clause as the grant. **Riftwalker and Echo Knight stated none at all**, so the campaign carried a trial recorded as *unlocked and unentered* for fifty-two levels with nothing ever establishing what entering it would involve. A Runtime asked to open it would have had to invent the trial's premise - precisely the class of invention Section 18.13.2 already stops for on the terminal lines, one rung further out.

**Both now carry a condition, and the shape is read off the class rather than invented for symmetry.** Section 18.3.4 authors the two shapes the eight hooks actually use:

- **A taking** - something removed from a boss and kept. Available only where the class's premise *is* acquisition from a defeated thing: Shadowbinder, Soulforged Armory, Devourer, Reliquary.
- **A deed** - the class's own premise demonstrated at its limit, no object taken. Herald and Seam already used this shape; Riftwalker and Echo Knight join them, **because there is no boss-part for a portal or an echo.**

**Riftwalker - the Threshold Trial:** *answer a disturbance at a set anchor from another ledgered container, arriving in the exchange it reached him.* This proves the standing anchor sense (1.73), the anchored step and the flat any-distance cost (1.97-1.98) as **one apparatus rather than three grants**, with the distance that should have made him too far away doing nothing at all.

**Echo Knight - the Chorus Trial:** *stand a scene in which a full arsenal of echoes is deployed and every one of them disperses on interception,* none expiring unspent. This proves the Endurance grant as Section 18.7 states it - not a larger pool but attacks that do not arrive - in the scene where every one of them was stopped by a copy of him. It binds to `slot cap = effective Perception / 20`, so the condition scales with the build rather than sitting at a flat number.

### 2. New Section 18.3.4 - the ladder itself

The hooks were authored one per class section and the rules governing all eight were never stated in one place, which is how two came to be conditionless and how the 50-to-100 relationship came to be unwritten. Section 18.3.4 states them once:

- **The two condition shapes**, and why a class takes one rather than the other.
- **A satisfied condition unlocks a trial and never enters one.** The trial is offered on Section 18.1's terms - once, never expiring, never re-offered - and **a Runtime never prompts him into it**, never treats it as a pending readiness obligation, and never narrates the System pressing him toward it. **A trial open for fifty levels is a normal state and not a defect in the record.** This is the answer to the fourth question `F-052` raised, which asked what happens to the other six hooks when a named deed is satisfied but the trial is never entered: nothing happens, and nothing is supposed to.
- **An evolution trial is Section 18.2's structure at Combat Tier, snapshotted at entry** - reusing the class quest's machinery rather than authoring a second kind of trial, and sized at entry for the reason 18.2.2 already gives: preparation raises the trial rather than outgrowing it. Its phases test the class's own axis rather than the five signals, which have already done their work.

### 3. The level-50 to level-100 relationship - owner ruling

**The evolution is not a prerequisite for the terminal trial, and the terminal trial carries it.** An unevolved Bearer's Section 18.13 trial runs **six phases instead of five**; the additional phase is his own evolution's examination, run first, before the class suppression the terminal trigger imposes on the middle phase, inside the same single budget. **Passing grants both lines in one settlement; failing re-opens both** at the next decade on 18.13's existing terms.

**Why subsume rather than gate.** Every terminal line is authored as strictly the further rung of its class's own axis, and Section 18.13 says so outright - *"the same shape as the Section 18.3 evolution, one rung further out."* Gate Walker's permanent public tunnel **is** the personal gate one rung out. A Bearer holding the terminal line while missing the evolution beneath it would hold a superset without its subset, which is incoherent on the profile's own account of the two rungs.

**Why it is not free.** A rung skipped is not a rung waived. The sixth phase is the evolution's examination actually run, under one budget with no refill, inside a trial already carrying three difficulties the level-50 version never had.

**A hard prerequisite was considered and rejected, and the reason is the live case.** Requiring the Threshold Trial first is the cleaner ladder on paper and it strands the only Bearer this profile has: sent back past the level-100 trigger to enter a level-50 trial that Section 18.2.2 would size at his **current** Combat Tier anyway, making it a second terminal-scale fight wearing an earlier name. The six-phase form collects the same cost in one trial instead of two.

### 4. Two stale cross-references from the 1.100 split

Sections 18.3 and 18.3.1 both pointed the class ladder at **Section 18.12** and called the level-100 rung a **subclass**. Under 1.100, 18.12 is *what the world does about him* and opens by stating it grants the Bearer nothing; **18.13** is the class line, and it authors a *terminal line* rather than a subclass. Both pointers now read 18.13, both carry the 18.3.4 reference, and the 18.3 pointer records what it previously said so the repair is legible rather than silent. Low-stakes individually - but it is the sentence a Runtime lands on when tracing the class ladder forward, and it routed to the section that grants nothing.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value, XP threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, price, skill, title, or class grant moves.** Every class's acquisition cap, magnitude ladder, Mana cost and evolution **grant** is byte-identical to 1.101; only the **unlock condition** on two hooks is added, and no hook's grant is touched. Section 18.2's trial structure, the five signals, 18.3's offer machinery, and all eight class sections' mechanics are unchanged. Section 18.13's grants, far-door rules and pricing are unchanged - only its Trigger gains the six-phase paragraph, which states the Section 18.3.4 rule rather than adding a second one.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.102** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. No stored state changes.** No class field, trial record, or progression counter is read or written by this migration. A campaign holding a class carries the same class with the same grants.

**3. No trial is retroactively entered, unlocked, or closed, and no trial state is written.** The condition is a statement about the record, tested against it at the moment the question is asked - not an Event that must fire after adoption. A Bearer whose record already satisfies his hook's condition holds the trial unlocked from adoption forward, exactly as the six conditioned hooks have always worked; a Bearer whose record does not holds it locked until it does.

**The live campaign's case, stated exactly.** `100_CHARACTER_SHEET.md` carries `class_quest` (completed, Riftwalker taken at `EVT-000519`) and a `riftwalker` block holding rungs, anchors and rulings. **It carries no field for the evolution trial at all** - not a status, not an unlock flag, not an entry record. So nothing in stored state flips from unlocked to locked here, because stored state never said either. What changes is what the **profile** says: through 1.101 the hook named no condition, so a Runtime reading it would have found the trial available on reaching level 50 and nothing else; from 1.102 it is available when the deed is in the record.

**Tested against the record, the deed is not there.** Anchors are set and the anchor sense has fired, but no resolved Event has him answering a disturbance at an anchor **from another ledgered container in the exchange it reached him**. **The Threshold Trial is therefore locked at adoption.** This is a real narrowing of what the campaign may do next and it is recorded as one rather than smoothed over - it follows directly from authoring a condition where none existed, which is the point of the change. It is not treated as a loss: the deed is ordinary Riftwalker play, needs no permission and no build change, and is reachable whenever he chooses to do it.

**4. The level-100 trial is unaffected by step 3.** It is enterable regardless of the evolution's state - that is the whole of the 18.3.4 ruling - and its six-phase form applies from adoption to any Bearer who has not cleared his evolution, which currently means the live Bearer. **A locked Threshold Trial does not block Gate Walker**, and this is deliberate: it is exactly the case the hard-prerequisite reading would have stranded.

**Left open, and owed to a play session rather than to this migration.** The campaign has **nowhere to record an evolution trial's state** - no `evolution` block beside `class_quest`, no unlock or entry field. Through 1.101 that was invisible because the trial had no condition and therefore no state worth holding; with a condition authored, whether the deed has been done is now a real question the sheet cannot answer, and today it is answerable only by re-reading the chronicle. Authoring that block is a campaign-side change under save discipline and is not done here.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs step 1 only.** There is no state to recompute.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Every checkpoint captured under 1.101 or earlier keeps what it was captured under.
