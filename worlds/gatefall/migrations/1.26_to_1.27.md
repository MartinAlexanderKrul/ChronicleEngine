## What 1.27 changed

Version 1.27 remains the **additive concealed-canon supply advance** over frozen 1.26, correcting a design defect that had produced zero Hidden quests across the whole of the Gatefall: Pendragon campaign — fourteen in-fiction days of play, and every day Section 8.4 has existed. Section 8.4.3 makes a Hidden pointer eligible only when a concealed discovery *already exists in authored canon* carrying a recorded reveal condition — a correct filter with no source behind it. Gatefall authored many secrets in `220_NOTABLE_FIGURES.md`, `210_PLACES.md`, and `260_SEED_EVENTS.md`, but none carried a reveal condition, an objective, or any statement of what physical proximity to it meant, so none could satisfy the section's fourth criterion; and the resident trigger audit correctly refuses to invent one. Supply was empty by construction, and every audit that ran was right to fire nothing. New `230_KNOWLEDGE_SUBJECTS.md` is the supply: ten world-scope `concealed-discovery` records translating already-authored secrets into the exact shape Section 8.4.3 consumes, sited where an E- or D-Rank hunter working ordinary contracts can reach them. New Section 8.4.5 authors the field contract those records must carry, forbids a stored reward, holds world records immutable against play, and authors a **promotion ritual** letting a Runtime write an unresolved thread the fiction already produced into eligible concealed canon — bound by time separation (a record authored and attached in the same exchange is manufacturing and stays prohibited), grounding, and completeness before attachment. Section 8.4.3's criterion 1 now names the ledger. **No stored field, magnitude, cost, counter, probability, owned item, completed transaction, or resolved outcome changes**, and Section 8.4.3's four criteria are untouched. Data Model 0.1.5 remains unchanged.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.26 → 1.27 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration: no skill, item, pool, counter, quest, currency balance, or resolved roll changes, and no past scene is reopened or reinterpreted. **No Hidden pointer attaches retroactively.** Section 8.4.4 already forbids a quest completing from conduct predating its attachment, and the fourteen in-fiction days played before adoption are not re-audited against the new ledger — the supply applies prospectively, from the next scene opened after adoption. `non_daily_quests` remains structurally unchanged and its `active` and `pending_offers` lists are not seeded. Record the additive-upgrade acknowledgement in mutable live state; immutable Profile 1.26-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the applicable compatibility chain through 1.27 at readiness.
