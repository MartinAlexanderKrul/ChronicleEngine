## Compatibility treatment

**1.35 → 1.36 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration: **no Stat Passive is held by the new clamp today**, because System Rank D already caps every one of them at C-Rank under the pre-existing ceiling and C is authored for all five. Alexander's base Stats are Strength 33, Agility 32, Vitality 32, Perception 38, Intelligence 36, deriving Flux Sight and Multitask at D-Rank and Overpower, Pre-empt and Shrug Off at E-Rank — identical before and after. The clamp becomes load-bearing only when System Rank reaches C and a governing Stat reaches 54, which is the first moment either could have gone wrong; reaching it now rather than then is deliberate, since a Rank already rendered cannot be un-rendered without contradicting what the Bearer was shown. No `/system` row changes. Record adoption as `EVT-000191`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.35-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.36 at readiness.
