# Alexander's Agenda - Gatefall: Pendragon

**Non-canonical.** A derived view: every dated commitment, deadline and meeting Alexander is holding, read from `180_CURRENT_STATE.md` (Immediate obligations) and `140_OBJECTIVES.md`. Canon is those ledgers; this file only feeds the index page's agenda. It is never loaded at runtime.

## Save Procedure

At every `/save`, before regenerating the ledgers:

1. Remove every row that is done or has passed at the save's campaign time. A deadline missed but still open stays, with *overdue* in its event.
2. Add every new dated commitment the span made, from `180`'s Immediate obligations and any objective's status line.
3. **Chicago** is the time in Chicago (24-hour `HH:MM`). Prefix `~` for an approximate time; leave it empty for "sometime that day". **Zone** is the IANA zone of where it happens (`America/Chicago`, `America/Detroit`, `Europe/London`, `Asia/Seoul` ...); the page shows the local time from it.
4. Keep the rows in date and time order.
