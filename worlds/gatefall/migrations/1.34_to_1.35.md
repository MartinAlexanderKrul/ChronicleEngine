## What 1.35 changed

Version 1.35 remains the **additive correction advance** over frozen 1.34, closing the last hole in the Section 7.2 Rank Dominance Law. Section 7.2 grants a modifier-step or stated-capability skill three things per Rank above native — whole-scene duration at the first step, one further affected subject, and one authored category — but on a skill whose effect is instantaneous and carries no scope count the first two are inert and the third existed for no skill outside Section 7.3's scope table. **Sprint and Flash Step could therefore ascend for nothing**, spending mastery levels under a law promising the trade is always a gain, and Section 7.5 asserted that promise in three separate places: its net-gain sentence, its `Rank N` ordering, and its rule making every Master-level skill below the ceiling ascension-ready. A skill is now **ascension-eligible** only where the target Rank's grant is authored and non-empty; where it is not, Section 7.5 withholds the offer and states the withholding rather than presenting a Rank step with nothing behind it. Section 7.3 additionally authors the reachable capability rungs — Sprint's **broken ground** at D and **vertical** at C, Flash Step's **through the barrier** at C — so both skills become eligible rather than merely protected. **No stored field, magnitude, cost, counter, probability, owned item, completed transaction, or resolved outcome changes.** Data Model 0.1.5 remains unchanged.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.34 → 1.35 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration: every skill's Rank, mastery level, counters, and rendered effect are identical before and after. **Sprint stands at native E-Rank and Flash Step at native D-Rank**, so no capability rung is live and none is retroactive — the ladders are prospective in full. **No ascension offer is reopened, withdrawn, or issued retroactively**, and none is outstanding at adoption. Flash Step stands at Expert, one mastery level below the Master that would have made it ascension-ready; this adoption reaches it before that settlement rather than after, which is the only order in which it could be reached at all, since Rules Section 13.2 forbids reopening what a settlement has spent. Record adoption as `EVT-000190`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.34-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.35 at readiness.
