## What 1.106 changes

**Found by play walking off the edge of the simulated world.** The campaign's Bearer reached carded S-Rank, signed a **BGM National** standing incident-response agreement covering geography Chicago's tick does not model, and committed to a multi-city chartered tour whose whole purpose is expanding his Section 18.8 personal-teleport reach into cities the world has never simulated. Raised as `F-056`.

Section 9.1's world clock is authored against **Chicago**, with **Prague** carrying a parallel instance as the world's other seeded city. Everywhere else on Earth had **no tick at all**: no Gate flow, no break clock, no posting board, no response chain, no market factor - nothing a Runtime could read when play reached there. Section 9.1's own justification applies one level up: an un-run world is a readiness finding rather than something nobody notices, and this is not a city whose tick went stale but cities that were never given one.

**The gap was wider than the tick, and that is why the fix is not "author more cities."** Everything downstream of Section 9.1 was keyed to the same two names - Section 9.8's break-response chain, Section 9.10's board depth, Section 9.11's two regulator call lists, Section 12's USD/CZK market factors, and Section 9.1.3's district pressure, which only Chicago's and Prague's districts can feed. A third city authored the way Prague was authored owes every one of them.

### The design that was rejected first

**Running Section 9.1's ten steps for every metro the campaign touches.** At the roster below that is a hundred-odd Gates a day, each with a siting roll, a contest roll, a break timer and a board line, almost all of it resolving as *the trade took the job and cleared it off-screen* - which Chicago already demonstrates under step 4, in numbers. It is bookkeeping no table survives, and it buys nothing: the Bearer never sees a D-Rank posting in Phoenix.

**What the wider map actually owes is the three things that reach a national asset**, and 1.106 authors those and refuses the rest.

### 1. Metro flow is derived, not authored

Chicago carries **~1,200 licensed hunters against 5.5 Gates a day**; Prague carries **~350 against 1.5** (`200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 3, Section 9.1). That is **0.0046** and **0.0043** Gates per licensed hunter per day - within 8% of each other, on two cities authored a month apart and never written to agree. The band table is that ratio:

| Band | Licensed hunters | Gates/day at tier III | Share of Band I | Worked example |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| I - Primary | >= 1,000 | `1d4+3` (~5.5) | 100% | **Chicago** |
| II - Major | 400-999 | `1d4` (~2.5) | 45% | - |
| III - Secondary | 150-399 | `1d2` (~1.5) | 27% | **Prague** |
| IV - Minor | < 150 | `1d2-1` (~0.5) | 9% | - |

**Neither authored row moves.** Prague's `1d2` *is* 27% of Chicago's `1d4+3`, which is where the share column came from. The bands ramp with Section 9.2.1's tier automatically and are never restated.

**The roster holds twenty-six named metros in four bands, and Band I holds exactly five** because `210_PLACES.md` calls Chicago *"a top-five US Gate city in a country that measures cities that way"*. It checks out against the country's own totals two independent ways: the roster's named metros plus a Band IV remainder give **~116 Gates a day in the United States**, and 25,000 licensed hunters at the ratio above give **115**. The **BGM's ten numbered regions** are authored here, with Region V remaining Chicago's as both base texts already have it.

**No Place or Settlement/Kingdom entity is allocated by the roster** - the same footing as `210_PLACES.md`'s own Global section. These are names with a band, not authored places.

### 2. The light tick rolls three kinds and nothing else

New **Section 9.1.6**, dispatched from a new **step 11** on the Section 9.1 tick. One d100 a day for the whole wider map:

| Major event | Wider-map traffic/day | Escalates when | Rate | Stream/day |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| S-Rank Gate | 0.55 | nothing carded S in range inside its two-day timer | 1 in 5 | 0.11 |
| Red gate | 2.64 | its true Rank is A or above | 3%, the A+S tail | 0.08 |
| Break already loose | 0.44 | the Gate is C-Rank or above | 25%, the C-and-above tail | 0.11 |
| | | | **Total** | **~0.30** |

Every escalation rate is read off **Section 9.2.1's own Rank distribution**, not chosen. Two of Section 9.6's four anomaly results - a population one Rank above the assessment, and a doubled archetype twist - **never reach this table at all**, because they are what a regional strike team exists for.

The event is then **sited against cover rather than with flow** (an escalation is least likely where the benches are deepest), including an `89-00` off-the-roster band that is authored rather than a leftover: `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 4 states that the map of who dies to Gates tracks the map of who was already poor, and that is a domestic fact before it is an international one.

### 3. Section 9.11 gains a national issuing authority, and an anchor is worth -20

Through 1.105 the directed-assignment call list had exactly **two** issuing authorities, BGM Region V and the CGA. A National agreement had no channel to arrive through. **BGM National** is now authored above both: a regional office calls inside its own region, National calls across regional lines when no region can answer in time, and a hunter on a regional list only is never called out of his region however capable he is.

Reach rolls Section 9.11 **unchanged** - same bands, same three refusal tiers including the draft - with two wider-map modifiers added. The load-bearing one is **-20 for a standing Section 18.8 anchor in the sited metro**: he is inside every response window by construction. **This is what makes an anchor tour mechanical rather than flavour**, and it is the exact thing the campaign that raised the flag was already flying around the country to buy.

### 4. The two smaller closures

**Section 9.8** gains the rest-of-country response chain: the metro's BGM regional office issues the emergency contract and the nearest guild strike team is dispatched, on Chicago's pattern, with BGM National escalating across regional lines where the release outruns the region.

**Section 12 is explicitly unmodified inside the United States.** One federal auction, one commodities exchange, and two licensed refiners set one price, so a core sells in Seattle for what it sells for in Chicago. **Prague's ~70% is a foreign-market factor** and does not generalize to Boston - which is what an unwary Runtime would otherwise have reached for.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value or threshold, damage formula, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, shop price, skill, title, class grant, cap, or Mana cost moves.** Sections 9.2.1 and 9.2.2 are byte-identical; the light tick reads the tier and **feeds the ramp nothing**, because Section 9.2.2's credit accrues from districts the campaign actually tracks and a break in a metro nobody is simulating may not advance the world's tier.

**Steps 1-9 of the Section 9.1 tick are untouched and never leave Chicago and Prague.** No Gate is rolled anywhere off the two simulated cities: no per-Gate record, no tracked posting, no break timer, no contest roll, no district `pressure` value, and no agenda advance exists on the wider map.

**Section 6.6 holds in full.** An A-Rank escalation is an A-Rank Gate, and Section 9.11's own fences - creates no Gate, sizes nothing to the Bearer, is never a solo clear - apply here unchanged.

**Section 9.1.4's `92-96` row is not superseded, and is now distinguished on its face.** It remains the weekly, shape-changing form acting on Chicago's own pressure; Section 9.1.6 is the daily, incident form acting on the Bearer. A tick that rolls both writes both.

## Migration procedure

This edge **transforms no stored state.** Nothing on a character sheet, in an inventory, or in a world ledger is recomputed, re-rolled, or amended.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.106** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`. This is the only touched state path.

**2. Past `world_ticks` entries are left exactly as captured.** The `wider_map` line is added to the Section 9.1.1 schema **prospectively**: every tick from the adoption boundary forward carries it, and no earlier entry is backfilled. A tick that ran before this edge did not roll step 11, and writing one in afterwards would be authoring a roll that never happened - the same defect Section 9.1.1 exists to prevent, arriving from the other direction.

**3. No metro is promoted by this edge.** Chicago and Prague run the full Section 9.1 tick and every other metro is on the light one, including any the campaign has already visited. A metro leaves the light tick only when the campaign anchors in it under Section 9.1.6's promotion rule, and that is a play-session authoring act.

**4. A capture restored across this edge runs step 1 only.** The transformation is deterministic and needs no roll and no ruling.

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Every checkpoint captured under 1.105 or earlier keeps what it was captured with; restoration applies this chain to mutable live state only.
