## What 1.30 changed

Version 1.30 remains the **additive trigger-telemetry advance** over frozen 1.29, and closes the four-part correction 1.27–1.29 carried out. Those three fixed the defect; this one makes the same class of defect **visible next time**. No Hidden or Urgent quest had fired in the whole of a campaign, every audit had been performed correctly, and nothing anywhere counted the zeros — so the gap surfaced only because the player noticed it. New Section 8.4.6 requires a `trigger_telemetry` block written into mutable live state at each checkpoint, recording lifetime Hidden attachments and Urgent offers, the in-fiction days since each, and — critically — the two **supply** counts that make a dry count interpretable: concealed discoveries available under Section 8.4.5, and live postings on the Section 9.10 board. Supply zero with a rising dry count is the design fault. Supply healthy with a rising dry count is **not a fault**: it means the world has things to find and the Bearer has not yet met a proximity, clue, or crisis condition, and the correct response is none. The section is **diagnostic and never a trigger** — no value it records is a precondition, no threshold releases the Runtime from Sections 8.4.2 or 8.4.3, and a rule that fired a quest at a dry-spell threshold would be a Tier-3 no-declared-precondition firing wearing a counter as a disguise, prohibited exactly as such. The counters are not System state, live outside `system_state`, and never render in any `/system` panel. **No stored field, magnitude, cost, counter, probability, owned item, completed transaction, or resolved outcome changes.** Data Model 0.1.5 remains unchanged.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.29 → 1.30 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration; the block is additive campaign state and changes no quest, roll, item, pool, counter, or outcome. **Immutable checkpoints are not retrofitted** — captures predating this section stay byte-unchanged and carry no telemetry block, and the counters begin at the first checkpoint written after adoption, counting from campaign start. Recording telemetry authorizes nothing: adoption attaches no pointer, issues no offer, and does not re-audit any past scene. Record the additive-upgrade acknowledgement in mutable live state; immutable Profile 1.29-and-earlier checkpoints run the applicable compatibility chain through 1.30 at readiness.
