## What 1.118 changes

**Sections 11.2 and 11.3 (the Boss Drop table and its skill table).** The Boss Drop's separate `41-65 Rune` (d8, the eight Section 7.3 starting skills only, at the Gate's Rank) and `91-97 Skill book` (d10, the same eight plus Rupture/Bulwark, at the Gate's Rank — the "rarer" distinction was never a Rank difference here, only a wider table and a narrower roll band) bands merge into one `41-72 Rune` band, rolling d10 on the renamed **Section 11.3, The Skill Rune Table**. Total probability is conserved (25 + 7 = 32 width), and the Rank behavior is unchanged: a boss drop's rune already taught at the Gate's own Rank before this edge in every path that reached the d10 table; what changes is that the narrower, weaker d8 band (Gate Rank capped to the eight starting skills, previously reachable at a **separate lower tier in Section 7.1's old route-1 prose** describing a rune-at-E/D-floor) is retired, so *every* boss-drop instructional result now reaches the full d10 table at the Gate's own Rank, not only the rarer 91-97 roll.

**Section 8.1 (the Daily Random Box).** `61-80 Rune` and `94-99 Skill book` — both already resolved at the Bearer's own System Rank in this table, differing only in table width (Section 7.3's eight vs. the full d10) — merge into one `61-86 Rune` band rolling the full Section 11.3 table. Total probability is conserved (20 + 6 = 26 width); the `81-93` gear band renumbers to `87-99` to keep the table contiguous, and `100` elixir is unchanged.

**Section 7.1 (Acquisition).** The six-route list becomes five: former route 1 (Rune) and route 2 (Skill book) merge into a single route 1 (Rune), now teaching at its source's own Rank rather than route 1 capping at the E-Rank/D-Rank starting-skill floor while only route 2 reached the source's full Rank. Former routes 3-6 (Earned by doing, Stat Passive, Taught, Succession) renumber to routes 2-5. Every cross-reference to a route number elsewhere in the profile (Sections 7.1, 7.3, 7.6, and the `acquisition_route` enum) is renumbered in the same pass — Taught is route 4 (was 5), Succession is route 5 (was 6). The `acquisition_route` enum drops the `skill-book` value; any live entry recording it is read as `rune` on restoration (step 2 below).

**Section 11.3 renamed** from "The Skill-Book Table" to "The Skill Rune Table." Its content (the d10 skill list, Rupture and Bulwark's authored entries) is unchanged — only its governing item name and the removed "rarer than a rune" framing.

**Scattered prose** referring to "runes and skill books" or "rune- or book-taught" as two drop-loot vessels is updated to say "rune" alone throughout `206_WORLD_RULE_PROFILE.md` and `240_RESOURCES.md`.

**"Mastery Rune" and "Mastery Book," checked and removed as dangling terminology, not preserved as a separate mechanic.** The first pass of this edge treated them as a distinct pair of items (spending a duplicate instructional item for mastery credit, "exactly as the matching Mastery Rune or Mastery Book does") and left them alone as out of scope. On a second look, prompted by the player asking what they actually were, neither name is authored anywhere in the profile, the shop catalogue (Section 12.5), or `240_RESOURCES.md` as its own purchasable or droppable good with a price or drop table — the two mentions (Section 7.1's Instructional Item Identity and Binding, and its Consumption Settlement) were comparison language pointing at the ordinary "spend a duplicate rune for mastery" branch as if a separate item existed to compare it to. Both mentions are removed: the binding line is replaced with the actual substantive fact it was gesturing at (spending a rune for mastery is a Bearer-only choice, regardless of the item's own binding — an NPC recipient is never offered it), and the self-referential "exactly as the matching Mastery Rune or Mastery Book does" clause is dropped from the mastery-spend branch, which already stands complete without it. No mechanic changes: "spend it for mastery" behaves exactly as before, under its own name alone.

## What did not change

**No stored skill entry is re-derived.** A skill already on a character sheet keeps its `teaches`, `teaching_rank`, `mastery_progress`, `acquisition_route`, `transmissible`, and every counter exactly as recorded — this edge touches only the tables that generate a *new* drop's identity, never a skill or item already settled.

**No banked, unconsumed instructional item is retroactively changed.** Section 11.3's own settlement rule already holds that "the item's `teaches` and `teaching_rank` are fixed at generation... nothing ever rerolls an item" — a Rune or Skill Book already sitting in a dimensional inventory keeps the Rank and vessel it was generated with. Only which item a *future* drop produces changes. Presentation of an existing character sheet's Rune/Skill Book holdings under a single skill-named line, where the campaign chooses to record them that way, is a campaign ledger convention (see `campaigns/gatefall_pendragon_001/100_CHARACTER_SHEET.md`'s `skill_rulings.instructional_item_naming_evt_001063`) and is independent of this profile edge — a campaign could keep them as separate historical lines without contradicting anything here.

**No XP, level, gold, cash, or Stat moves.** This is a drop-table and acquisition-route ledger-shape change, exactly as 1.112 -> 1.113's render-grammar edge and 1.115 -> 1.116's consolidation edge were similarly scoped changes with no economic movement.

**Section 7.6's Succession economics are untouched**, including `F-065`'s comparison between the taught route and a Succession kill — only that comparison's route *number* moves (route 5 to route 4), never its substance.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.118** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Any `skills_known` entry recording `acquisition_route: skill-book` is read as `acquisition_route: rune`** on restoration — the two were never mechanically distinct at the skill-entry level (both are inscriptions off a drop table); only the retired enum value changes, and no other field on the entry moves.

**3. A capture restored across this edge with no `skill-book`-tagged entry present runs step 1 only.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.**
