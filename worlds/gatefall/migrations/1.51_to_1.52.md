## What 1.52 changed

**Section 7.3 had two ladder tables and eight skills in neither.** The scope table covers Keen Sense, Silent Step, Exploit Pattern, Field Command and Resonance Extraction; the capability table covers Sprint and Flash Step. Dagger Mastery, Stone Skin, Bulwark, Rupture, Mend, Mana Bolt, Twin Fang and Broken Rhythm appeared in no table at all, and the profile's whole account of them was one sentence in Section 7.2 — the quantitative axes are closed-form, so the grant is always authored.

**The sentence was true and it was not enough**, in two different ways.

**Thin.** Comparing a higher Rank's Novice value against the lower Rank's Master value — the exact comparison the Rank Dominance Law makes — damage and healing hold at **1.56×** at every step, because the Section 7.2 baseline table multiplies. The passive-multiplier ladder gives **1.17× / 1.09× / 1.06×** and the reduction ladder **1.10× / 1.07×**, converging toward parity, because both add a *fixed* increment to a growing base against a mastery track that also adds a fixed increment. The law held and the Bearer could not feel it: Dagger Mastery ascended twice, E-Rank to C-Rank, and gained a larger number and no capability whatsoever.

**Absent.** An earned technique whose damage formula reads **weapon power rather than a Rank baseline** — Twin Fang, and Dimensional Projection when it forms — takes nothing from the baseline table, holds no reduction fraction and no granted multiplier, carries no scope count, and has no duration to saturate. Every axis is inapplicable rather than small. Under Section 7.2's eligibility rule the offer was withheld **correctly and permanently**: the skill could be carried to Master across twelve qualifying scenes and then be finished, by either road, for the rest of the campaign. Twin Fang reached exactly that state and held it.

**1.52 authors a third table — the magnitude-axis ladder — and every mastery-tracked skill now has a row.**

- **Dagger Mastery** *(native E, thin)* — D **Any blade**, C **Thrown**, B **Blind edge**.
- **Stone Skin** *(native D, thin)* — C **Impact spread**, B **Shared skin**, A open.
- **Bulwark** *(native E, thin, reaches the 90% reduction cap at C)* — D **Anticipated**, C **Covering**, B governed by Section 7.2's cap rule (one further exchange of duration).
- **Twin Fang** *(native E, absent)* — D **Second target**, C **Through the answer**, B **Continuation**.
- **Broken Rhythm** *(native E, absent)* — D **Unseen cadence**, C **Relayed**, B **Denied**.
- **Rupture, Mend, Mana Bolt** — *magnitude only, by design*, with the 1.56× ratio recorded in the row. A stated design position, not a gap.
- **Dimensional Projection** — unauthored deliberately; the skill does not exist yet, and its rungs are authored at ratification under Section 20.3 rather than speculatively.

**Three constraints bind every new rung.** No rung adds a modifier step (Section 4.3's +1 cap is untouched). No rung changes a Mana cost (mastery's axis alone, Section 7.2). **Every quantitative grant is unchanged and still applies in full** — a category is added beside the number, never in place of it, so no ascension returns less than it did under 1.51.

**The same defect exists on the mastery axis, and 1.52 closes it too.** Auditing what *levelling* grants each skill found **Broken Rhythm gaining nothing from Novice to Master**: a permanent passive at the Section 4.3 step cap has no multiplier, no reduction, no duration to lengthen, no authored scope count, and Mana 0, so every closed-form axis in Section 7.4 was inapplicable at once. Its mastery track is authored as **scope — 1/2/3/4/5 beneficiaries**, the ladder Keen Sense and Silent Step already use, and it gains a `scope_floor` under the Section 7.2 ratchet. Two repairs follow: Broken Rhythm's **effect is authored into Section 7.2** as a ratified earned technique, having existed only as a rendered line on the Bearer's sheet; and its C-Rank category is **Read the room** rather than an ally grant, because ally count is now mastery's axis and Rank may not compete for it.

**Section 7.4 gains a per-skill mastery table**, one row per mastery-tracked skill naming its axis and its Novice→Master span, mirroring Section 7.3's Rank tables. A closed-form rule with no coverage list cannot report a skill it fails to cover, which is how Broken Rhythm went unnoticed.

**Three further changes carry the same finding into the sections that let it go unnoticed.**

- **Section 7.4** now states that a skill at Master **still earns and still writes its qualifying scenes.** `mastery_progress` renders complete and cannot advance, but `qualifying_scenes_total` and `successful_uses` are lifetime counts, and the delta is written even though no rendered value moves — because ascension returns the skill at Adept with a live progress track, and the scenes credited at Master are what that track reconciles against.
- **Section 7.5** makes the ascension-readiness check **standing**: at every dangerous-scene settlement and every promotion barrier, evaluate the whole mastery-tracked set from stored state, rather than surfacing an offer only where memory says a barrier recorded an advance.
- **Section 14.3** adds **skill-credit coverage** and rules that an Event recording a resolved dangerous scene carries a danger-bearing kind. `kind: scene` is not available to a Gate or instant-dungeon clear.

**Section 7.3 no longer asserts which rungs are occupied.** Two lines carried copies of live campaign state — *"every skill in this table is at its native E-Rank today"* and *"Flash Step at native D-Rank today"* — hand-maintained, false since `EVT-000390` and `EVT-000220` respectively, with nothing comparing them to the ledger. That is `F-013` one layer up, and the fix is the same shape: the rules file authors rungs and reads held Rank from the Bearer's own ledger.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.51 → 1.52 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption recomputes nothing numeric; it re-renders skill rows against rungs that now exist, and re-runs one standing check.

**1. Grant every authored category the Bearer's held Rank already covers.** Section 7.2 grants one authored category per Rank above native. A Bearer standing above a previously unauthored rung **takes that category at adoption**, exactly as the 1.47 → 1.48 Stat Passive adoption did — a deferral being paid, not a new grant. In the Gatefall Pendragon campaign this reaches **Dagger Mastery alone**, at C-Rank, two Ranks above native: it gains **Any blade** (D) and **Thrown** (C). Every other skill in the new table stands at its native Rank, so no other category is owed.

**2. Change no number.** The chassis multiplier, reduction fraction, Rank baseline, Mana cost, mastery level, `mastery_progress`, `successful_uses`, `qualifying_scenes_total`, `rank_ascensions` and `scope_floor` of every skill are **untouched**. Dagger Mastery's +0.70 is unchanged and both dagger damage previews stand at 127 and 111. A category is not a number and grants none.

**3. Run the standing ascension-readiness check once, at adoption.** Section 7.5's check now reads stored state, and the new rungs change its answer for skills that were ineligible only because nothing was authored above them. **Surface every offer it produces and settle none of them without the Bearer.** Section 7.5 makes the offer the Bearer's to take or decline, and Section 14.3's Tier 3 forbids the System authoring his decision; adoption presents arithmetic, it does not spend mastery. In the Gatefall Pendragon campaign this produces exactly one: **Twin Fang, E-Rank Master → D-Rank Adept**, gaining **Second target**. It stands open until answered and, once open, re-surfaces at every later barrier and expires never.

**3b. Give Broken Rhythm its scope counter and its authored mastery value.** Its mastery track now exists, so the skill carries a `scope_floor` `tracked_counters` entry like every other scope-axis skill, written at its ratifying Event's baseline with `current_value: 0` — the floor binds only after a first ascension, and it has none. Its scope value is the Section 7.2 formula's: at native E-Rank and Novice, `1 + 0 + 0 = 1`, the Bearer alone. **No mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, or resolved outcome changes**; the skill gains an axis it did not have, from adoption forward.

**4. Add the missing `mastery_level` and `rank_ascensions` counters.** Section 7.5 has required both as authoritative `tracked_counters` entries since the `EVT-000158` adoption, and two mastery-tracked skills acquired afterward never received them — **Mana Bolt** (`EVT-000303`) and **Broken Rhythm** (`EVT-000308`). Silent Step, acquired in the same window, has both. Write each as a baseline entry at its acquiring Event with `current_value` equal to the level the skill already holds, computed as `min(5, 1 + floor(qualifying_scenes_total ÷ 3))` for a skill with no recorded ascension. **This changes no value**, exactly as the `EVT-000158` adoption changed none; it stores one that was being derived.

**5. Nothing retroactive.** No past exchange is re-resolved, no strike recomputed, no clear re-credited, and no category applied backward to a scene that resolved without it. The new rungs apply **from adoption forward**. Rules Section 13.2 forbids reopening what a settlement has spent, and every prior scene resolved correctly under the capability the Bearer actually held at the time.

**6. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP total, level, System Rank, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched. Skill-credit coverage is prospective from its declared baseline and rewrites no historical Event; `EVT-000327` and `EVT-000332` keep their recorded kind and their empty `counter_deltas`, their repair having already been settled at `EVT-000390`.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.51-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.52 at readiness.
