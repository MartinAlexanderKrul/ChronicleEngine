## What 1.59 changed

**A player read Dimensional Projection's entry and asked the obvious question: *the launched weapon stays where it lands* does not survive a Bearer who can store anything.** Checked against the two sections that govern it, the objection is correct and the defect is larger than the sentence.

**The pocket dimension's two directions were never symmetric, and only one of them was ever written down.** Section 12.9 bounds **withdrawal** explicitly — *to his own hand*, with remote destinations denied by name: an item cannot be summoned beside, behind, above, or inside a target, and cannot be launched from the pocket dimension. **Storage carried no stated bound at all.** Its whole text was *he may send an item to the inventory … from anywhere*. Section 7.2 nonetheless asserted that the pre-skill inventory was **hand-delivery only**, in both directions, on the strength of a sentence Section 12.9 never wrote — and the play record had already contradicted it: `EVT-000326` swept **three crates and a workbench** into the pocket dimension on a single roll, which no hand-delivery reading supports.

**That omission was load-bearing rather than cosmetic.** It was the sole justification for Dimensional Projection's recovery cost, and therefore for whether the technique's **D rung granted anything at all**. *Recall* reads *the launched weapon returns to his hand at the action's end rather than remaining where the fiction puts it, ending the ordinary recovery cost of using the technique at all* — and if storage already worked at range, the Bearer could do that at native E-Rank for free, so the rung sold nothing. Section 7.2 defines a category as **a kind of thing the Rank beneath cannot do at any value of the governing Stat**; the old wording would have failed the Rank Dominance Law the moment anyone read the two sections together. This is the same defect class 1.57 recorded against Mana Bolt's ladder — a rung the tier below already covers — reached this time through an unstated rule rather than a redundant one.

**Storage now reaches what he perceives; withdrawal still reaches only his hand.** He may send to the inventory any inanimate object he can perceive and could lawfully pick up. Three limits are authored with it, and they are what keep this from becoming a different ability: **nothing a person is holding, wearing, or carrying**; nothing behind a barrier he cannot perceive through; and a contested attempt is an uncertain action the die resolves like any other. **Nothing here disarms anyone**, and the withdrawal side is untouched — remote deployment and launch still do not exist without Dimensional Projection.

**The cost lands where the tension actually is.** Remote storage is **free outside an exchange** — picking a room clean, sweeping a dock, drawing a folder off a car boot — and **inside an exchange it is that exchange's one inventory change** under Section 12.9's existing *Equip and swap* budget. Hand-delivery in both directions stays free and unlimited exactly as it always was; only the remote reach is rationed. The currency was already this skill's: Section 7.2 has said since 1.51 that *its remote withdrawal is the action's one inventory change for that exchange*.

**What that buys, stated as the trade it is.** A launched weapon is **never lost and never an errand** — it comes straight back, no walking. But at native Rank the Bearer may **launch or recover in an exchange, never both**, so the technique costs a beat of tempo, paid in the same currency as a weapon swap. **`Recall` (D) survives as a genuine grant**: it returns the weapon at the action's end **without** spending that change, so at D he does both in one action and keeps the change for a swap besides. The E-Rank drawback becomes a tempo cost rather than an errand, which is the more interesting version of the same restriction and the one the ladder can actually sell against.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.58 → 1.59 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.59"`.**

**2. Re-render any Bearer's Dimensional Projection `skills_known` line** to carry the recovery terms: the launched weapon lands and stays until recovered or recalled, recovery is Section 12.9 remote storage, and inside an exchange that spends the exchange's one inventory change. **This is a rendering change over a restriction that did not previously exist in writing** — no counter, magnitude, range, Mana cost, mastery level, Rank, `magnitude_floor`, or `scope_floor` moves. A Bearer who does not know Dimensional Projection has no line to re-render and this step is inert.

**3. No skill changes and nothing is granted.** Dimensional Projection keeps its Rank, its Novice mastery, its 5 m band, its 6 Mana, and its Agility-to-hit resolution. No other skill is touched. The *Recall* rung is reworded to name what it now removes; **it is not newly granted and remains unreached** until a Bearer holds the skill at D-Rank.

**4. Nothing retroactive.** No past exchange is re-resolved and no resolved action becomes illegal. `EVT-000326`'s crate-and-workbench sweep is **retrospectively legitimate rather than newly permitted** — it was resolved on a strong success at the time, outside any exchange, and this edge writes down the rule it was already played under. `EVT-000384`'s report folder was drawn from a car boot the Bearer stood beside and is unaffected either way. Dimensional Projection has never been used in play, so no projection or recovery exists to re-cost.

**5. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched, and no ascension offer opens or changes terms.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.58-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.59 at readiness.
