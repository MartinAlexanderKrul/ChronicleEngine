## What 1.69 changed

**Section 18 has been titled *"The Class Quest and Hidden Classes"* since Profile 1.6 and contained no hidden class.** All five were published on Section 18.3's signal map, each with its signal printed beside it. Nothing in the section was hidden by any reading of the word, and the title had been describing an absent feature for sixty-three profile versions.

**The five are the Assay's catalogue.** Bible Section 9 (owner-facing) names the class quest *"the specialization gate that shapes what kind of champion the candidate becomes"* — so the signal map is the process's own list of champion shapes, and the trial is it choosing from that list. **A hidden class is what the candidate becomes when he does something the instrumentation had no category for**, which is not a loophole but the thing the Assay selected him for in the first place: an adaptation that does not saturate, returning on the process that went looking for it.

### The tier — Section 18.3.3

- **Conduct, not degree.** One authored condition per hidden class, each a thing the trial can observe and Section 18.3.2's tally cannot measure.
- **At most one revealed per trial**; where two conditions fired, the earliest takes it.
- **Added to the offer, never replacing a signal class.** Section 18.3's floor of two holds.
- **The condition is never published or hinted in advance**, anywhere in the profile.
- **Rendered anomalous** — its own `✧` frame, no signal attributed, and one flat sentence: *"This offer exceeds the catalogue."* The System does not explain itself and is not the Bearer's friend, but it is **instrumentation**, and an instrument returning a reading outside its own scale reports the exception rather than clamping it silently. He is shown that something happened; nothing tells him what he did.
- **Capped on a Stat pair** rather than a single Stat. The five singles under Section 18.3.1 are spoken for, and standing outside that scheme is the point.

### The three

| Class | Reveal | Combat identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Reliquary** (18.9) | a resolved action examining something he already killed, when the exchange gave him better to do | +2 steps and an authored counter against creatures of a relic's **origin** — and nothing at all against any other |
| **Herald** (18.10) | clearing the trial scoring in **all five** signals | +1 step per bonded ally engaged in sight, Mana returned on their kills; weakest class alone, strongest with four |
| **Seam** (18.11) | a resolved action on the **seal** of the sealed trial | severs a creature from its mana suffusion: half Rank Health and **full vulnerability to ordinary mundane damage** |

**Every one of them fights, and Section 18.3.1 now requires it.** Two of the three began as knowledge-shaped concepts, and a class whose grants do not reach a fight is a **trap** in a world where death is final and the class is held permanently — indistinguishable in kind from the inert arsenal slots 1.68 removed, except that the Bearer discovers it too late to choose again. Each takes its combat capability **from its own premise**: Reliquary's relic *is* how a dead world fought the thing that killed it; Seam's severance is `GTF-OVR-003` run backwards.

**Herald is the largest world change in the section.** Rank fixed at Awakening is load-bearing for BGM licensing, contract law, insurance, guild politics, and the whole Section 19 secrecy economy. The class is deliberately bounded — the loan ends with the scene, accumulates nothing, grants no System, no levels, no skills and no XP, and changes no card or Awakening measurement — but Section 19.2's instrument reads **maximum Mana**, so a lend is measurable while it lasts. The profile states the consequence rather than leaving it to be discovered: a Herald who lends in public manufactures evidence **on someone else's card**, and the reawakening classification cannot absorb it, because that story explains one correction and this is a series, on other people, at will.

### Section 18.3.2 — the tally

Section 18.3 said the System *"tallies the five signals"* and nothing anywhere defined a tally. Each signal now scores authored qualifying events, and **each criterion scores at most once per phase.**

That normalization is the load-bearing part. Un-normalized, a Bearer who one-shots fourteen commons in Phase 1 scores Kill Efficiency fourteen times against a Positioning ceiling of four, and **Devourer becomes near-inevitable for anyone** — which is what the one played trial produced. Once per phase, the same run scores efficiency **once**, and what the tally measures is a signal demonstrated *across* the trial rather than farmed inside the phase built for it.

Every point must **cite the exchange** it came from; a point that cannot name its exchange was not earned. The full tally renders with the offer — all five scores, not only the two that surfaced — because an examination shows its marking, and a Bearer told only the result cannot tell a reading of him from the only reading available.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.68 → 1.69.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.69"`.**

**2. Nothing stored changes, and nothing is recomputed.** No pool, Stat, level, XP, System Rank, Card Rank, skill, mastery, counter, title, item, gold, cash, quest, key, bond, relic, or candidate is touched. Section 18 governs a class, and **no campaign in this repository holds one** — `class: null` on every live character sheet.

**3. Nothing retroactive, and no trial is re-run.** A Class Quest already resolved stands exactly as promoted, with the class it granted and the signals it tallied. **No hidden class is awarded retroactively**, and no completed trial is re-examined for a condition that did not exist when it was played — the conditions are conduct, and conduct that was never recorded cannot be adjudicated afterwards. A class is held once for the campaign (Section 18.1) and this edge reopens none.

**4. An offered-but-unentered Class Quest gains all of it.** `class_quest.status` does not move on adoption. A trial entered from adoption forward is sized and structured under Sections 18.2.1–18.2.3, tallied under 18.3.2, and may reveal a hidden class under 18.3.3.

**5. No campaign owes a re-audit.** No promoted Event, counter, or progression audit derives from Section 18, because no class has ever been held and no trial has ever been promoted.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.68-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.69 at readiness.
