## What 1.100 changes

**The defect was written into the profile in its own words and left there.** Section 16.1: *"Past level 50 the ladder is spent and the decade is not; assessments continue at 60, 70, and beyond."* They did - against a **twenty-one-entry catalog** that a Bearer at the top of the ladder has largely exhausted, into a **slot count frozen at five since level 50**, with System Rank spent at S and the class's only evolution spent at 50. A level past 50 therefore granted stat points and nothing else, and a decade assessment could only ever report `none`. **An assessment that cannot grant is a ceremony.**

### 1. New Section 16.4 - the Second Catalog

Eligible from the **level-60 assessment**, tested by Section 16.1's existing machinery with no new procedure: whole record since onset, no ruling, no partial credit, no published criterion, granted in the same assessment alongside Section 16.2's entries.

**Eleven titles in two grades** - eight **Ascendant** (+15) and three **Absolute** (+30). Their criteria read the systems a Bearer only meets past the ladder: Section 9.11 directed assignment, Section 9.12 auditions, Section 9.2's escalation tiers, Section 9.1.3 district pressure, Section 7.4's contest test, and Section 19's carded standing.

**The point grants are stated as vestigial and that is deliberate.** Section 16.2's +2 / +5 / +10 were calibrated against a Bearer climbing from a creation array of 8s to level-50 parity near 1,016. Against base Stats already near a thousand, **+15 is under two percent of one Stat**. They are kept because Sections 3.2 and 4.2 name a title grant as a stat-point source and removing it would break that contract, and raised enough not to insult - **but the reward of a second-catalog title is its passive**, and the section says so rather than letting a player work it out.

**Three further slots: a sixth at 60, a seventh at 80, an eighth at 100.** Slower than the first five (three across fifty levels, where the first came across forty) so a loadout keeps being a decision against a catalog now thirty-two deep.

**`The Ledger's End` is foreclosed for this campaign and the section says so.** It requires all twenty-one Section 16.2 entries, one of which - `The Unmeasured` - needs a carded Rank three or more below System Rank at an assessment, which the Section 19.4 trade permanently ends. The live Bearer carded S-Rank at `EVT-000683`. **This is the cost of that trade rather than an oversight**, and it is written into the profile so it is not discovered at level 90.

### 2. New Sections 18.12 and 18.13 - level 100, in two halves

**18.12 is what the WORLD does, and it grants the Bearer nothing.** That is the design and it is the third answer to the same complaint: he arrives at level 100 already able to clear content the world has no answer for, and three prior systems answered *"nothing new"* with more capability, which is the surplus he already had.

- **The instrument has no reading for him, and this was already true.** Section 19.2's band table classifies by measured maximum Mana and its top row is an open **1,225+** bracket, written when nobody imagined its floor being cleared several times over. The live Bearer measures **8,036**. A re-assessment now returns **beyond scale** - not a Rank, a refusal - and **every rule keyed to card Rank loses what it reads**: Section 9.4's minimums, Section 19.4's cap, Section 13.2's terms. That is a **political** problem for BGM long before it is a convenience for him, and what they invent to replace it is play.
- **The board stops competing.** A-Rank take rates fall from 90% a day to **25%**, and S-Rank work is not tendered - no guild spends a bench on what one man clears for nothing. Section 9.1.4's *a guild falls* row is **rolled at double weight**, and the Runtime attributes it honestly: the guild did not fail, it was made unnecessary.
- **The draft points the other way.** He is not on a call list; **the call list is him.** Every A- and S-Rank Gate the tick rolls is offered by default, the Section 9.11 roll is not made, and **what he declines is a world event** - it goes to the Section 9.10 board at the new rates, runs its timer, and **the Runtime reports what happened to every Gate he refused.**
- **Section 9.1.4 gains three rows he causes**, taking the *world holds* band from `01-55` to `01-33`: something reorganises around his availability, a state asks for him, and an awakening that should not read the way it reads.
- **The cost, which is the point.** Section 9.1.3 pressure **decays one slower in every district he did not work that week**. Where he goes the world is safer than it has ever been; where he does not it is **worse than before he existed**, because the response that used to be there has been reorganised around someone who was somewhere else. **A world that acquires one person who can answer anything stops building the capacity to answer it itself.**

**18.13 is what the CLASS becomes**, one line per class on that class's own axis, opened by a trial: Section 18.2's structure at Combat Tier, with his class **suppressed for the middle phase**, **one phase unsolvable by killing**, and **the fifth phase instantiated from his own record**.

**Riftwalker -> Gate Walker.** He stops being a thing that uses Gates and becomes a thing that makes them.

- **The tunnel network** - any two places he has stood joined by a **permanent two-way gate anyone may cross**, with or without him, bounded by his anchor cap. Chicago to Prague is one step for a courier. **A change to the world's logistics, not to his reach** - and 18.12's reorganisation is the world discovering that.
- **The far door** - Section 10 says a Gate interior is *a slice of the beasts' own world held in the void between it and ours*. **He opens onto the world itself**: not a slice, not a pocket, not an instance, but the place the slices are cut from.

**A far door is not a Gate, and that is the whole grant.** Stated as what is absent: **no Rank** (Section 9.2's distribution, Section 9.5's roll and Section 9.6's table all have nothing to say); **no population formula** (Section 9.7 does not fill it - what is there was not put there for him); **no boss and therefore no clear** (a dungeon is a pocket whose apex thing dies and takes the pocket with it; **a world has no bottom**); **no collapse window and no timer** (Section 9.7's two hours and Section 9.3's countdown are properties of a puncture trying to close, and a far door is not trying); and **no barrier**, so it is **two-way** and what is on the other side may walk through it at any time.

**An earlier draft of this grant let him open an ordinary Gate and was rejected in play as redundant, correctly** - Section 17's instant keys already sell a Rank-sized instance on demand, and a manufactured Gate with the rewards stripped out is a worse one.

**The System pays nothing for a far door, and the reason is the information boundary rather than an anti-farm rule.** Nothing there carries a Rank, so Section 3.3 has no row, Section 6.1 no Rank Health, Section 3.4 no clear, Section 11 no drop table. **The System has never seen these things and does not price them**, which is why a far door **cannot be made into a progression space** and is therefore the capstone rather than another key. Matter is still matter: what he carries back is real, unranked, unappraisable, and has **no chain of custody in either jurisdiction** - the widest Section 19.1 exposure surface in the profile.

**He may shut one only while nothing is standing in it**, from the side he opened it on, at the cost he opened it for. **A door with something coming through cannot be shut.** Opening costs **Mana equal to S-Rank Rank Health, 4,000** - the only fixed price in the section, because a doorway onto a world is not sized by anything smaller.

**And the profile says what this is.** `200_WORLD_BIBLE.md` Section 9 authors this world's terminal event as **a single break that does not close**. A far door is that in miniature, by his own hand. **What is actually on the far side is owner-authored campaign canon and deliberately unspecified**; a Runtime reaching one without an authored destination **stops and asks the owner** and **never improvises a world**.

**The other seven classes are owed and named as owed** - authoring terminal lines for classes nobody holds is how the pocket cache and Broken Rhythm both came to grant nothing. A class reaching 100 without its line **does not improvise one**. And **no terminal line has a growth ladder past it**.

### 2b. Superseded drafts, recorded

**Two earlier designs for this section were rejected in play and are recorded because the third is better for reasons they make legible.** The first offered a shared triad - Aegis, Spearpoint, Bannerlord - scored on three signals: a generic tank/rogue/commander split, nothing about it needed Gatefall, and Bannerlord leaned on an NPC-command subsystem the profile does not have. The second gave Riftwalker a *standing gate* and a *closing hand* and rested its drama on the closing hand not scaling to S-Rank - **which was false arithmetic**: it priced an S-Rank mouth at 4,000 Mana against a Bearer whose pool is 8,036. **Both failed the same way**: they answered *"levels bring nothing new"* with more capability, for a Bearer whose surplus is capability.

## What did not change

**No Rank, Rank Health, XP value, XP threshold, damage formula, Gate rule, escalation tier, anomaly band, break timer, population, loot table, price, skill or class ability moves.** Section 16.2's twenty-one entries are untouched in criterion, grade, point grant and passive. Section 16.1's assessment procedure, schedule, settlement invariant, render block and stored state are untouched apart from the slot count. Section 18.1 through 18.11 are untouched: the class quest trigger, the five phases, the five signals, all eight classes and every level-50 evolution stand exactly as 1.99 left them.

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.100** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Recompute the slot count.** It is derived from `titles.last_assessment` and never stored, so nothing is written - but a Bearer already past level 60 holds **six** slots from the moment this version is adopted, not five, and the `/system titles` panel and any rendered slot count must show it. The live campaign stands at level 59 with `5 slots`; **the sixth arrives at its level-60 assessment**, which is the next one due.

**3. No second-catalog title is granted retroactively at adoption.** Section 16.1 governs unchanged: entries are tested **at an assessment**, and the next assessment is level 60. A Bearer who already satisfies several Ascendant criteria earns them **then**, in one block, exactly as a first assessment reaching back across ten levels grants several at once. **Nothing is owed at the moment of adoption and no assessment is re-run.**

**4. Nothing is recomputed and no past assessment is disturbed.** Every title already earned keeps its grade, its points and its granting assessment. No point already allocated moves.

**5. A capture restored across this edge needs steps 1 and 2 and no other treatment.**

**Immutable checkpoints are byte-unchanged.** Checkpoint 0122 and earlier keep what they were captured under.
