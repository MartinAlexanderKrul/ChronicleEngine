## What 1.79 changed

1.77 and 1.78 gave the tick guild collapses, policy shifts, awakenings, market movements, non-Gate opportunities and district failures. **Every one of them was narration with nowhere to go** — which is the same defect as a tick that advances a timestamp and rolls nothing, one surface over.

New **Section 9.1.5** requires each rolled event to resolve to at least one durable landing, all of which already existed:

| Landing | Authority |
|---|---|
| Supply opening, or a new source | Section 7.5 |
| Standing need | Section 7.6, Decision 088 |
| Objective | `140_OBJECTIVES.md` |
| Entity change | the campaign's NPC ledger |
| World fact | Section 20.1, the world ledger |
| **`none`, with its reason** | written, never assumed |

**`none` is a legitimate landing and must be written as one.** What is forbidden is silence: an event with no recorded landing is indistinguishable from an event that was never rolled. That is Decision 080's negative-assertion discipline, which the supply anchor, the tick record and the district settlement all already use.

Worked routings are given per event class — a guild falling produces standing needs for its stranded hunters, supply openings where its contracts transfer, and a world fact; regulatory action produces a world fact and, where it obliges the Bearer specifically, an objective; a standing Gate thread produces an objective and explicitly **not** a Section 9.10 posting. They are written down so routing is not left to taste.

## Why there is no eighth quest type, and this is the substance of the change

The obvious way to make world events matter would be a System quest channel for non-Gate content. **Section 14.4 forbids it.** The System knows *"Bearer state, quest state, its current shop stock, and Gate state once resolved"*, and explicitly does **not** know *"NPC minds, hidden identities…"* A quest to investigate a guild's finances or broker a contact's motives would require it to know something it is authored not to know — the same violation as an NPC asserting a Bearer-only quantity, pointed the other way.

The world bible supplies the reason underneath: Earth is being **assayed** by a *"distributed, largely non-conscious process."* The System is an instrument that measures the Bearer. It sets trials — daily training, Gate danger, the Level 25 Class Quest, concealed discoveries. **Guild politics is not something an assaying process has an opinion about.**

So: **the world generates work, obligations and news; the System measures the Bearer.** Different surfaces, and 1.79 keeps them apart rather than merging them. Section 8.4.2's criteria are untouched, and no new quest type exists.

**The consequence is stated rather than left to be discovered: non-Gate content earns no System XP.** Progression stays tied to what the System can measure. An investigation pays in money, standing and access — which is what those things are worth in this world — and not in levels.

## What did not change

**No roll, rate, band, timer, distribution, population formula or contest rate moves.** Sections 9.1 through 9.10 are unchanged in effect apart from the new subsection and the `landed` field on the tick record. Section 8.4.2 is untouched. Section 14.4 is untouched — it is cited, not amended.

## Migration procedure

**Compatibility treatment, 1.78 → 1.79.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.79"`.**

**2. Nothing stored changes, and nothing is recomputed.** No pool, Stat, level, XP, Rank, skill, mastery, counter, title, item, gold, cash, quest, candidate, class, fragment, tracked posting or district pressure value is touched.

**3. No past event is retroactively landed.** Events narrated before this version stay as they were told. The routing requirement is prospective, from the first tick under this profile.

**4. Declaring standing needs is authoring from canon, not invention.** `gatefall_pendragon_001` gains its first two Decision 088 instances with this adoption, both derived from played, cited state: Dale Pruitt short of the eight-plus combat-rated hunters a B-Rank client requires (`EVT-000544`, the same constraint at `EVT-000415`, tracked as `OBJ-31`), and Ironline Guild's continuous recruiting need — which `EVT-000384` records as **met badly rather than unmet**, since Brannigan's office answers a thin roster with bench padding and *"nobody checks unless someone dies."* Both are `advanced` to the current anchor: each has been read, neither has been met, and that is a settlement rather than a silence.

**5. Do not back-fill needs or openings for actors whose prose merely implies wanting something.** The candidate tool lists them; they are declared as play establishes them, on the same derive-from-canon rule the supply sources use.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.78-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.79 at readiness.
