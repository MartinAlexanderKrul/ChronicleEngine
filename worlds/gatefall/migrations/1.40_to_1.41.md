## What 1.41 changed

**The authored class of Section 6.1.2 now names the campaign ledger.** Since 1.32, individual pool variance has had three classes: authored ±10% for a recurring named NPC, rolled once on a d100 band for an elite or boss, and none for a common monster. The authored class named only `220_NOTABLE_FIGURES.md` and `institutions/` rosters — both world-layer files. Every crewmate, contact, employer, and rival a campaign actually plays lives in that campaign's own `130_NPCS_AND_FACTIONS.md`, which the rule did not name, so those NPCs belonged to **no class at all**: not authored, not rolled, and not excluded. The rule simply did not reach them.

The authored row now names all three sources as one class on identical terms. A crewmate in a campaign ledger and a world-ranked hunter in `220_NOTABLE_FIGURES.md` carry variance the same way and to the same limit.

**The `institutions/` clause is stated as forward-looking.** All eight institution directories contain zero `type: Character` blocks, so half the 1.32 authored-class definition addressed an empty set. It is retained and labelled: it binds any roster that later carries a Character.

**Two derivation boundaries are closed.** Both only become visible once an entity's pools differ from the flat table, which is why 1.32 did not need them and 1.41 does:

- **Section 13.5's mender field-touch reads the flat table.** The touch restores one standard-hit baseline of the mender's Rank; 6.1.2 already pinned Section 6.2's baseline to the flat value, but 13.5 states the touch in its own words and a reader deriving from a varied maximum would get 68 for a +10% C-Rank mender instead of the correct 62. A deeper well never makes a mender stronger per touch. It lets them spend more before the pool stops them, which is the only axis variance is meant to move.
- **Section 5.2's Mana tiers read the varied maximum.** A fixed-Rank entity has always paid 10%, 25%, or 50% of *its own* maximum; with variance in play that is now a different number per individual — a +10% C-Rank hunter's Signature ability costs 34 rather than 31 — and Section 5.3's running-dry threshold is 25% of the same varied maximum. This is the intended consequence and not a second rule.

**Section 13.1 gains a recording convention.** Section 5.1's discipline is unchanged and restated: *no creature is authored by the baseline*, and no NPC hunter receives a stat block, an ability list, or a cost sheet. What a named NPC's entry may now carry is the **derived** form of rules stated elsewhere — `rank`, `pool_variance`, `pools`, `signature_ability`, `learned_techniques`, `equipment` — written down once so a Runtime reads a settled number rather than re-deriving it mid-scene, and so a Section 4.4 Flux Sight read at the C rung can be answered from the ledger instead of recomputed. An entry omitting any field is not defective, because the rules supply the value; an entry contradicting the rules is, and the profile governs.

A companion clarification is recorded here rather than in the profile because it resolves an apparent conflict rather than changing law. Section 13.6 says an NPC learned technique "does not create Stats, XP, a Mana ledger, Bearer-style mastery, or use counters," written before 1.32 gave every fixed-Rank entity a Rank Mana pool. Both stand: the *technique* creates no ledger, and the *entity* has one anyway under 1.32, from which the technique is paid at a Section 5.2 tier. The clause that does bind is the one about mastery — an NPC holding a technique whose Bearer-side entry carries a mastery ladder holds the **base authored effect at its Rank** and nothing above it.

## Compatibility treatment

**1.40 → 1.41 compatibility treatment.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon. Adoption requires no recomputation and no state migration.

**Variance is authored forward, and nothing is re-resolved.** Assigning a factor to an NPC who already exists changes what their pool *is* from adoption onward; it does not reach back. No exchange is recomputed, no fight re-resolved, no injury re-tiered, no Mana spend restated. This is the same prospective boundary 1.32 drew for Rank Mana itself, applied to the same class of fiction, and it holds for the identical reason: nothing already resolved was ever charged against a varied pool.

**No elite or boss is retroactively rolled.** 1.32 made a variance roll mandatory at an elite's or boss's first contact after adoption, and in the Pendragon campaign that has never once been performed — the three D-Rank instant dungeons of 2026-08-09 (`EVT-000232`, `EVT-000234`, `EVT-000235`) each fielded elites and a boss, all resolved after 1.32's adoption at `EVT-000210`, and no variance was rolled for any of them. Those instances are closed, their populations discarded with them, and none is reopened or re-rolled. The omission is recorded here so the history is honest, not corrected: every one of those fights resolved at flat table values, which is a legal outcome of the band (31–70 is *none*) and in any case cannot now be distinguished from one. Section 6.1.2's roll binds elites and bosses met from adoption forward.

**No narrated cost is restated.** Where a scene resolved before 1.32's adoption described an entity spending a share of its capacity in prose, that prose stands as written and is not converted into tier arithmetic. In the Pendragon campaign the case is Owen Callahan (`ENT-000139`) sustaining Stone Skin through an elite on 2026-08-06 (`EVT-000180`) at a narrated cost of roughly two-thirds of his capacity — a fraction larger than any Section 5.2 tier, resolved two days before 1.32 was adopted at `EVT-000210`, and therefore never bound by the tier table. It is not recomputed, and it does not set a precedent: from adoption forward that technique costs a tier of his own maximum like any other.

**Ranked pools remain underivable, which is the point.** 6.1.2's stated purpose is that reading an entity's pools should return something a Rank read did not already give. Until this edge, no entity in the repository carried a variance of any kind — 0 of 14 figures in `220_NOTABLE_FIGURES.md`, 0 of the campaign ledgers, 0 rolled in play — so a Section 4.4 Flux Sight read at the C rung returned the table value to a Bearer who could already name the Rank. The capability was a no-op against every human in the world. It is not one after this edge.

Record adoption as `EVT-000248`; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.40-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.41 at readiness.
