## What 1.120 changes

**Section 15.3 gains one general clause.** Where a campaign declares a derived ledger for a panel's surface in its own `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`, that panel renders its **summary and totals inline** and serves the **per-item listing** as the ledger's link instead of restating every row in the frame. The clause is **inert for any campaign that declares no ledger** - such a campaign renders every panel exactly as 1.119 did.

**Section 15.3.1 `/system skills`.** The panel keeps its header, its per-category counts, and the Offense/Defense/Utility classification rule; the per-skill rows are served from the ledger. The categories still govern, because they are what the ledger groups by and what the counts count.

**Section 15.3.2 `/system gear`.** `WORN` and `WORN . TOTALS` still render inline in full, and `STORED` renders each kind's live line count followed by the ledger link in place of the per-item rows.

**Nothing interactive moves.** Equipping, unequipping, withdrawing, buying, selling and title changes all still resolve through the panel. `WORN` was kept inline deliberately: it is the interaction surface and it is nine lines rather than a hundred. A withdrawal names an item the player read in the ledger, which is generated from the same canon the panel reads.

## What did not change

**No stored field changes shape or meaning**, so no capture requires recomputation and no checkpoint is rewritten. This edge is a render-surface treatment only.

**No mechanic moves.** Section 15.3.1's category definitions, Section 15.3.2's five stored kinds, the quantity-collapse rule, and every totals line are untouched. The ledger is a view of the same canon and is regenerated from it; it authors nothing.

**No XP, level, pool, gold, cash, Stat, skill, or holding moves.**

## Migration procedure

**1. Advance `profile_version` to 1.120** on the campaign's live character sheet, and re-pin `world_rule_profile:` in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`.

**2. Confirm the campaign's ledger declaration.** A campaign adopting 1.120 that wants the new behavior declares its derived ledgers in `090_CAMPAIGN_STARTUP.md`; one that declares none is already conformant and renders as before. `gatefall_pendragon_001` declares both, generated by `tools/generate_campaign_ledgers.py`.

**3. Regenerate the runtime index** (`tools/generate_runtime_index.ps1`), then confirm with `-Check`.

**4. Validate** with `tools/validate_repository.ps1`.

## What is owed and not done here

**The adoption Event is campaign canon and belongs to play.** This record lands the world side only. `gatefall_pendragon_001` owes an adoption Event at its next save, per every prior profile adoption.
