## What 1.56 changed

**A signature offensive skill had become strictly worse than swinging the knife.** Measured on the live Bearer at Level 21, effective Strength 66, S-Rank dagger equipped:

| Action | Damage | Mana |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary main-hand strike, no skill | **128** | 0 |
| **Rupture, D-Rank Expert** | **61** | 9 |
| Twin Fang, E-Rank Master | 316 | 4 |

**The cause is structural, not a bookkeeping slip.** A Rank baseline is **fixed per Rank**; a weapon strike **compounds** with effective Strength, weapon power and the Dagger Mastery chassis, and does so every session the Bearer grows. Section 7.2's own worked example set Rupture and Twin Fang as a deliberate pair at E-Rank — 29 damage for 12 Mana against 18 for 6. Ninety in-fiction days later it read 61 against 316. **No amount of Rank ascension closes a gap that widens on its own**, which is why this could not be fixed by settling owed breakthroughs.

**Section 6.2 splits into two formulas.** A Bearer damage skill reads `(skill_rank_baseline + effective Intelligence + equipped_focus_power)`; a Bearer healing skill keeps `(skill_rank_baseline + equipped_focus_power)`. The Intelligence connection was already half-written: **Rupture takes an equipped focus**, which is caster gear rather than a weapon, and **Mana Bolt already had Intelligence governing its control**. 1.56 lets it govern the magnitude too, exactly as effective Strength governs a physical strike.

**Healing is deliberately excluded.** Mend reading Intelligence would restore **91 Health for 5 Mana** against a Lesser Healing Potion's 30, repeatable, on a Bearer whose maximum is 412. That does not rebalance the Section 12.6 consumable economy; it deletes it.

**Two Rank ladders flatten, and take categories accordingly.** A large flat Intelligence term added to a small baseline compresses the Rank ratio:

- **Mana Bolt's E→D step returns nothing at all on either road** — `(10+60)×1.60 = 112` at E-Rank Master against `(25+60)×1.00 = 85` by rune and `×1.30 = 111` by breakthrough, both held flat at 112 by the magnitude ratchet.
- **Rupture's breakthrough road inverts above Intelligence 105**, and is thin below it.

Both therefore leave *magnitude only, by design* and gain authored rungs — Rupture **Compound** (D) / **Structural** (C) / **Ward-breaking** (B) / **Propagating** (A); Mana Bolt **Arcing** (D) / **Detonating** (C) / **Ward-piercing** (B) / **Homing** (A). **Mend keeps its magnitude-only row and keeps it honestly**, because it was excluded from the change and its 1.56× dominance is untouched.

**The general lesson is on the record: a *magnitude only, by design* row is a claim which can expire.** It was true for all three skills at 1.52 and false for two of them the moment the damage formula moved. `tools/test_rank_dominance_law.ps1` is what caught it.

## Migration procedure

**Required 1.55 → 1.56 migration.** Preserve every stored value and all resolved canon.

**1. Advance `profile_version` to `"1.56"`.**

**2. Re-derive the damage preview of every Bearer damage skill, once, at adoption.** Read effective Intelligence from the Bearer's own `effective_stats` and apply the new Section 6.2 damage formula. **Nothing stored changes** — a `/system` damage preview is derived live and never held in a ledger (Section 6.2) — so this is a re-render, not a state write. In the Gatefall Pendragon campaign, at effective Intelligence 60: **Rupture 61 → 299** at C-Rank Expert, **Mana Bolt 29 → 98** at D-Rank Practiced.

**3. Leave every healing value alone.** Mend's magnitude is unchanged at every Rank and mastery level, and no potion, seal, or mender effect moves.

**4. Grant the authored categories each skill's held Rank already covers.** Section 7.2 grants one category per Rank above native, so a Bearer already standing above native takes them at adoption — the 1.47 → 1.48 precedent, a deferral being paid. In the Gatefall Pendragon campaign: **Rupture at C-Rank takes Compound (D) and Structural (C)**; **Mana Bolt at D-Rank takes Arcing (D)**.

**5. Nothing retroactive.** No past exchange is re-resolved, no resolved strike recomputed, no kill re-credited, and no wound reclassified. Every prior scene resolved correctly under the formula in force at the time, and Rules Section 13.2 forbids reopening a settlement.

**6. Nothing else moves.** No pool, Stat, maximum, XP, level, System Rank, mastery level, use count, qualifying-scene count, `scope_floor`, `magnitude_floor`, title, item, equipment, gold, cash, deadline, streak, quest, or objective is touched.

Record adoption as a campaign Event at the adopting campaign's current anchor; consume no fictional time and reopen nothing. Immutable Profile 1.55-and-earlier checkpoints remain byte-unchanged and run the compatibility chain through 1.56 at readiness.
